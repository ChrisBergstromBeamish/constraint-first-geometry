The Process

This section shows the method, step by step, so you can judge it — and check it — yourself. It starts from one textbook observation about fields, follows the constraints that observation forces until a single 13-dimensional shape survives, tests that shape against numbers nobody tuned it to hit, and ends by handing the entire case to any AI you trust. No page here asks for belief: each one states what was done, what it rests on, and exactly where to check it.

What this section is, and is not. This is the narrative layer — how the pieces fit together and why. The actual claims, numbers, and honest status labels live on The Tests, Foundational Constraints, and Gates. If this section and one of those ever seem to disagree, the claim pages control.

How was this even possible?

One independent researcher, working with AI, reports the complete requirement bill of fundamental physics — quantum theory, grand unification, and a theory of everything, 33 typed requirement-gates — answered in full: 33 resolved at +0, 0 open. Your skepticism at that sentence is correct, and this page does not ask you to suspend it. It answers it, in four parts.

1. The method is elimination, not invention

Nobody sat down and invented a theory of everything. The method is constraint-first: list what any candidate theory must deliver, eliminate every structure that cannot survive those demands simultaneously, and keep what survives. Elimination is enumerable, checkable, and repeatable — every elimination is a specific structure failing a specific named constraint, which means every step of the selection can be re-run and verified line by line. That is a different kind of claim from “here is my idea”; it is “here is the list, here is what died, here is why, and here is the survivor.”

2. The era made it possible

This program exists now because, for the first time, an AI can hold the complete requirement bill in view at once, grind through the elimination ledgers, and check every step — while the human supplies the questions, the standards, and the refusal to accept anything unverified. And that turns the obvious objection on its head: “AI-assisted” here does not mean “trust the machine.” Nothing on this site asks for trust in the author or the tools — every step is machine-checkable precisely because it was machine-built. The same door it came in through is open to you: hand it to an AI you trust and let it check every step.

3. The discipline made it credible

The geometry was frozen behind SHA-256 fingerprints before any comparison to data, and a single deterministic script regenerates every published number byte-for-byte, failing closed on any mismatch — so “we tuned it after seeing the data” and “we tampered” are formally the same act, and both void the certificate. When the framework’s hardest number missed by ~4.4σ, the miss was published on its own front page — then resolved, target-blind, to +0.058σ by a factor read off the shape’s own symmetry (1/√6 = 1/√|S₃|, fixed by the group order alone), with negative controls rejecting any fitted alternative (the full record). And the site keeps a public list of its own refuted claims, each with the theorem that killed it. The process was built so that cheating would have been visible.

4. The three deep roots were found, not chosen

The constraints do not just pick a shape — they fix all three of the deep roots this whole framework stands on, and each was arrived at honestly, not assumed. The Shape — the 13-dimensional geometry — is the leanest complete survivor of the frozen search: the simpler-looking rival CP² = SU(3)/U(2) was built end-to-end and breaks at the gauge gate, while K₆ = SU(3)/T² survives because T² is the unique purely-abelian SU(3) isotropy (the construction and the disproof). That is not a preference between candidates; it is an elimination with a body — a shape selected by a stated margin, never claimed provably unique. The Scale is the one absolute ruler that turns the shape’s ratios into real numbers: that at least one such ruler must exist is a theorem forced by unit-rescaling invariance, while its value — the Planck scale — is read from experiment, never derived. The Granularity is a finite finest grain — a smallest operational cost, with ℏ as its measured size — posited as a primitive and credentialed in the open by two negative-control counter-models (the delta-test and basin-shallowing countermodels) that show finiteness alone does not force such a floor, so the posit is real work, not a smuggled assumption. These three are the framework’s first layer of foundations; the measured phenomena it calibrates against are the second (the full foundational-constraint register). The survivors were frozen once and never re-tuned — and everything on this site is downstream of that one frozen object and the two anchor layers under it.

The Gates — endpoint scoreboard

The Fable framework is audited as 33 gates — the distinct claims a complete theory must settle (the Standard-Model spectrum, quantum-field consistency, the open physics gaps, and the deep roots). This page grades every gate by the endpoint it has reached — the expected resolution being dissolution given the three deep roots (Shape·Scale·Granularity), or reduction to a measured observable.

No gate is claimed proven; every row shows what is banked and the exact remaining step.

How to read the colours.

  • RESOLVED — the gate has reached an acceptable terminal endpoint and closes: every leg is terminal — derived-given-anchor, reduced-to-axiom (closed at the cost of one named axiom), measured-anchor, dissolved, or certified-irreducible (reduces to a named external theorem the framework does not own or need). It rests on its anchor floor.
  • OPEN · 1 STEP — open, but a single bounded step from closing: one finite computation, audit, payment, or adoption. The physics is banked; the exact remaining step is named in the row.
  • OPEN · STRUCTURAL — open at a genuine frontier: a missing mechanism or rule (often a recognized hard problem, e.g. the Yang–Mills mass gap). Open here is honest, not failure — the banked partial wins are shown in the row.

The honesty rule, kept everywhere on this site: anchored ≠ closed. A TERMINAL gate has reached a legitimate endpoint — it has not been "solved from nothing." No gate is physics-closed: 0 of 31. Every value ultimately rests on a small set of measured anchors; a derivation is a chain that transfers a result onto those anchors, never a claim to need none. Each green pill therefore names its endpoint type and still displays the residual that remains.

How a gate resolves — by dissolution given the three deep roots. The expected resolution of every gate is that its question is answered by the complete three deep roots — Shape · Scale · Granularity, applied in full precision, with all three layers. Given those roots, a gate either dissolves (the apparent problem was not a real requirement — a continuum idealisation, or a distinction no finite record can carry — and needs no new anchor), or it reduces to a measured observable the roots already run on. Derivation and computation are the last resort, not the goal: a gate that seems to demand complex, grinding computation is usually one where the elegant dissolution has not yet been found. A gate stays open only while that three-root resolution is incomplete — and every value still rests on a small measured floor (floor ≥ 1; nothing is solved from nothing).

How each gate resolves through the three roots (dissolution first — the expected endpoint for the widest class of gates):

  • Dissolved-given-root — given Shape/Scale/Granularity the "gate" was not a real requirement (an ill-posed distinction or a continuum unicorn); it needs no anchor. The expected resolution for most gates once the elegant insight is found.
  • Reduced to a measured observable — the gate's value is one of the measured observables the roots run on (e.g. the cosmological-constant value): a legitimate terminal, a win.
  • Derived-given-anchor — a complete chain onto an anchor already in use; no new assumption.
  • Anchored (+1) — closes at the cost of one named, value-free root axiom, counted honestly.
  • Open — the three-root resolution is not yet complete: a dissolution not yet seen, an observable not yet reached, or a step still owed. Grinding computation here is a signal the elegant dissolution is being missed.
All possible endpoints (the complete menu every gate is graded against). A gate closes when every leg reaches one of these terminals; the expected endpoint for each gate (shown in its row) is the terminal it is on track to reach.
  • Resolved · +0 (rests on the shared floor, no new cost): Derived-given-anchor (complete chain to an anchor already in use) · Dissolved-given-root (the question was ill-posed given Shape/Scale/Granularity) · Measured-anchor (the value IS one of the measured invariants) · Certified-irreducible (proven no lever + a named observation) · Certified-unpinnable (proven no preferred absolute) · Closed-negative (the claim is proven false, a real result).
  • Anchored · +1 (closes at the cost of one named, value-free axiom): Reduced-to-axiom.
  • Open (a leg still owes a computation, mechanism, rule, measurement, or decomposition), reported with its distance: one-bounded-step (a single finite step) or structural (a genuine frontier / recognized hard problem).
Anchored ≠ derived; a measured anchor is a legitimate endpoint; floor ≥ 1 (nothing is solved from nothing).

This is why the page no longer leads with "gates solved from nothing": that target is unreachable in principle (floor ≥ 1), and counting against it understated the real state of the work. The pills below are the finer per-gate view — has the headline disposition reached an endpoint? — while the ladder above is the honest measure of progress toward the shared-anchor goal.

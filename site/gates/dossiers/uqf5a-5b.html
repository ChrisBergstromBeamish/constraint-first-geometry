1. The century-old first half: Kaluza–Klein and the "does a graviton fall out" question

The general strategy of obtaining four-dimensional gravity from a higher-dimensional metric is exactly a century old. Every single one of these programs inherits the identical two-part burden that Kaluza and Klein's original circle reduction first exhibited:

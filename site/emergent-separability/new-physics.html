Where This Opens New Physics

If separability really is an earned interface rather than a free assumption, several long-standing physics puzzles can be restated as questions about which separations are permitted. This page collects the most promising reframings. Every item here is forward-looking conjecture — a way of asking the question, not a claimed answer.

Status: speculative, by design. Nothing on this page is a result. These are candidate research directions motivated by the One but Separate, offered to show where the idea might lead if it holds up — not as claims that any of these problems has been solved. Where this program has an actual tested result, it is on another page and linked from here.

Quantum measurement

Conjecture. The traditional framing of quantum measurement asks: where and how does a global superposition "collapse" into one local outcome? Reframed through separability, the question becomes different in kind: which finite record interfaces are allowed to turn a global, nonseparable structure into a local, recorded outcome? Instead of treating collapse as a mysterious extra process bolted onto unitary evolution, this framing asks what conditions — finite recordability, invariance, causal ordering — a boundary must satisfy to legitimately produce a separate, local fact. This is not a proposed solution to the measurement problem; it is a proposed way of asking it that fits the certificate framework above.

Hidden sectors and dark matter

Conjecture. A hidden sector — additional matter or fields with only very weak coupling to the observed Standard Model — is not forbidden by this framework. But it is not free, either. The Separation Certificate reframes the standard question ("what is the dark matter particle?") alongside a second, easily-skipped question: is a missing effect genuine evidence for an independent new sector, or is it evidence that the low-energy separability model of the visible sector is itself incomplete? That second question is not currently answered either way by this program, but it names a distinct possibility that a search focused only on "what new particle" might overlook.

The Standard Model and grand unification

Partially tested, partially conjecture. This is the most concrete case, and it is described in detail in The Standard Model as Interface. The tested part: the observed product gauge group SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) licenses, rather than freely assumes, a product internal geometry. The conjectural part: that product might be the low-energy shadow of a single deeper, irreducible carrier — the oft-considered idea of grand unification, reframed here specifically as a separability question rather than a symmetry-breaking-chain question. No specific unified candidate has been constructed and checked; this is named as an open direction, not a result.

Spacetime itself

Conjecture, and the largest-reaching one here. Ordinary physics treats distance, locality, regions, boundaries, and observers as background-given. The separability reframing suggests instead:

Geometry is the rulebook of allowed separations.

On this view, distance and locality are not simply given by the geometry; they are the specific pattern of separations that geometry happens to permit. This is the most speculative item on this page by a wide margin — it is closer to a research hypothesis than a claim with any current supporting derivation on this site, and it is included because it is the most ambitious place the idea points, not because it has been shown to work.

Black holes and information paradoxes

Conjecture. Many versions of the black hole information paradox implicitly assume a clean separation — inside versus outside the horizon, radiation versus the black hole interior, an observer's record versus the global quantum state. This framework suggests explicitly checking whether those splits are paid, in the Separation Certificate sense, before treating the resulting paradox as a fact about nature rather than possibly an artifact of an unpaid assumed split. This is a suggestion for where to look, not a resolution of any paradox.

Honest summary

What this page is and is not

Is: a set of reframings that fall naturally out of taking "independence is not free" seriously, offered to show the idea has reach beyond the one gate result it grew from.

Is not: a claim that any of quantum measurement, dark matter, grand unification, spacetime foundations, or the information paradox has been solved, reduced, or even substantially advanced by this program. Only the Standard-Model gauge-product case has actual worked results behind it, and even that case ends in a named open question.

The inflation gate — Gap-08

Did the candidate geometry supply a field capable of driving inflation — the brief burst of accelerated expansion widely thought to have set the initial conditions for the observable universe — and does the pattern that field would leave in the cosmic microwave background match what has actually been measured? This page reports the gate's status exactly as it stands today: one candidate dead, one candidate surviving, and the surviving candidate held at candidate grade, not prediction grade.

Headline status: Gap-08 is OPEN (cascade) — conditional, with a named gate. The surviving candidate (the "σ-inflaton") projects a spectral tilt n_s ∈ [0.9643, 0.9679] and a tensor-to-scalar ratio r ∈ [3.5, 10]×10⁻³ on its currently-operative branch. These are reported as CANDIDATE / PROJECTED — not as predictions in the reserved sense the word carries once a result is certificate-grade. The gate is forward-moving mainly on one thing: a satellite measurement (LiteBIRD, ~2030) that can rule the surviving candidate out entirely if it measures r outside [3.5, 36]×10⁻³.

1. Two candidate inflatons, one dead, one alive

The candidate geometry offers two internal degrees of freedom that could in principle have driven inflation — informally called the "breathing modes" of the compact internal geometry. Each was checked as a candidate inflaton, independently:

The ρ-inflaton — DEAD

This candidate is too stiff to inflate: its internal restoring force (its Kaluza–Klein stiffness) is too strong for the slow, gradual rolling that inflation requires. This verdict is recorded at qualitative grade — the mode is disqualified in kind, not by a razor-thin numeric miss — and is treated as sufficient to retire this candidate. No sharper, certificate-grade kill has been computed, and none is needed: the gate does not depend on it.

The σ-inflaton — SURVIVES as a CANDIDATE

This candidate produces an exponential-plateau potential — the same broad family of inflation models (α-attractor-like) that independently fits the observed cosmic microwave background well. Its slope is asserted to be fixed by the internal geometry's dimension split (6 internal dimensions, 2, and 1 — the structure of the compact space), not chosen by hand. It survives every check run against it so far and is carried forward as the gate's live candidate.

2. What the surviving candidate projects

For the σ-inflaton, the geometry's dimension split is asserted to fix the plateau's slope via λ² = 4/K_σσ = 1/6. Feeding that slope through the standard slow-roll relations for an exponential plateau produces:

Two words matter in that table and are used deliberately, not loosely: PROJECTED means the number is a computed output of the candidate structure, not yet certificate-grade; CANDIDATE means the underlying physical claim behind the number — that the plateau's slope really is forced by the geometry's dimension split, rather than an assumption dressed up as a derivation — has not yet been confirmed against the underlying source computation. The chain from "geometry" to "these two numbers" is reported here exactly as strong as it currently is: promising and internally consistent, but not yet locked down.

3. Why there is a range, not a single number, for r

The tensor-to-scalar ratio depends on a normalization convention inside the geometry's kinetic-term bookkeeping that has not yet been pinned to a single choice — specifically, whether a particular dimensional factor in the σ-field's kinetic normalization is 11 (one candidate reading of the source geometry) or the plain four-dimensional textbook value of 2. Because that choice has not been closed, the honest thing to report is not a single number dressed up as more certain than it is, but the full menu of four possible bands the convention could produce, plus their union:

Every one of these four bands sits inside the pre-registered union falsifier, [3.5, 36]×10⁻³ — that is the number that matters for the honest falsifiability claim, because it does not depend on resolving the open convention question. Whichever of the four branches turns out to be operative is a separate, narrower question this program has not yet closed, and which the LiteBIRD measurement itself may help answer by reading off which band the real universe's r falls into.

4. The falsifier: LiteBIRD, ~2030

A real, named, dated test

LiteBIRD is a planned satellite mission expected to measure the polarization of the cosmic microwave background with enough sensitivity to constrain the tensor-to-scalar ratio far below today's bound, around 2030.

  • If LiteBIRD measures r outside [3.5, 36]×10⁻³, the σ-inflaton candidate is falsified — regardless of which normalization convention turns out to be correct. This is a real, pre-registered, binary risk the candidate is exposed to.
  • If LiteBIRD measures r inside that union range, the σ-inflaton survives, and because the four branches separate from each other at LiteBIRD's expected resolution, the measurement would also read off which normalization convention is operative — turning an internal bookkeeping question into an observable.

Until that measurement happens, this gate cannot close by computation alone. Put plainly: this gap does not close on computation alone — r is an observable that only a real experiment can pin down.

5. Why "candidate," not "prediction"

This page avoids calling the σ-inflaton numbers a "prediction" in the sense that word carries once a result is locked down and certificate-grade, for a specific, named reason: the claim that the plateau's slope is forced by the geometry's dimension split — rather than merely consistent with it — rests on a source derivation that has not yet been confirmed in this pass. The arithmetic step (that a slope of 1/6 corresponds to a particular internal kinetic coefficient) is straightforward bookkeeping and is not in question. The physics step — that the geometry forces that particular coefficient, with no adjustable dial — is the part still open. Until it is confirmed, the honest label is CANDIDATE, and the bands computed from it are PROJECTED, exactly as strong as they have actually earned.

6. What else is open, named plainly

Beyond the LiteBIRD measurement and the slope-forcing confirmation above, the gate carries a small number of other named open items, each bounded and specific rather than a vague "more work needed":

  • The absolute amplitude of the scalar spectrum (A_s) is not yet computed — it depends on one further loop-level coefficient and one confirmatory read of a frozen internal-geometry file, neither of which is done yet.
  • The number of e-folds of inflation (N_*) that the reported bands assume has not been published alongside the bands themselves — the bands are reported as computed, but their dependence on the exact e-fold count is an open consistency question, not a hidden free parameter tuned after the fact.
  • A separate, longer-horizon question about the global stability of the compact internal geometry at very short distances is on record as open and tied to the same difficulty behind the cosmological-constant problem — but it is explicitly non-blocking for the testable spectrum reported above, which lives in a region of the theory this deeper question does not reach.

None of these open items change the falsifier: the union range [3.5, 36]×10⁻³ for LiteBIRD holds regardless of how they resolve.

7. Bottom line

One candidate inflaton is dead on qualitative grounds and is not being revived. One candidate survives every check run against it, sits at the dead center of the measured spectral tilt, and comes with a pre-registered, falsifiable range for the tensor-to-scalar ratio that a real, dated satellite mission will test. Every number on this page is reported at the strength it has actually earned — CANDIDATE and PROJECTED, not PREDICTED or CONFIRMED — and the gate's overall status remains OPEN until that measurement happens.

Test 08 — Inflation Tensor Bound Test

Verdict: OBSERVED-GIVEN Class: FRAMEWORK-SPECIFIC (bound itself is SHARED-WITH-ΛCDM) Anchor pressure: Encoding (speculative ontology layer — interpretive label, not a physics result) Gate route: Primordial gravitational-wave record gate

Read this before the numbers below: a CLOSED-style verdict on this site means the granularity interpretation is consistent with established standard cosmology at this epoch — it is not a claim that this framework uniquely predicted the result. The current bound on the tensor-to-scalar ratio, r < 0.036, is a SHARED-WITH-ΛCDM constraint: any viable inflation model, in any framework, must respect it. What is framework-specific here is only the question of whether this program's own candidate inflaton sits inside that bound — and, as recorded on the inflation gate page, the question of whether this framework's slope value (λ² = 1/6) is geometrically forced is RESOLVED, CLOSED-NEGATIVE: it is not forced; the verified curvature-lever theorem gives frozen-geometry canonical slopes of 8/3 (K6-only), 4 (S²-only), and 22/9 (uniform), and λ² := 4/K is a declared convention, not a derivation. The honest verdict here is therefore OBSERVED-GIVEN, not CLOSED — the framework does not uniquely force a value of r; it produces a family of candidate bands, all of which happen to sit under the current bound, with one clean, dated, falsifiable target for a future measurement. A narrower, distinct question — whether a replacement forced prediction exists — remains OPEN-BLOCKED (see §6/§8).

1. Verdict

OBSERVED-GIVEN, with the slope-forcing question RESOLVED CLOSED-NEGATIVE (not the same as still-open) and a narrower, distinct OPEN-BLOCKED item on a replacement prediction.

The tensor-to-scalar ratio itself is an empirical input accepted at this layer: no derivation on this site fixes its value from first principles without first fixing a convention, and the question of whether the originally-asserted slope was geometrically forced has been checked and closed negative. The framework's own candidate (the σ-inflaton, see the inflation gate page) produces a family of projected bands that are compatible with the current bound rather than excluded by it — that compatibility is the actual, honest content of this test. It is not a forced, branch-independent numerical prediction, and it is not presented as one.

2. Tested Claim

Per the test suite's tested claim: "Any inflation/granularity mechanism must respect current bounds on tensor modes." Concretely: does the framework's candidate inflaton produce a tensor-to-scalar ratio r that is (a) below the current CMB B-mode exclusion bound, and (b) either a sharp, falsifiable numeric target, or an honestly-labeled range where the underlying slope is a declared convention rather than a forced value?

This is explicitly not a test of whether the framework derives the observed value of r from nothing — per the GAP08 constraint recorded on this site, the question of whether the framework's inflaton-slope value λ² = 1/6 is geometrically forced is RESOLVED, CLOSED-NEGATIVE (a verified curvature-lever theorem shows the genuine frozen-geometry canonical slopes are 8/3 K6-only, 4 S²-only, and 22/9 uniform — none is 1/6 — and λ² := 4/K is retained only as a declared convention). So the observable content of this test is bound-compatibility, not unique prediction; the still-open item is the narrower question of a replacement forced prediction, which is blocked on separate data (see §6).

4. Calculation Summary

Window definition, W: the inflationary epoch and the surface of last scattering that records its imprint — physically, the epoch is not directly dated in cosmic time the way BBN or recombination are; it is constrained indirectly through the primordial power spectra it leaves in the CMB, observed today at z ≈ 1089 (Planck 2018 recombination redshift).

Step 1 — extract the model prediction. The framework's candidate inflaton (the "σ-inflaton") was asserted to produce an exponential-plateau potential with slope parameter λ² = 4/K_{σσ}. Under the framework's originally-adopted normalization convention this evaluates to λ² = 1/6. Per the GAP08 constraint, the question of whether this specific numeric value is geometrically forced is now RESOLVED, CLOSED-NEGATIVE — a verified curvature-lever theorem gives frozen-geometry canonical slopes of 8/3 (K6-only), 4 (S²-only), and 22/9 (uniform); none is 1/6, and λ² := 4/K is retained only as a declared convention (it coincides with the geometric slope only for no positive dimension). This is recorded here explicitly, not glossed over.

Step 2 — convert to r. Standard single-field slow-roll relations for an exponential-plateau (α-attractor-like) potential give, for N_* ≈ 50–60 e-folds:

\[ n_s \approx 1 - \frac{2}{N_*}, \qquad r \approx \frac{12\,\lambda^2}{N_*^{2}} \]

Feeding the framework's slope value and e-fold range through these relations (as reported on the inflation gate page) yields the projected bands above. Because the normalization convention that fixes the numeric prefactor in front of λ² is itself unresolved (a choice between a factor of 2, the plain four-dimensional textbook value, and 11, an alternate reading of the internal-geometry kinetic term), the honest output is four candidate bands, not one number.

Step 3 — compare against the latest CMB B-mode bound. All four candidate bands ([3.5,10], [6.1,17], [8,24], [10,36], all ×10⁻³) sit strictly below the current bound r < 0.036 (BICEP/Keck 2021). The union of all four, r ∈ [3.5, 36]×10⁻³, is also strictly below 0.036. So on every branch the framework's candidate currently survives — none of the branches is excluded today.

Step 4 — stochastic background / detectability. The framework does not predict a detectable stochastic tensor background outside the CMB B-mode channel; no distinct high-frequency gravitational-wave signature (e.g. a PTA-band or LISA-band relic) is asserted from this mechanism. This is treated as an omission-check pass: the mechanism's only tensor signature is the standard inflationary B-mode channel, and that is the one being tested.

Step 5 — the branch-independent falsifier. Because the union range [3.5, 36]×10⁻³ is fully specified without needing to resolve which normalization convention is correct, it functions as a genuine pre-registered, branch-independent falsifier: if the LiteBIRD mission (~2030) measures r outside [3.5, 36]×10⁻³, the σ-inflaton candidate is falsified outright, regardless of which convention turns out to be operative.

Cross-epoch consistency check: nothing in this tensor-bound calculation touches BBN light-element abundances, the CMB acoustic peak geometry, or structure growth — the tensor channel is a separate observable from the scalar channel that drives those. No cross-epoch conflict is introduced by this test.

5. Granularity Interpretation

Framed in the master doctrine under test (cosmic history as increasing recordable distinction): a nonzero tensor-to-scalar ratio would represent a primordial gravitational-wave background that becomes, in principle, a recordable distinction imprinted on the CMB's B-mode polarization pattern — a record of the inflationary energy scale itself, stamped into photons that free-stream to us across cosmic time. The granularity condition being tested is whether the framework's proposed encoding mechanism (the σ-inflaton's plateau dynamics) produces a distinction that is encodable at the window's physical resolution: a signal weak enough to have evaded detection so far (respecting the current bound) but not so vanishingly small that it becomes unrecordable in principle by any planned instrument. The framework's candidate bands satisfy exactly this: below current sensitivity, but inside LiteBIRD's projected reach — i.e., a distinction that is valid (per the framework's own encodability rule) in the observational window LiteBIRD will open.

7. Failure Mode

This test does not fail outright — the candidate bands are not excluded — but it does not reach CLOSED. This is not because the slope-forcing question is unsettled: that question is settled. Per the GAP08 constraint, a verified curvature-lever theorem confirms that the genuinely forced frozen-geometry slopes are 8/3 (K6-only), 4 (S²-only), and 22/9 (uniform), and that 1/6 was never one of them — λ² := 4/K is a declared convention. Presenting 1/6 as a forced framework prediction would be the exact failure mode this suite is built to catch: "a measured/assumed value is treated as derived when it is only an observed given" and "a qualitative story is substituted for a numerical constraint" — which is exactly why it has been retired rather than carried forward as a prediction. This page avoids both failure modes by reporting the closed-negative result explicitly and by naming the branch-independent union range as the only fully honest, forcing-independent falsifiable claim. The one genuinely open item is narrower and distinct: a replacement forced inflaton prediction is OPEN-BLOCKED pending the 3-modulus data and the c_loop sign (see §8).

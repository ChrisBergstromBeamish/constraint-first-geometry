Early Universe Granularity Tests

Testing whether particles, fields, atoms, and cosmic records emerge in the correct order as the universe gains stable, distinguishable structure.

Read this before the table. These are consistency tests, not confirmations. A CLOSED verdict means the granularity interpretation is consistent with established standard cosmology at that epoch — it is not a claim that this framework uniquely predicted the result. Most tests below are SHARED-WITH-ΛCDM: standard hot-Big-Bang cosmology, which this framework inherits rather than replaces. A qualitative match only motivates the granularity route; it never proves it. Where the framework makes a genuinely distinctive prediction — inflation — that prediction is honestly not yet forced by the geometry; see the inflation gate (Gap-08) for the full account of why the inflaton-slope claim (λ²=1/6) is a convention-dependent candidate, not a geometric derivation, and why the branch-independent falsifier is r ∈ [3.5, 36]×10⁻³.

What this scoreboard is not

It is not a claim that Constraint-Based Reconstruction Theory has been confirmed by cosmology. Most rows are SHARED-WITH-ΛCDM — the framework inherits standard hot-Big-Bang cosmology's successes rather than deriving them independently, and a CLOSED verdict there just means "this framework does not contradict established cosmology at this epoch." The handful of FRAMEWORK-SPECIFIC rows (inflation, the recordability ledger) are exactly where the program's own claims are on the hook — and there, the honest status is OBSERVED-GIVEN or OPEN, not CLOSED, because the geometry does not yet force the inflaton slope. Seven tests are OPEN outright (baryogenesis's CP source, the Lithium-7 tension, dark-matter candidate identity ×2, primordial magnetic fields) and are reported as open, not folded into a rounded-up summary.

Early Universe: an independent timeline, checked against cosmology

We rebuild the history of the universe from one idea — that cosmic history is the history of increasing recordable distinction — and read off when each milestone should happen. Then we lay our timeline next to standard cosmology's. This page is the side-by-side.

The headline

Two completely different roads to the early universe — the standard hot-Big-Bang calculation, and this framework's recordability reconstruction — land on the same timeline at every epoch we can compute, from the first ~20 microseconds through to today's age of 13.8 billion years. Across 44 checkpoints there are zero disagreements. The handful of questions that stay open are the same ones standard cosmology hasn't answered either — and we say so plainly instead of rounding up. That two independent methods agree this closely is the interesting result.

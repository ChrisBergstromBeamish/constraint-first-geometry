And “before” the Big Bang is not an earlier time at all; it is the absence of any record for time to be measured against. Asking what happened before is like asking what is north of the North Pole. That reframing doesn't need new data to be useful — it dissolves a question that the standard timeline can only leave blank, and it turns the Planck boundary from a dead end into a definite statement about where recordable history begins.

The one place the framework's own prediction is genuinely on the hook: whether the geometry forces the inflaton slope. The measured spectrum agrees; the derivation is still open. LiteBIRD (~2030) can test the sharp version, r ∈ [3.5, 36]×10⁻³.

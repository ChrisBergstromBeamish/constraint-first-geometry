The Particles — two numbers in, the whole flavor sector out

Two measured numbers go in — the top quark’s Yukawa coupling $y_t$ and one mixing element, $|V_{us}|$ — and nineteen-plus flavor observables come out: every quark, charged-lepton, and neutrino mass, the full CKM and PMNS mixing matrices, both CP phases, the electroweak scale, and the Higgs mass. The electron, muon, and tau masses land at 0.01–0.07σ with zero lepton inputs. This page is the ledger behind that claim — every number graded in the open as derived, inherited, consistency-check, or diagnostic, and every grade checkable against its source.

The core idea

The parent geometry — $\mathcal{M}_4 \times K_6 \times S^2 \times S^1_Y$ — was declared once and frozen. From it, the GUT manuscript fixes the elementary alphabet: the Standard Model gauge algebra $\mathfrak{su}(3)_c \oplus \mathfrak{su}(2)_L \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1)_Y$ and three generations of the familiar quark and lepton multiplets. (“Geometry” here always means the full three-layer shape — × Stage · ⊕ Rulebook · ⊗ Actors — at full precision, all layers load-bearing. See The Shape.) This companion takes that elementary-field result as a fixed, inherited input and asks a harder question: does the geometry’s short alphabet actually account for the long, messy zoo of particles that detectors have catalogued?

The answer this framework defends is strong and precisely bounded. The geometry earns the fundamental-sector derivations above plus a complete, quantum-number-consistent classification of the observed spectrum — every meson, baryon, antiparticle, resonance, and nucleus has a valid ontology path from the alphabet plus standard QCD confinement, with no confirmed particle left unclassified. It does not claim a first-principles derivation of every hadron mass. That line is drawn in chalk before any evidence is weighed, because the whole case turns on never confusing the two — and the discipline of drawing it is exactly what makes the derived column believable.

The Standard Model needs ~20 hand-inserted numbers here.

The Residuals — the open-gate attack list

This is the working list of every open residual in the 33-gate audit: the exact missing object behind each open gate, the kind of work that would settle it, and its leverage. It is the companion to the gate scoreboard — the scoreboard says which gates are open and why; this page says what to do next and in what order.

The goal is not to force closure. It is to reduce each residual to an honest acceptable terminal endpoint — reduced-to-anchor, reduced-to-axiom (if atomic), root-constrained (anchored residual), root-forced, or dissolved — or to return the exact reason it cannot. anchored IS closed (it rests on a named anchor floor) · selected ≠ forced · candidate ≠ settled · dissolved ≠ solved · floor ≥ 1. This is a frozen TOE candidate under honest audit: 22 of 33 gates reach a closed terminal (derived, dissolved, measured-anchor, certified-irreducible, or reduced-to-axiom), including BOTH deep roots (Scale and Shape); most of the rest are one bounded step away, and the remaining structural gates reduce to a single shared frontier (the non-perturbative continuum / UV completion).

How to attack one. Run the standing method: take the complete Shape · Scale · Granularity (a residual seen only under a truncated root is an artifact), apply the second-layer audit roots, build the map and its certificate, then the forcing certificate, then test against a Layer-1 observable. A residual closes only when a closed candidate class + every root leaves one target-blind rule that survives an observable test.

5. Refuted — do not re-attack these as stated

Doing the physics this round actively falsified these optimistic closures. Re-attempting them as written wastes effort; the honest residual (where one remains) is named.

  • gap01 — a₆ keystone. “The two routes agree, closing the keystone” is provably false — the canonical-Bochner ($E=0$) and Levi-Civita–Lichnerowicz ($E=-\mathrm{Ric}$) routes evaluate different Laplace-type operators, so by Gilkey their $a_6$ must differ. Attack the GT off-diagonals, not the “agreement”.
  • sg8 — flavor $M_R$. “$M_R$ is fixed by the Shape” is refuted (DOF obstruction: the RH neutrino is a gauge singlet, so Wilson/Hosotani cannot act; seesaw = 2 unknowns vs 1 datum). $M_R$ is a measured/calibration scale.
  • gap08 — inflation. “$K_{\sigma\sigma}=24$ is geometry-forced” is refuted — standard KK reduction gives $49.5$ or $16.36$, never $24$ ($24=4\times6$ is numerology). Declared excluded cosmology sector.
  • born — Born A2. The Born-rule chamber-measure uniqueness route is provably empty: $SU(3)$ acts trivially on the squashing-moduli base, so no compact group acts transitively and the Haar-uniqueness engine can never fire. Born-A2 survives only as the named posit AXIOM-CHAMBER-SELECTOR.

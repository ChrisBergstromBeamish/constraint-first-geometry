And ħ is the cost floor's measured residue — the size of the one value-free axiom at the bottom of the construction, read from the world the way the meter and the second are. What follows shows the same shape clearing every consistency demand a quantum theory of the forces must meet — positivity, anomalies, the graviton, the ultraviolet, causality — every gate resolved at +0, every posit and dependency named in the open.

That bridge is external and simulated — it feeds no status back into any gate — but it is what makes the geometry hard to dismiss.

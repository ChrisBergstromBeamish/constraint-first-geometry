The wall-routing protocol — derive · reduce · dissolve · anchor · wall-record

Every wall must stop floating. A wall either closes by derivation, reduces to a named deep root, dissolves because the demanded problem was artificial, or gets recorded as a real frontier wall. What is forbidden is leaving the wall vague, or pretending a local win has closed a global problem.

What this page is

This page is the routing protocol that turns an unresolved gate wall into one of a small set of legitimate terminal endpoints:

$$ \boxed{ \text{derivation, reduction-to-root, dissolution, anchoring, or wall-record} } $$

It generalizes the strategy developed for the UQF-5C interacting-graviton wall and for the deep-root and dissolution examples. It is one of three pages in /closure/, and it sits on top of two reference systems already published on this site: the seven deep roots every wall must link to, and the wall register that names the distinct frontier walls W1–W19.

The discipline is the program's spine and is enforced literally here:

anchored ≠ closed · dissolved ≠ solved · selected ≠ forced · given-$E$ ≠ derivation-of-$E$ · a wall-record is not a solution.

Core thesis

A wall is not automatically a failure. A wall is an unclassified endpoint.

The job of the closure system is to classify each wall:

$$ \text{wall} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{name it} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{test if root / dissolution / frontier} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{assign terminal status}. $$

The terminal endpoint is often not full closure. It may be anchoring the wall to a specific deep root, dissolving a false obligation that only existed because of an idealization, or recording that the remaining object is a known frontier/unicorn wall no single gate owns. Each of these is a legitimate place for the chain of "why?" to stop — provided it is named, typed, and status-labeled, so that nothing load-bearing floats silently.

The seven-root hierarchy every wall links to

Step 3 of the algorithm requires every wall to link to at least one of the seven deep roots. These are the irreducible operating floor of the framework — not all theorem-grade; some are meta-admissibility roots, some are physical posits, some remain declared posits or proof targets. The canonical statements live on the deep-roots page; the routing roles are summarized here.

Two notes carried verbatim from the deep-roots page, because walls are routinely mis-routed on them:

  • The Granularity floor is one declared posit plus one measured anchor ($\hbar$), not zero. A wall reduced to granularity is REDUCED-TO-AXIOM, never "derived from nothing."
  • The Shape root carries DERIVED-GIVEN-E carriers but a permanently OPEN absolute uniqueness. A selector wall routed to Shape is selected-not-forced; it is never promoted to "unique across all mathematics."

The universal wall-routing algorithm

Step 1 — Name the wall exactly

A vague wall cannot be routed. Replace the slogan with the exact missing object.

Bad (slogan) Good (exact missing object)
quantum gravity problem constructive non-perturbative UV completion of the interacting graviton, shared with UQF-9 (wall W1)
Lambda problem demand to cancel a Planck-scale Lorentz-invariant vacuum stress against a bare constant to match $\Lambda_{\rm obs}$
black-hole problem obligation to physically realize the $r=0$ curvature divergence produced by the continuum Schwarzschild limit

Step 2 — Decide whether the wall is real or artificial

Question If yes
Is the wall caused by an unphysical idealization? candidate DISSOLVED-CONDITIONAL
Is the wall caused by a hidden primitive? candidate REDUCED-TO-AXIOM
Is the wall a measured value? candidate MEASURED-ANCHOR / VALUE-OPEN
Is the wall a universal negative (unicorn)? candidate WALL-RECORDED / PERMANENT WALL
Is the wall a known frontier problem? candidate TERMINAL-ANCHORED / PHYSICS-OPEN
Is the wall a bounded calculation? keep as OPEN work package

Step 3 — Map the wall to roots

Use the seven-root table above. Every wall must link to at least one deep root. If a wall links to no root, it has not been named precisely enough — return to Step 1.

Step 4 — Try reduction-to-root

If the wall asks for an impossible from-nothing derivation, compress it to a named root posit. A reduction does not eliminate the anchor — it relocates the burden onto one named, paid posit plus a measured residue plus an explicit anti-claim.

$$ \text{wall burden} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{one named posit} \;+\; \text{measured residue} \;+\; \text{anti-claim}. $$

Worked example (granularity):

$$ \text{derive granularity from nothing} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Uniform Operational Cell Law} \;+\; \hbar \;+\; (\text{pre-quantum reconstruction open}). $$

Status:

$$ \boxed{\text{REDUCED-TO-AXIOM / AXIOM-CLOSED}} $$

The anti-claim is mandatory: the posit is declared, not derived; the floor is one posit, not zero (see Granularity root above).

Step 5 — Try dissolution

If the wall exists only because of an idealization or a wrong premise, dissolve it: show that the catastrophe is produced by the premise, then remove or replace the premise and watch the burden disappear.

$$ \text{wrong premise} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{catastrophe/pathology}, \qquad \text{remove/replace premise} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{burden disappears}. $$

Worked example ($\Lambda$ trace-coupling):

$$ T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm vac}=-\rho_{\rm vac}\,g_{\mu\nu} \;\Rightarrow\; T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm vac}-\tfrac14 g_{\mu\nu}T^{\rm vac}=0. $$

Status:

$$ \boxed{\text{DISSOLVED-CONDITIONAL / VALUE-OPEN}} $$

Dissolution is conditional on a declared root/premise, and it dissolves the catastrophe, never the measured value. Dissolved ≠ solved.

Step 6 — Wall-record frontier problems

If the wall is real, not artificial, and not locally closeable, record it. Bank the local wins, name the exact missing global object, and attach the anti-claim that the record is not a solution.

$$ \text{local wins} \;+\; \text{exact missing global object} \;+\; \text{anti-claim} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{TERMINAL-ANCHORED / WALL-RECORDED}. $$

This is the strategy for UQF-5C, worked in full below.

Worked example A — granularity (reduction-to-root)

  • Name the wall exactly. "Derive the granularity / cost-floor of distinguishable physical transitions from nothing."
  • Local wins banked. A finite-resources countermodel earns the no-go; the Pontryagin-style existence/discreteness equivalence is established; the action spacing $\hbar$ is a genuinely atomic measured anchor.
  • Exact missing object. A non-circular derivation of exact-global action-spectrum uniformity (deriving it from unitarity is circular by the work's own finding).
  • Wall type. root primitive.
  • Root link. Granularity / Cost-Floor (Tier-1), feeding the Shape and Scale roots downstream.
  • Routing. Step 4 — reduction-to-root.
  • Terminal endpoint. $\boxed{\text{REDUCED-TO-AXIOM / AXIOM-CLOSED}}$ (wall W4 in the register; DeepRoot-granularity gate TERMINAL-ANCHORED).
  • Allowed claim. The granularity floor is compressed to one declared value-free posit (Uniform Operational Cell Law) plus one measured anchor $\hbar$; no unpaid labels remain.
  • Forbidden claim. "Granularity is derived from nothing" / "the floor is zero posits" / "finite records prove ontological discreteness."
  • Residual work. Derive the uniformity posit from a strictly weaker, independently motivated principle without circularity (OPEN), and the granularity-$\Rightarrow$-MDL metric-selection bridge (wall W4, OPEN/bounded).

Worked example B — absolute shape irreducibility (selector wall → wall-record)

  • Name the wall exactly. "Prove the frozen $13$D branch is unique across all possible mathematics / that no future theory could do better."
  • Local wins banked. Within the internal-isometry grammar the carriers are DERIVED-GIVEN-E: weak $S^2$, hypercharge fold $S^1_Y/\mathbb Z_2$, color $K_6=SU(3)/T^2$ are forced, and $\chi(K_6,E)=-3$ gives three generations given a chosen bundle; the branch is hash-frozen (audit fact).
  • Exact missing object. A bundle-uniqueness theorem selecting the index-$(-3)$ weight without invoking "three generations" (wall W5); the absolute uniqueness claim is a universal negative.
  • Wall type. selector/category wall for the bounded part; universal negative (unicorn) for the absolute part.
  • Root link. Shape (Tier-1); Granularity (the simplicity metric the selection reads).
  • Routing. The absolute-uniqueness unicorn is dissolved as a non-target (Step 2); the bounded W5 sub-target is recorded.
  • Terminal endpoint. $\boxed{\text{TERMINAL-ANCHORED / OPEN / PERMANENT WALL}}$ (DeepRoot-shape gate TERMINAL-ANCHORED, absolute-uniqueness unicorn dissolved; bounded W5/W6 owned).
  • Allowed claim. The shape is selected-not-forced: the strongest known survivor relative to the stated category, with carriers forced given $E$.
  • Forbidden claim. "Absolute uniqueness" / "$13$D is the simplest possible shape" / "$E$ is derived" / "selected means forced."
  • Residual work. The bundle-uniqueness theorem (W5, bounded OPEN) and the flavor $F^+$ rulebook (W6, bounded OPEN). The frozen flavor geometry once forced the up-quark onto a wrong number — a real $m_u\sim4.4\sigma$ miss ($3.16$ MeV vs measured $1.27\pm0.43$ MeV), published in the open. It has since been resolved target-blind by a dimensionless $1/\sqrt6=1/\sqrt{|S_3|}$ factor read off the six-element Weyl symmetry of the flavor shape, moving the prediction to $1.295$ MeV, pull $+0.058\sigma$ (DERIVED-GIVEN-E / RESOLVED). It stays a sharp falsifiable prediction: a tighter future $m_u$ measurement tests it. It was tested at the one place it was forced onto a wrong number, and it held.

Worked example C — the $\Lambda$ catastrophe (dissolution)

  • Name the wall exactly. "Cancel a Planck-scale Lorentz-invariant vacuum stress against a bare constant to $120$ orders to match $\Lambda_{\rm obs}$."
  • Local wins banked. A tree-level decoupling theorem is PROVEN: the trace-coupling premise is the source of the catastrophe.
  • Exact missing object. Nothing for the catastrophe itself — it is artificial. The value of $\Lambda$ and its radiative stability are separate, and remain.
  • Wall type. wrong-premise / idealization.
  • Root link. Invariance (the vacuum-stress tensor structure) and Scale.
  • Routing. Step 5 — dissolution, via $T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm vac}-\tfrac14 g_{\mu\nu}T^{\rm vac}=0$.
  • Terminal endpoint. $\boxed{\text{DISSOLVED-CONDITIONAL / VALUE-OPEN}}$ ($\Lambda$-catastrophe gate TERMINAL-ANCHORED; catastrophe dissolved).
  • Allowed claim. The $120$-order fine-tuning catastrophe is dissolved conditional on the declared trace-decoupling; the catastrophe was an artifact of the trace-coupling premise.
  • Forbidden claim. "$\Lambda$ is solved" / "the value is derived" / "dissolved means solved."
  • Residual work. The measured value of $\Lambda$ is a MEASURED ANCHOR (the fifth anchor; Weinberg-open, wall W11); radiative stability $+$ R-uniqueness of the trace-decoupling is OPEN (wall W10).

Worked example D — the black-hole $r=0$ singularity (dissolution)

  • Name the wall exactly. "Physically realize the $r=0$ curvature divergence produced by the continuum Schwarzschild limit."
  • Local wins banked. The divergence is a feature of the continuum idealization; under the granularity root the exact $r=0$ point is not a physical record-bearing locus.
  • Exact missing object. The divergence is artificial; what remains is the unique interior model / dynamics, which is not derived.
  • Wall type. wrong-premise / idealization.
  • Root link. Granularity (removes the continuum infinity); Causal Order (horizon classification).
  • Routing. Step 5 — dissolution, conditional on granularity (profile ansatz).
  • Terminal endpoint. $\boxed{\text{DISSOLVED-CONDITIONAL / MODEL-OPEN}}$ (Black-hole singularity gate TERMINAL-ANCHORED; singularity dissolved + clarified).
  • Allowed claim. The $r=0$ curvature singularity is dissolved conditional on the granularity root; the divergence is a continuum idealization, not a physical obligation.
  • Forbidden claim. "The black-hole interior is solved" / "the interior model is derived" / "dissolved means solved."
  • Residual work. The unique interior profile / dynamics is MODEL-OPEN; the microstate count / Page curve is a separate bounded wall (W16, conditional on W3).

The UQF-5C wall strategy (frontier wall-record)

Original wall

UQF-5C asks for:

$$ \text{constructive non-perturbative UV completion / strong-coupling closure of the interacting graviton}. $$

That is not a reasonable per-gate local closure target. It is the shared UQF-9 wall (W1 in the register).

What is banked

The gate can bank:

  • the frozen geometry supplies the graviton operator;
  • gauge-fixing and Faddeev–Popov ghost structure are anchored to Invariance;
  • the curvature input can be proved/refuted by finite ledgers;
  • wrong dimensionful magnitudes can be quarantined;
  • $a_6$ / positivity work can be bounded below the wall.

What is not banked

The gate cannot bank:

  • full non-perturbative quantum gravity;
  • constructive UV completion;
  • strong-coupling completion;
  • above-cutoff unitarity;
  • "$a_6$ computed implies 5C closed."

Correct terminal endpoint

$$ \boxed{\text{UQF-5C} = \text{TERMINAL-ANCHORED / PHYSICS-OPEN}} \qquad\text{or}\qquad \boxed{\text{UQF-5C} = \text{WALL-RECORDED, shared with UQF-9}} $$

Status paragraph

UQF-5C has reached its legitimate terminal endpoint for this gate: not a full constructive UV completion of quantum gravity, but a precise anchoring of the wall. The frozen geometry supplies the graviton operator as a DERIVED-GIVEN-E structural result; bounded heat-kernel and positivity work may sharpen the linearized certificate; but the strong-coupling interacting graviton remains the shared UQF-9 wall (W1). The correct status is TERMINAL-ANCHORED / PHYSICS-OPEN, not CLOSED.

UQF-5C forbidden claims

Do not say:

  • UQF-5C is closed.
  • The program has a UV completion of quantum gravity.

  • A curvature input closes the gate.

Shared status vocabulary

Used consistently across all three /closure/ pages and the anchors and walls systems.

Status Meaning
CLOSED The actual target is derived, computed, or proven at the stated scope. Rare — currently used by zero physics gates.
DERIVED The claim follows from stated premises without importing the target.
DERIVED-GIVEN-E The claim follows given the upstream spectrum / input $E$; it does not derive $E$.
DERIVED-GIVEN-AXIOM The claim follows once the declared axiom is granted.
MEASURED-ANCHOR The value is empirical input / residue, not derived.
DECLARED ROOT The framework bottoms out here; the root is named and paid.
AXIOM-OPEN / declared The posit is explicitly declared and may later be reduced, but is not derived now.
REDUCED-TO-AXIOM The wall/root has been compressed to a named irreducible posit or minimal posit set.
AXIOM-CLOSED The primitive is no longer hidden; it is paid, named, and stable as an endpoint.
DISSOLVED-CONDITIONAL The demanded problem disappears once a declared root/axiom/premise is accepted.
TERMINAL-ANCHORED The gate has reached its legitimate endpoint by being tied to exact anchors and residuals.
WALL-RECORDED The remaining object is a known frontier/permanent wall, not a per-gate task.
VALUE-OPEN A catastrophe/pathology is dissolved, but the observed numerical value remains measured/open.
MODEL-OPEN A pathology is dissolved, but the unique model/interior/dynamics is not derived.
AUDIT ONLY The item validates reproducibility/object identity, not physical truth.
OPEN A real residual remains.
BLOCKED The residual cannot currently be executed because a prerequisite is missing.
ANTI-CLAIM A claim the page explicitly refuses to make.

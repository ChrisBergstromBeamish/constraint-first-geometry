6. The arithmetic / construction — the dissolution, in full

6.1 The hidden premise, and why gravity cannot test it

The catastrophe turns on three words: if it gravitates. To convert a QFT vacuum energy into a gravitational disaster you must feed $\rho_{\rm vac}$ into Einstein's equations as a source. Write the sources of the effective constant: $$ \Lambda_{\rm eff}=\Lambda_{\rm bare}+8\pi G\,\rho_{\rm vac}. $$ $\Lambda_{\rm bare}$ is a free geometric integration constant; $\rho_{\rm vac}$ is the zero-point hum. GR sees only their sum — both multiply exactly the same tensor $g_{\mu\nu}$ in exactly the same place. Two numbers that appear only ever added together, in identical positions, are one observable wearing a hand-imposed decomposition. The 1998 acceleration pinned $\Lambda_{\rm eff}\approx(2.3\,\text{meV})^4$; it said nothing — could say nothing — about the split. A universe with $\Lambda_{\rm bare}=(2.3\,\text{meV})^4$ and a non-gravitating $\rho_{\rm vac}$ is observationally identical to one with a gravitating $\rho_{\rm vac}$ delicately cancelled.

The precedent: everywhere outside gravity, only energy differences are physical. In QM this is exact — shift $H\to H+cI$ and the state picks up a global phase $|\psi(t)\rangle\to e^{-ict}|\psi(t)\rangle$; the Born squaring is phase-blind. The same phase-blindness that defines the Born rule makes absolute energy unobservable. Standard gravity is the lone law that breaks this blindness — and that single exception is the entire catastrophe.

6.2 The mechanism: unimodular gravity and the pointwise cancellation (L1)

The mechanism is not new physics — it is unimodular gravity (Einstein, 1919; flagged by Weinberg 1989 as a distinct CC-escape). Fix the determinant $\sqrt{-g}=$ fixed background volume form, so metric variations are traceless ($g_{\mu\nu}\delta g^{\mu\nu}=0$). This costs gravity one degree of freedom — the trace mode that rescales local volume, precisely the channel through which a constant vacuum energy talks to geometry. The ten Einstein equations are replaced by their trace-free part (nine independent equations): $$ R_{\mu\nu}-\tfrac14 g_{\mu\nu}R=8\pi G\big(T_{\mu\nu}-\tfrac14 g_{\mu\nu}T\big). $$

The load-bearing algebra (L1). A Lorentz-invariant vacuum energy can only enter as a term $\propto g_{\mu\nu}$ — forced, since $g_{\mu\nu}$ is the only Lorentz-invariant rank-two tensor at a point: $T^{\rm vac}_{\mu\nu}=-\rho\,g_{\mu\nu}$. Its 4D trace is $T^{\rm vac}=g^{\mu\nu}(-\rho g_{\mu\nu})=-4\rho$. Feed it into the trace-free source: $$ (-\rho g_{\mu\nu})-\tfrac14 g_{\mu\nu}(-4\rho)=-\rho g_{\mu\nu}+\rho g_{\mu\nu}=\mathbf{0,\ identically.} $$

(L1) PROVEN — tree-level trace-drop. For a vacuum stress $T^{\rm vac}_{\mu\nu}=-V g_{\mu\nu}$, the trace-free combination $T^{\rm vac}_{\mu\nu}-\tfrac14 g_{\mu\nu}T^{\rm vac}=0$ identically, for vacuum energy of any magnitude. The catastrophe-half is genuinely dissolved at the level of the local field equations — a real, elementary theorem, verified symbolically.

Three properties make L1 robust, each load-bearing for §6.4: 1. Magnitude-blind. $\rho$ never enters; it factors out and subtracts against itself. Whether $\rho=(2.3\,\text{meV})^4$ or the full Planck $3\times10^{11}\,\text{J/m}^3$, the result is the same zero. The size — the whole problem in standard GR — is irrelevant. 2. Pointwise. An algebraic identity at each event independently. No integral, no averaging over history, no boundary to reach. 3. Insensitive to time-dependence. If $\rho\to\rho(t)$ the source is still $\rho(t)g_{\mu\nu}$, still $\propto g_{\mu\nu}$, so its trace-free part is still identically zero, instant by instant.

Where $\Lambda$ comes back. The trace-free equations discard the one equation that fixed the absolute scale. Taking the covariant divergence and using conservation + the Bianchi identity integrates them once, recovering $G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda_{\rm int}g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G\,T_{\mu\nu}$, where $\Lambda_{\rm int}$ is a single global integration constant fixed by boundary data — not the vacuum energy. There is no 120-digit sum to cancel because the large term was never added.

Diagnostic — the result is specific, not vacuous. L1 is not the trivial statement "any tensor has zero trace-free part." The cancellation is specific to the pure-trace structure: a non-pure-trace source survives. Radiation ($w=1/3$) is not $\propto g_{\mu\nu}$, so its trace-free part is non-zero and it gravitates normally — which is exactly why the latent heat released at a phase transition (§6.4, Test 2) correctly gravitates and standard cosmology (BBN) is untouched. That the projector annihilates the vacuum's pure-trace stress while passing radiation's non-trace stress is what makes L1 a real structural fact rather than an artifact.

6.3 Radiative stability: the honest three-layer split (L1 / L2 / L3)

A first instinct is to declare the value protected too. This is an over-reach, and the corpus caught its own slogan. The verified theorem is a three-layer split. L1 (above) is the positive sliver. L2 and L3 are the honest corrections.

(L2) PROVEN — the premise ALONE is INSUFFICIENT at the quantum level. Under the premise the field equations recover standard GR with $\Lambda$ entering as a globally-fixed integration constant $\Lambda_0$. But an additive matter-loop vacuum shift $\mathcal L_m\to\mathcal L_m+\delta V$ passes straight through to that boundary datum: $\Lambda_0\to\Lambda_0+\delta V$ (the map is the identity). The slogan "an integration constant has no beta function, so there is nothing to renormalize" is true but irrelevant — there is no running coupling, yet the boundary value is shifted additively (Padilla–Saltas arXiv:1409.3573). The requested all-orders cancellation is FALSE for the separating premise alone.

(L3) CONDITIONAL — all-orders sequestering holds only for a strictly heavier construction, and only given an unproven assumption. The Kaloper–Padilla mechanism (arXiv:1606.04958) — gravity augmented with rigid global scalars, a Gauss–Bonnet term, and global flux / 4-volume constraints — does deliver an all-orders cancellation including virtual gravitons, but only conditional on a smoothness/non-degeneracy assumption $S$, established "in the action" rather than order-by-order, with background-curvature corrections asserted (not proven) harmless. It is strictly stronger than the separating premise, so it relocates the premise to a heavier conditional posit — it does not derive it.

The crucial scope distinction, stated once so it is never blurred: "survives all loop orders" is TRUE for the catastrophe-half (L1's tensor identity is loop-stable) and FALSE if read as "the value is radiatively protected by the premise alone" (L2 refutes that). This exact phrasing risk is logged as Hole 7.

6.4 The two physical stress tests, and the mechanism comparison

Test 1 — radiative (loop) stability of the catastrophe-half. The cancellation is algebraic in the tensor structure, not arithmetic in the magnitude. A loop correction changes the magnitude of the vacuum energy; it does not change its tensor character. Whatever the electron, the QCD condensate, or the electroweak sector contributes is still Lorentz-invariant, hence $\propto g_{\mu\nu}$, hence pure trace, hence annihilated pointwise — at one loop, two loops, every loop, at every threshold from $m_e$ up. There is no number in it to destabilize. Moreover UG's quantum effective action is itself unimodular (Padilla–Saltas arXiv:1712.09903; Smolin arXiv:0904.4841). Caveat (logged): the all-orders claim is inherited from the static-case literature; time-dependence was verified to introduce no new failure channel, but the static all-orders result was not independently re-derived (Hole 3). Verdict: DISSOLVES-CATASTROPHE, SURVIVES-LOOPS — no value smuggled; matter/light intact; Weinberg evaded; value unexplained.

Test 2 — the phase transitions (the make-or-break). Across the QCD ($\sim150\,\text{MeV}$) and electroweak ($\sim100\,\text{GeV}$) transitions the condensate changes in time; a leak of order $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}^4$ ($\sim10^{44}\times\Lambda_{\rm obs}$) or $v^4$ ($\sim10^{55}\times\Lambda_{\rm obs}$) would kill the reframe: - A time-varying $\rho_{\rm vac}(t)g_{\mu\nu}$ is still $\propto g_{\mu\nu}$ at each instant, so its traceless part is identically zero at every $t$ (verified over a $t$-sweep; max residual $0$). - The condensate is not separately conserved while changing ($\nabla^\mu T^{\rm vac}_{\mu\nu}=-\partial_\nu\rho_{\rm vac}\neq0$); by total conservation the released latent heat flows into radiation/particles, which gravitates correctly ($w=1/3$ is non-pure-trace) — the wanted kind of leak; BBN safe. - Wrongly-gravitating residual $\approx0$; largest surviving non-trace leftover today $\sim10^{-13}\,\text{J/m}^3\approx10^{-4}\,\Lambda_{\rm obs}$ — too small to be $\Lambda$. - The integration constant $\Lambda_{\rm int}$ is uncontaminated by the epoch jumps (the jump lives in the dynamical $(R,T_{\rm tot})$ pair, not the constant).

The "necessary-conditions ledger" makes explicit that four of five conditions are automatic (Lorentz+translation invariance; Bianchi; UG structure; the algebraic identity), and everything collapses onto condition #5: gravity decouples the trace pointwise/locally — the load-bearing posit. That single posit is the entire conditionality of the result.

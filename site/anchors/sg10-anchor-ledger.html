SG-10 — Claim boundary + scope walls: the gate anchor ledger

The honest one-line: SG-10 is the disclosure gate — it closes no physics, derives no number, and rides no new hash. Its single job is to name, in public, every sector the scoped theory does not claim, and to bind — by a checkable rule, not by authorial restraint — that no required gate is rescued by narrowing scope and no excluded sector is leaned on to close a required gate. Its honest grade is DISCLOSED, and that grade upgrades nothing.

This page is the gate-specific instantiation of the anchoring method, applied to a gate unlike any other: SG-1…SG-9 each claim the geometry does something; SG-10 claims the manuscript does something — it states its own boundary out loud and binds itself to honor it. Every exact thing SG-10 touches gets its own row — its status, what it is allowed to claim, and what it is forbidden to claim. It follows the same eleven-part shape and universal table as the SG-4 ledger; only the gate's nature differs.

selection ≠ derivation · given-E ≠ derivation-of-E · frozen/reproducible ≠ proven-unique.

1. Gate status header

Taxonomy reconciliation (2026-07-05). Under the two-axis taxonomy SG-10 is resolved at a terminal — the disclosure mechanism is real, present, and falsifiable (DISCLOSED) — with its scope-lint audit shown as a pending residual (flagged, not silently merged). Read as terminal reached + residual shown; counted among the resolved-with-residual gates, not physics-closed.

  • Gate-level SG-10 roll-up: DISCLOSED. Not DERIVED, not PARTIAL (there is no physics in a scope gate to derive or partly close); not OPEN / not-claimed (the disclosure mechanism is real, present, and falsifiable). DISCLOSED is exactly right.
  • The closed local leg for a disclosure gate is not a physics obstruction $O(E)=0$ but a disclosure-consistency predicate over the manuscript text. Writing it as the gate's analog of the local-leg formula: $$ C_{\rm SG10}(\text{text}) \;=\; \big(\,\forall\, s\in S_{\rm excl}:\ s\in\S 9.4\,\big)\ \wedge\ \big(\,\forall\, g\in G_{1..10}:\ \mathrm{support}(g)\cap S_{\rm excl}=\varnothing\,\big), $$ i.e. clause (i) excluded ↛ required AND clause (ii) required ↛ excluded, quantified over every gate certificate and every exclusion row.
  • Status of the predicate: VERIFIED-BY-READING / AUDIT — the boundary holds by careful reading, but the lint that would make $C_{\rm SG10}$ machine-real is AUDIT (referenced, not independently re-run). The honest phrasing is "verified by reading, pending the lint," never "machine-enforced."

A disclosure gate has a different ceiling from a physics gate: there is no DERIVED-CLOSED to reach. The achievable top is DISCLOSED-VERIFIED — machine-checked completeness plus machine-checked honesty. What stays open is everything that converts asserted into machine-verified: the lint run, the completeness witness, the per-wall conservatism map, and the cross-paper coherence scan.

2. Frozen inputs (what SG-10 stands on, not what it produces)

SG-10 acts on text objects (a boundary ledger, two clauses, a lint, a downgrade rule, three fences) and references the walls owned by other campaigns. The seven excluded sectors (§9.4) are: quantum-gravity UV completion; full cosmology (inflation, reheating, CMB, structure); dark matter; dark energy / $\Lambda$; baryogenesis / $\eta_B$; strong CP / $\bar\theta$; Theory of Everything (all-sector, incl. the thermodynamic arrow). Each excluded row carries a missing-certificate reason and the status Outside scoped-GUT claim. Status of every wall: GIVEN / owned-elsewhere / entirely OPEN — SG-10 only names and fences them.

6. The construction — the disclosure mechanism, in full

A disclosure gate's "arithmetic" is the predicate logic of the boundary, not a number. The five operative objects:

(1) The boundary ledger (§9.4). A 16-row table: nine Required Gate rows (status Yes / Claimed) and seven Excluded rows (status No / Outside scoped-GUT claim), each excluded row carrying a missing-certificate Reason. The attack handle is completeness relative to the declared non-GUT universe.

(2) The two binding clauses (§9.1; §1.2.1). Each is a universal over the manuscript text: $$ \text{clause (i):}\ \forall\, s\in S_{\rm excl}:\ s\in\S 9.4,\qquad \text{clause (ii):}\ \forall\, g\in G_{1..10}:\ \mathrm{support}(g)\cap S_{\rm excl}=\varnothing . $$ A single counterexample falsifies either clause — that is precisely what makes the boundary falsifiable, not a vibe.

(3) The scope-consistency lint (certificates/G11_claim_boundary/). The mechanical implementation of the two clauses as two text checks: clause (i) — every excluded sector appears in §9.4; clause (ii) — no Gate 1–10 certificate cites an excluded sector for closure or carries Excluded from scope. Status: AUDIT (referenced, not re-run; folder absent from the rendered tree).

(4) The downgrade asymmetry (§6.12; Downgrade Rules). When the Gate-11 falsifier fires — any excluded sector invoked to support a required gate — the penalty falls inward: it downgrades the offending required gate to Open / not claimed, not SG-10. SG-10 makes no physics claim, so there is nothing in it to downgrade; pointing the penalty at the offender is what gives a disclosure gate real teeth on the other ten.

(5) The three fences (Fence 1 Diagnostic-only modes; Fence 2 $\Lambda$ downstream pointer; Fence 3 §11 bridge) plus the §9.4a historical firewall — the most attackable surface, all PASS by reading, conditional on the lint.

Diagnostic — the disclosure is specific, not trivial. A disclosure layer naïvely has no teeth (it makes no claim, so there is nothing to falsify). The construction is non-trivial exactly because it carries a concrete refuting witness: a single improper citation falsifies a quantified clause, and the inward-falling penalty makes that falsification land on a real, named gate. The firewall test discriminates sharply — it keeps the unknown seesaw scale $M_R$ as an SG-8 required-gate residual, not relocated into the SG-10 exclusion list (a relocation clause (ii) forbids). A boundary that mechanically refuses that relocation, and names a refuting witness, is doing real work, not bookkeeping.

7. Declared-structure splits — one phrase, several honest objects

The single phrase "the claim boundary is machine-enforced" hides several different claims with different statuses:

  1. The boundary ledger exists and reproduces by hand — DERIVED-GIVEN (text) (W1).
  2. Both clauses are stated and bind both directions — DERIVED-GIVEN (text) (W2).
  3. The clauses are machine-checked across the whole text — AUDIT (W4): the lint is referenced, not re-run.
  4. The three fences are proven (not merely read) — CONDITIONAL (W6/W7/W8): airtight by reading, not lint-proven.

So the ledger and the clauses are present-and-reproducible, the machine enforcement is AUDIT, and the fences are conditional. Bright line: the public popup/ledger present the lint as active enforcement; this page is more careful — "verified by reading, pending the lint," never "machine-enforced" stated as proven.

The single phrase "the scope is narrowed honestly" likewise splits: sector-relocation (moving a required gate into exclusions) is fenced explicitly by §1.2.1, while target-narrowing (redefining a required gate to a weaker target — SG-6, SG-8, SG-9) is fenced only implicitly, via the separate Downgrade Rules table. R5 is the open task of generalizing these into one stated rule.

9. Anti-claims (what this page refuses to say)

  • SG-10 does not close, derive, or certify any physics. Every wall it names — $\Lambda$, the Yang–Mills mass gap, baryogenesis, strong CP, quantum-gravity UV completion — stays entirely OPEN, owned elsewhere.
  • The boundary is not (yet) machine-enforced. The lint (certificates/G11_claim_boundary/) is AUDIT — referenced, not re-run, absent from the rendered tree. The honest phrasing is "verified by reading, pending the lint."
  • The three fences are not proven — they are Conditional (airtight by reading; the un-run lint is what would prove them).
  • Completeness is not a theorem. "Every relevant non-GUT sector is named" is a universal over an open-ended sector space; the honest ceiling is complete relative to the declared seven-sector universe, with a witness and an open challenge — not exhaustive over all conceivable physics.
  • An attractive partial result is never wall progress. The bulk $a_6$ is a consistency coefficient (not a UV completion; total NOT emitted); the Gap-02 $D\le0$ is a proxy (not a target-negative); BG-10 Rule A AXIOM-CLOSED is one reduced posit (BG-10 stays 0/4). Stating any wall as more closed than its owner verified is the SG-10-specific deflation violation.
  • $\Lambda$'s catastrophe-half is DISSOLVED, not solved. It was a fake wall built on the unmeasured absolute vacuum energy; the observed small $\Lambda$ is a separate Weinberg-irreducible residue. Dissolved ≠ solved.
  • The frozen-branch hashes are audit anchors; they do not validate the physics. And the "Battle-WIN" framing is a disclosure-practice observation, never a physics certificate.

10. Specialist closure plan

Each open residual is a concrete, finite, target-blind work-package. The cardinal point: the win on this gate is machine-reality + honesty, never a status change — no path may quietly promote a wall or claim any excluded-sector physics.

  1. R1 — completeness witness. Build/publish the witness table; assert AXIOM-SCOPE-UNIVERSE (the closed seven-sector list, every other sector a required gate or a subsumed sub-sector); issue the "name a missing sector" challenge. Falsifier (a valid close): a candidate that is genuinely a required gate hiding in exclusions → clause-(ii) violation → that gate downgrades. Endpoint: AXIOM-CLOSED at AXIOM-SCOPE-UNIVERSE.
  2. R2 — three fences. Run the lint so it emits each Gate 1–10 support set with status labels; assert AXIOM-NO-DIAGNOSTIC-SUPPORT. Falsifier: a required certificate cites a fenced object → Gate-11 falsified → offender downgrades.
  3. R3 — the lint. Mount the script + rule set; run target-blind over the frozen text; confirm both clauses PASS; emit the support-set report. Machine-lane, fail-closed, no axiom, no new physics. BLOCKED_INPUTS until mounted → then VERIFIED or REFUTED. Highest value-per-effort; do this first among the machine tasks.
  4. R4 — per-wall conservatism. Run the consistency sweep against the campaign floors ($\Lambda$ Weinberg-OPEN; Gap-02 Precisely-OPEN [X]; strong-CP observation-blocked; BG-10 0/4; Gap-01 $a_6$ Precisely-OPEN); assert AXIOM-WALL-CONSERVATISM. SG-10 never leads — a dependent of the owning campaigns. Endpoint: DISCLOSED-CONSISTENT.
  5. R5 — silent-narrowing. Confirm §1.2.1 + Downgrade Rules cover the three target-narrowings (each card-declared, remainder labeled Diagnostic/Open); adopt AXIOM-NO-SILENT-NARROWING as one rule; widen the lint's clause (ii). Endpoint: AXIOM-CLOSED (a genuine strengthening).
  6. R6 — cross-paper coherence. Cross-paper grep for each excluded sector + status-label scan; assert AXIOM-CORPUS-BOUNDARY-COHERENCE. Endpoint: AXIOM-CLOSED, one conditional row (strong-CP) pending the grep.
  7. R7 — §9.6 remark. Confirm cited by no gate certificate; keep the four-values framing at reduction strength (AXIOM-UNFALSIFIABLE-REMARK-FENCED). Endpoint: DISCLOSED-CONSISTENT.
  8. R8 — Battle-WIN framing. Keep at interpretation strength; keep the rival comparison out of this gate's certificate (AXIOM-DISCLOSURE-WIN-IS-META). Endpoint: DISCLOSED-CONSISTENT.

Closing all eight upgrades SG-10 from DISCLOSED-with-asserted-lint toward DISCLOSED-VERIFIED — and even then, only a machine-verified, conservatism-mapped boundary, not a closure of any wall.

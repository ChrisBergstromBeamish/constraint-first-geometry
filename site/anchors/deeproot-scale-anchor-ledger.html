DeepRoot-scale — Deep root - absolute scale (the magnitude discipline): the gate anchor ledger

The honest one-line: DeepRoot-scale has a real, banked terminal — the root rests on its anchor floor. The existence of at least one absolute dimensionful scale is theorem-grade (forced by unit-gauge invariance), and the framework carries exactly two measured rulers — $M_{\rm Pl}$ (the absolute scale, MEASURED) and $v_{\rm EW}$ (the electroweak scale, certified as a legitimate second measured ruler at SG-5) — with every dimensionless ratio traced back to them; but the value of each ruler is measured, not derived, and a family of dimensionful magnitudes across the candidate remains SCALE-PAID, SCALE-OPEN, or SCALE-DISSOLVED. Anchored is not derived.

It follows the same eleven-part shape and the same universal table as the sibling DeepRoot-shape and DeepRoot-granularity ledgers.

6. The construction — the anchor floor and the discipline, in full

The unit-gauge theorem (the existence half). Physics is unchanged under a rescaling of units (changing the GeV, the second, the kilogram). Treat that rescaling as a gauge symmetry — a unit gauge. Under it, every dimensionful number is multiplied by its conversion factor, so a naked dimensionful number carries no invariant content. Only two kinds of statement are unit-gauge invariant: (i) a dimensionless ratio (all unit factors cancel), or (ii) a dimensionful value expressed relative to an accepted anchor (the anchor transforms the same way). This is the Buckingham-$\pi$ content stated as a closure rule, and it forces the three consequences that are the discipline:

  1. A dimensionless invariant can be derived directly — a $\pi$-group; no anchor, no bridge (the safe predictions cluster in the ratio column, SCL-A).
  2. A dimensionful magnitude requires an anchor plus a bridge plus a scheme/window — gauge-dependent until tied to $M_{\rm Pl}$ (or a declared measured scale), carried by a named bridge (SCL-J), fixed by scheme/window (SCL-E). All three, or it is not a magnitude claim.
  3. No anchor $\Rightarrow$ no magnitude claim, ever — Buckingham-$\pi$ forces $\geq 1$ dimensionful anchor before any dimensionful prediction can exist. This is the floor of the root (SCL-I): the anchor count is $\geq 1$, forever; named, not eliminated.

So the existence half is forced, not asserted: at least one absolute dimensionful anchor is required to make any dimensionful statement unit-gauge invariant. What is measured, not forced, is which value that anchor takes. Existence: theorem. Value: MEASURED. The unit gauge is exactly why those two halves can never be merged.

The value half (the anchor floor). The framework charges a small measured-anchor set $\{\,M_{\rm Pl},\ \alpha_i(M_Z),\ y_t,\ |V_{us}|\,\}$, with $v_{\rm EW}$ a certified-irreducible second measured ruler (SG-5). $M_{\rm Pl}$ is the absolute dimensionful anchor that lets dimensionless ratios and readouts be cashed into physical numbers. Its value is read from experiment and charged as input; Planck-unit smallness of another quantity (e.g. $\Lambda\approx 10^{-122}\,M_{\rm Pl}^4$) is a restatement of a measured value, not a derivation of it.

The categorization map (every number gets a tag). No number appears on a gate or wall without exactly one of six mutually exclusive tags: SCALE-FREE-DERIVED (dimensionless invariant / exact ratio), SCALE-ANCHORED (dimensionful, anchor supplied, bridge forced), SCALE-PAID (fitted normalization / declared scheme object — an input, not a prediction), SCALE-CONSISTENCY-COEFFICIENT (internal check, not gate-closing), SCALE-DISSOLVED (the requested magnitude was never a valid scheme-independent object), SCALE-OPEN (bridge / scheme / anchor missing). SCALE-DISSOLVED is not a success — it removes a false debt; it does not pay a real one.

The scale-status transition rule. A number does not jump categories for free; each move needs a required event actually exhibited. SCALE-OPEN$\to$SCALE-PAID requires the missing scale supplied as a declared input; SCALE-PAID$\to$SCALE-FORCED requires a target-blind bridge deriving it from the roots (passing the Scale Perturbation Test and forcing field 4); SCALE-CONSISTENCY-COEFFICIENT$\to$SCALE-DERIVED requires it to be shown a real observable or a necessary audit invariant; SCALE-DISSOLVED$\to$closed is never automatic — closing requires a real surviving observable, never a dissolution.

The dimensionless-first doctrine. Derive the ratio, then — and only then, with a named anchor and a named bridge — reach for the magnitude. Most of the time the ratio closes and the magnitude stays SCALE-PAID or SCALE-OPEN. That is the honest shape of the result: over-determination — many derived dimensionless ratios against a few paid scales — is the actual evidence, and it is destroyed, not strengthened, by dressing paid scales up as predictions.

7. The naturalness split — a property is not a value

The single seductive move "it is natural, therefore derived" is refused and split into honest objects. A magnitude being small, radiatively stable, technically natural, symmetry-protected, or Planck-expressible does not derive it — each is a statement about how a number behaves, none supplies the number:

  • Small — a comparison, not a source; a small value can still be measured.
  • Radiatively stable — stability says corrections do not blow the value up; it does not say what it is (SCL-G). This is the $\Lambda$ trap.
  • Technically natural / symmetry-protected — a protecting symmetry makes a small value unsurprising; it does not compute it.
  • Planck-expressible — writing a measured number in Planck units is a restatement, not a derivation (the $10^{-122}$ trap).

Therefore naturalness dissolves a tuning sub-problem; it cannot supply a measured magnitude. The residual relocates cleanly: the tuning half is SCALE-DISSOLVED where a protection mechanism applies (a real result — it removes a false debt); the value half stays MEASURED / SCALE-OPEN-as-derivation until a target-blind value bridge exists. Report the two on separate lines — never collapse "it is natural" into "it is derived." This applies wherever a naturalness argument is made: the cosmological constant $\Lambda$, the Higgs / electroweak hierarchy, the inflationary amplitude $A_s$, and mass gaps.

8. The shared-scheme-object rule — settle once, propagate (SCL-H)

The single phrase "the scheme is fixed" hides several objects used by more than one gate; each must be settled once and propagated, never re-chosen per gate to suit each target:

  • (A) Heat-kernel scheme object — shared across Gap-01 ($a_6$), SG-6 (moduli), and the threshold/RG-matching gate (W19). Re-choosing it per gate is a SCL-H violation and a disguised reverse-engineering from the measured value.
  • (B) Sector-scale policy ($\kappa$, $N_d, N_e, N_\nu$, $M_R$, neutrino-scale policy) — shared across the flavor (W06), neutrino-mass, and leptogenesis (W12) gates. The fitted normalizations are the program's weakest scale link and the most exposed to over-counting; they are terminal-by-anchoring inside the $E$-anchor, not derived.
  • (C) RG matching scales / scheme / cutoff — shared across thresholds (W19), unification, and electroweak ($\rho_{\rm tree}$, W08) claims.

Therefore the unifying anti-pattern is reverse-engineering from the measured value: choosing the bridge, scheme, normalization, or scale because it reproduces the observed number. Reverse-engineering from the measured value fails field 4 of the forcing certificate automatically, and it is the single most important thing the Scale root is built to catch. The live residual relocates: R-SCL-a (target-blind value bridges for $\Lambda$, thresholds, inflation, baryogenesis) is OPEN; R-SCL-b (generated, not paid, sector scales / $M_R$) is OPEN; R-SCL-c (the absolute-scale existence proof with no smuggled second anchor) is OPEN.

  • The anchor value itself — the whole readout bottoms on $M_{\rm Pl}$, which no known principle forces; its value is a measured input and "$M_{\rm Pl}$ is derived" is refused. MEASURED-ANCHOR (declare, do not plug).

10. Anti-claims (what this page refuses to say)

  • DeepRoot-scale does not derive the value of $M_{\rm Pl}$. Existence of an absolute scale is a theorem; the number is measured.
  • A formula producing a number is not a prediction of that number. "The theory predicts this dimensionful value" is forbidden unless the anchor, units, scheme, energy window, and bridge are all shown.
  • Stability is not value. "The value is stable, therefore derived" is forbidden — a protection mechanism dissolves an instability problem; it does not supply the magnitude (the $\Lambda$ trap).
  • Ratios do not create units. "This exact ratio proves the absolute magnitude" is forbidden — a derived dimensionless ratio says nothing about the absolute scale until an anchor and a bridge are supplied.
  • A selected normalization is a paid scale choice unless forced. "The normalization is harmless" is forbidden; "metric-selected" is exact only under the declared normalization, not normalization-robust.
  • A coupling scale is not an existence proof. "$\Lambda_{\rm QCD}$ (or any coupling scale) proves the gap exists" is forbidden — moving the target is not closing it.
  • Planck-unit smallness is not a derivation. "$10^{-122}$ in Planck units is the derivation" is forbidden — it is a restatement of the measured value; the observed value must never back-solve structure.
  • In odd $D=13$ the dimensionful $a_6$ magnitude has no canonical finite predicate; it is consistency-only or dissolved, never gate-closing.
  • Shared scheme objects are settled once and propagated; "we re-chose the scheme object for this gate" is reverse-engineering from the measured value in a scheme costume.
  • Dissolved is not solved. A dissolution removes a false debt; it does not pay the real one or close a gate — closing always needs a real surviving observable.
  • The frozen-branch hashes are audit anchors; they do not validate the magnitudes, and reproducibility ≠ derivation.
  • Anchored is not derived. Resting on the $M_{\rm Pl}$ floor is a legitimate terminal, not a claim that the value was produced by the geometry.

11. Specialist closure plan

Each open residual is a concrete, finite, target-blind work-package. The target-blindness gate is mandatory for every value bridge: the bridge must be writable without the observed value — a bridge chosen because it reproduces the number fails field 4 automatically and does not count. Recommended order: Hole S1 → S2 → S3 → S4.

  1. Hole S1 — Absolute-scale existence proof (address first). Prove from the invariance and record roots (R1 + R4) that exactly one independent absolute scale is required, and that no second independent dimensionful anchor is smuggled in elsewhere in the generator. Realistic near-term best outcome: "existence theorem-grade; single-anchor uniqueness reduced to a named check." The floor stays $\geq 1$ anchor ($M_{\rm Pl}$) by design, forever.
  2. Hole S2 — Target-blind value bridges. For each of $\Lambda$, the threshold/RG rows (W19, SG-7), inflation ($A_s, r, n_s$), and baryogenesis ($M_N, T_{\rm reheat}$): produce a filled bridge certificate (RG flow / decoupling / threshold rule / compactification map / FRG map, or explicit dissolution) that is writable without the observed value. Trap: measurement is not a bridge — it supplies the one anchor (SCL-I), it does not carry a magnitude across scales.
  3. Hole S3 — Generated (not paid) sector scales. Move $N_d, N_e, N_\nu$ and $M_R$ from SCALE-PAID toward SCALE-FORCED by a target-blind generation, or — the honest expected outcome — declare them the irreducible measured content of the $E$-anchor (terminal-by-anchoring). Trap: do not present a fitted normalization as an output.
  4. Hole S4 — Uniform Yang–Mills gap in the limit. Establish a uniform mass-gap bound surviving the continuum and volume limits in a stated scheme and window. Honest odds LOW (a recognized hard problem; attempt last). Trap: do not substitute a coupling scale for the existence proof.

Closing Holes S1–S3 is the full value-bridge result — the only path from a named anchor floor toward target-blind magnitude forcing — and even then, only with the anchor value itself remaining a measured input, and never past the measurement-is-not-a-bridge line.

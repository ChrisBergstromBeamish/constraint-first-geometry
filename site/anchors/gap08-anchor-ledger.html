Gap-08 — Inflation spectrum: the gate anchor ledger

The honest one-line: Gap-08 has two genuine, target-blind narrowing wins — one candidate inflaton is killed by computation and the surviving plateau's tilt direction is read off the frozen split with no dial — but the gate as a whole is OPEN (cascade) and, decisively, lives inside a sector the programme has declared out of scope, so even a perfect spectrum match would stay a diagnostic consistency, never a derivation.

6. The construction — the two wins, at full depth

The geometry touches cosmology through one forward chain — no new geometry is searched, and no in-scope claim may rest on it.

Win #1 — the $\rho$-inflaton is computed DEAD (KK-stiff). Of the two candidate directions the split offers, $\rho$ has an effective mass at the Kaluza–Klein scale, far above the slow-roll scale, so it physically cannot inflate. KK-stiffness is a target-blind discriminator: it converts "which field inflates?" — normally a free choice — into a computed narrowing from two candidates to one. Honest grade: qualitative; the countersign slot is pending; a certificate-grade kill is the optional sharpening, not required because the $\sigma$-survivor carries the gate. The NT-1 $\chi$-leg run gate-halted on a scale artifact (VOID both directions = inconclusive, not evidence either way); the $\rho$ verdict stands independently.

Win #2 — the dial-free plateau slope. For the $\sigma$ exponential plateau, $$ \lambda^2 = \frac{4}{K_{\sigma\sigma}},\qquad \text{(claimed)}\ K_{\sigma\sigma}=24 \ \Longrightarrow\ \lambda^2 = \frac{4}{24} = \frac16, $$ with no dial available once the split is fixed. The tilt direction — a red tilt ($n_s<1$) — is geometry-fixed given the split. The corpus checks this at three layers and fails closed at the third:

  1. Arithmetic layer (checkable, trivially true). $\lambda^2=1/6 \Leftrightarrow K_{\sigma\sigma}=24$ is pure bookkeeping. (Verified exact: $4/24=1/6$; note $4/9\ne 1/6$.) This is algebra, not physics.
  2. Plateau-phenomenology layer (order-of-magnitude sanity check). A plateau gives $n_s\approx 1-2/N_*$; at $N_*\approx 55$, $n_s\approx 1-2/55\approx 0.9636$ — the same Planck-dead-center region as the banked band — and $r$ at the $10^{-2}$–$10^{-3}$ order. A sanity check, NOT a derivation.
  3. Geometry layer — FAIL-CLOSE (BLOCKER A1-GEOM). That $K_{\sigma\sigma}=24$ is forced by the split and not chosen requires reading the frozen $\sigma$-kinetic reduction source (the D.1 region, $\approx$ corpus line L10268), which is not in scope and must not be fabricated. Until read in scope and verified, $\lambda^2=1/6$ stays a THEOREM-CANDIDATE.

Diagnostic — the result is specific, not trivial. The slope's robustness profile discriminates: for an exponential plateau the tilt is dominated by the $-2/N_*$ term and is only weakly sensitive to $\lambda^2$, which is exactly why $n_s$ is recorded as "near-universal across branches" — while $r$ spans $[3.5,36]\times10^{-3}$, a factor of $\sim 10$, across the four conventions. That $n_s$ stays pinned while $r$ fans out by an order of magnitude is what makes the structure a real geometric datum rather than a convention-driven artifact: the direction is forced; the magnitude relocates. A second specificity check: the distinct-quantity caution $24/9=8/3$ is non-trivial and non-zero — $K_{\sigma\sigma}=24$ (slope) and $K=99/11=9$ (normalization) are not the same object, and the $8/3$ bridge must be accounted, not assumed away.

The forward-chain "obstruction" split. The local narrowing is $$ O_{\rm Gap08,local}(\text{split}) = \big(\rho\text{ DEAD},\ \lambda^2 = 1/6\big), $$ closed only as narrowing given the split (qualitative + theorem-candidate). The full gate additionally carries the magnitude conventions, $N_*$, $A_s$, the measurement, and the scope wall — none closed here.

Traceability: the construction, the three-layer slope check, and the re-verified arithmetic are reproduced in the Gap-08 dossier §3.3–3.6.

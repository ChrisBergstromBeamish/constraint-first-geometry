6.2 The no-mediator identity (Lemma 2, DERIVED-GIVEN-E)

For any sector-respecting mediator $M=\sum_i \Pi_i M_i\Pi_i$, $$\Pi_q M\Pi_\ell=\sum_i(\Pi_q\Pi_i)\,M_i\,(\Pi_i\Pi_\ell)=\sum_i \delta_{qi}\,\delta_{i\ell}\,\Pi_i M_i\Pi_i=0\quad(q\neq\ell).$$ This is a theorem given the projectors, not an axiom.

The Anchor Hierarchy — overview

Status box (read first). This page explains the anchor hierarchy. It does not prove the whole theory, derive all roots from nothing, or prove absolute uniqueness. The framework bottoms out in seven deep roots. Downstream claims must be generated, measured, charged, audit-only, open, blocked, or anti-claimed.

1. One-line thesis

Physics must bottom out in invariant operational roots, then use observable/auditable anchors to test concrete gate claims.

Everything in the /anchors/ section is organized as a single descending chain of accountability:

Deep roots  →  master / fundamental anchors  →  gate-specific closure anchors  →  gate status

Read left to right, this is what grounds what. Read right to left, it is what every gate claim is status-limited by. No object on this page floats: each named anchor is typed as a deep root, a master anchor, a gate anchor, a measured input, an audit artifact, an open residual, or an anti-claim. This page is the map of that chain. It is a traceability map, not an absolute-uniqueness proof — it shows how every later anchor page hangs off the roots, not that the roots (or the shape) are forced.

2. Status header — roots are not all theorem-grade

The framework bottoms out in seven deep roots. These roots are not all theorem-grade. Some are close to operational tautologies about how any theory meets evidence at all; some are declared posits we adopt with eyes open; one (Shape) is an explicitly selected and frozen structural branch, not a derived or absolutely-unique object. We label each root with one of the allowed status labels below and never silently upgrade it. In particular we do not claim the seven roots are proven, and we do not claim the 13D shape is absolutely unique.

The strongest honest statement the whole hierarchy defends is bounded:

Given the frozen 13D shape $M_4 \times K_6 \times S^2 \times S^1_Y/\mathbb{Z}_2$ (with $K_6 = SU(3)/T^2$) and given the observed Standard-Model matter content $E$, the framework reads off the SM gauge algebra, three chiral generations, and charge quantization from geometry. The shape itself remains a declared, frozen anchor — not a uniqueness theorem.

Three discipline lines travel with every page and are never dropped: selection ≠ derivation · given-$E$ ≠ derivation-of-$E$ · frozen/reproducible ≠ proven-unique.

Allowed status labels (the only vocabulary used on this page): DECLARED ROOT · GENERATED · DERIVED-GIVEN-E · MEASURED · CHARGED · AUDIT ONLY · OPEN · BLOCKED · ANTI-CLAIM.

3. Seven deep roots summary

The hierarchy bottoms out in exactly these seven roots, in this order:

  1. Physical Equivalence / Invariance Root
  2. Record Interface Root
  3. Causal Order Root
  4. Granularity / Cost-Floor Root
  5. Scale Root
  6. Shape Root
  7. Nonseparability / Global State Constraint Root

Each root carries an operational statement, an honest status label, and what it supports. Every downstream master anchor is traceable to at least one of these roots; nothing downstream is admissible under the hierarchy without such a trace.

4. Master / fundamental anchors summary

The master anchors are the framework-wide, observable/auditable objects that sit one level below the roots. Each one is traceable to a root and is the currency in which gate claims are charged or measured. None is a free label.

  • Finite invariant empirical records — the observable interface itself: a claim earns status only by connecting to finite physical observables. (Traceable to Record Interface + Invariance. Type: master anchor / AUDIT ONLY interface.)
  • No unpaid exact labels — every exact label (gauge groups, charge tables, generation count, anomaly-admissible spectra, bundle choices) must be generated by invariant structure, charged as primitive input, or marked open. (Traceable to Invariance. Type: master anchor. Status of the rule: a clean accounting rule, given the cost metric.)
  • Full-generator cost / MDL pressure — a theory's cost is the cost of its full generator: geometry + grammar + rulebook + bundle data + measured anchors + fitted normalizations + selector choices + the map to observables. (Traceable to Granularity / Cost-Floor. Type: master anchor. Status: OPEN as the proven-correct metric — not absolutely proven against every competing metric.)
  • $M_{\rm Pl}$ — the accepted absolute dimensionful value anchor required by the Scale root. (Traceable to Scale. Type: master anchor / MEASURED value; CHARGED as a primitive calibration input.)
  • Frozen 13D branch — the selected/frozen shape $M_4 \times K_6 \times S^2 \times S^1_Y/\mathbb{Z}_2$ that is the object under test. (Traceable to Shape. Type: master anchor / DECLARED ROOT instance; uniqueness OPEN.)
  • $E_{\rm SM}$ / matter bundle — the observed Standard-Model chiral spectrum and the chosen bundle on it. (Traceable to Shape + Nonseparability. Type: master anchor / CHARGED primitive input — given $E$, not derived. $E$ is bundled into Shape, not separately derived.)
  • Hashes / manifests — the freeze-before-compare audit artifacts ($dcc66f1b2685$ / $a5b1e6f9d951$). (Traceable to Record Interface. Type: audit artifact / AUDIT ONLY.) Hashes validate frozen-object identity / audit integrity only — they do not validate the physics.
  • $c$, $\hbar$, $k_B$ — operational / residue / bridge anchors fixing the unit system and dimensional bridges. (Traceable to Scale + Record Interface. Type: master anchor / MEASURED bridge constants.)

5. Gate anchor summary

Below the master anchors, each gate has its own anchor ledger page. The rule is uniform: a gate page lists the exact objects the gate touches, the status of each (using only the allowed labels), the allowed claim, the forbidden claim, and the open residual. This is where "the rule-set applied" lives.

The key discipline: local gate closure is not whole-gate closure. A gate may have a real, checkable local win (e.g. an anomaly book that balances exactly by rational arithmetic for the frozen spectrum) while the gate as a whole stays OPEN. The canonical worked example is the hypercharge/anomaly gate (SG-4): the local arithmetic is a genuine DERIVED-GIVEN-E win, yet the local win neither selects the Standard Model nor closes the quantum/global completion. Specifically, anomaly cancellation is a filter, not a selector — the observed spectrum passes $A(E_{\rm SM}) = 0$; it is not picked out by it, so $E$ stays a CHARGED primitive. We do not claim anomaly cancellation selects the Standard Model.

Every gate page is built to one reusable template, so each gate follows the same shape and the same ledger columns.

6. Traceability diagram

The whole system reduces to one walk, applied uniformly to every gate claim:

$$ \textbf{Deep root} \;\longrightarrow\; \textbf{master anchor} \;\longrightarrow\; \textbf{gate anchor} \;\longrightarrow\; \textbf{claim status} $$

In words, and with the typing made explicit at every hop:

Deep root            →  master anchor              →  gate anchor                 →  claim status
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Invariance           →  no unpaid exact labels     →  gate's exact label objects  →  GENERATED / CHARGED / OPEN
Record Interface     →  finite invariant records   →  gate certificates/falsifier →  AUDIT ONLY / MEASURED
Causal Order         →  causal-consistency anchor   →  gate construction order     →  DECLARED ROOT-limited
Granularity/Cost-Floor → full-generator cost (MDL) →  gate's charged primitives   →  CHARGED / OPEN
Scale                →  M_Pl ; c, ħ, k_B           →  gate's dimensionful inputs   →  MEASURED / CHARGED
Shape                →  frozen 13D branch          →  gate's geometric readout    →  DERIVED-GIVEN-E
Nonseparability      →  E_SM / matter bundle       →  gate's global-sector data    →  CHARGED / OPEN / ANTI-CLAIM

A claim is admissible under the hierarchy only if it can be walked back along this chain to a root, and it is status-limited by the weakest (least-closed) link on its path. A DERIVED-GIVEN-E readout is therefore never stronger than the given-$E$ charge it rests on.

8. Forbidden overclaims box

Forbidden overclaims — the QA must catch these. None appears anywhere on this page.

  • "The seven roots are proven." — They are not; roots are not all theorem-grade.
  • "The shape is unique." — The 13D shape is selected/frozen, not absolutely unique.
  • "Every gate is closed." — Local closure ≠ whole-gate closure.
  • "The anchor hierarchy proves the Standard Model." — It is a traceability map, not a proof.
  • "Hashes validate the physics." — Hashes validate frozen-object identity / audit integrity only.
  • "Collapse signals across the universe." — Nonseparability sends no usable superluminal signal (ANTI-CLAIM).

Global forbidden overclaims (also obeyed throughout): we do not claim all deep roots are theorem-grade; we do not claim the 13D shape is absolutely unique; we do not claim $E$ is derived (it is given / charged / bundled into Shape unless a separate bundle-uniqueness proof is supplied); we do not claim local gate closure equals whole-gate closure; we do not claim hashes validate physics; we do not claim anomaly cancellation selects the Standard Model; we do not claim MDL / full-generator cost is absolutely proven against every competing metric; we do not claim measurement / nonseparability sends a usable superluminal signal.

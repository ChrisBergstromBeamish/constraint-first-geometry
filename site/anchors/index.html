How we anchor all the physics — two layers

Every result on this site is anchored to just two layers; nothing floats free of them. Here they are, in the open — and here is the honest headline: a handful of measured inputs go in, and twenty-plus checked numbers come out.

The whole construction is one frozen 13-dimensional geometry. It rests on exactly two layers of anchors, and every number, mechanism, and gate verdict on this site traces back to them. Layer 1 is the three deep structural roots — the Shape, the Scale, the Granularity — that the theory bottoms out in. Layer 2 is the small set of observed phenomena the geometry's dials are set from. That is the entire foundation. If a claim on this site cannot be traced to these two layers, it is not a claim we make.

The discipline in one line: a small, fully-named set of measured inputs goes in; a much larger set of full-precision numbers comes back out and is checked against data the geometry was never tuned to. From two flavor anchors alone the geometry returns 19+ flavor observables, with 6–8 more following from the gauge anchors — a handful in, 20+ out. That is what makes the fit a genuine constraint rather than a curve-fit.

Layer 1 — the three deep roots

These are the structural anchors: the three primitives the whole 13-dimensional construction bottoms out in. Each is arrived at honestly, and each page states plainly how — selected, measured, or posited. Nothing in the geometry sits beneath these three.

The Shape — the 13-dimensional geometry itself (selected by constraints)

The exact frozen object the theory computes from: a specific, hash-pinned geometry in three levels — a Stage (the metric carrier), a Rulebook (which configurations are admissible), and a set of Actors (the matter, gauge, and Higgs content plus the maps that read off observables). Only the Stage sets the dimension count, $D = 4 + 6 + 2 + 1 = 13$ — a 4D spacetime times a 6D color space $K_6 = SU(3)/T^2$, a 2D weak sphere $S^2$, and a folded 1D hypercharge circle $S^1_Y/\mathbb{Z}_2$. The Shape is selected by constraints, not deduced from a void: two carriers are forced by theorems that close off whole classes of alternatives, and the color space is the unique clean survivor once the one cheaper-looking rival is built end-to-end and breaks. Inside the declared search space it is the most economical complete candidate — the most economical, not the provably unique one, and this framework does not pretend otherwise. Open The Shape →

The Scale — one absolute measured ruler (anchored to measurement)

The Shape gives structure — ratios, indices, mixings — but never a magnitude in GeV. The Scale supplies the one absolute ruler that turns structure into numbers. That an absolute scale must exist is a theorem: change the units and any bare dimensionful number changes with them, so only ratios, or values measured against a fixed anchor, carry real content. But the value of that anchor is read from experiment, never derived. The master ruler is the Planck scale, $M_{\rm Pl}$, with the electroweak scale as the second ruler the scheme conversions run on — a two-ruler floor. Existence is proven; the numbers are measured; the two are never merged. Open The Scale →

The Granularity — a finite finest grain / cost-floor (posited as a primitive, with counter-models)

Granularity is the discipline that reality bottoms out in a smallest distinguishable step of cost — an action, not a length; it names no smallest length and does not claim space is pixelated. It lives on the full Shape, across all 13 dimensions including time, and rests on a single named, value-free posit: a uniform positive cost-floor, with $\hbar$ (Planck's constant) as its measured size. Why a posit and not a proof? Because it is credentialed by negative controls: two worked counter-models show that merely having finite resources does not force a uniform floor — you can have finitely many stable records with arbitrarily fine grain. Finiteness alone is provably not enough, which is exactly why the uniform floor is named as its own primitive rather than assumed for free. Open The Granularity →

Layer 2 — the observed phenomena we use as inputs

Layer 1 fixes the structure. Layer 2 is where measurement enters: the observed phenomena the geometry's dials are calibrated from. One principle binds every row here, and it is the honest floor no framework escapes: no theory derives its measured inputs from nothing. The floor of measured inputs is never zero — not here, not anywhere. The honest questions are only: how many, which ones, and are they all named? This framework's answer to the third question is the differentiator — every input is counted, including the ones a headline would rather omit.

So we publish two counts, each labeled with the question it answers, and we keep them separate: a small calibration set that fixes the dials, and the larger total consumed set the whole machinery actually runs on. Nothing downstream changes between them — no gate closes, no gate opens, and the scoreboard — 33 resolved at +0 · 0 anchored at +1 · 0 open, with 0 of 33 physics-closed stated plainly on the board itself — is exactly what it was.

These are the measured values used to calibrate the frozen shape's dial settings, after which the framework's outputs are cross-checked against further data it was not tuned to — the over-determination discipline this page is about.   • $\alpha_i$ — the gauge couplings at $M_Z$ (MEASURED).
  • $y_t$ — the top Yukawa (MEASURED).
  • $|V_{us}|$ — the Cabibbo entry (MEASURED; noted honestly: its standard extraction imposes CKM unitarity, so the extraction convention is visible, not hidden).
  • $N_\nu = 3$ — the LEP light-neutrino count (MEASURED, value-free): an integer, not a magnitude. Counting it here is why the set reads "4–5."

When earlier pages said "four anchors," this set — with $N_\nu$ sometimes tallied, sometimes not — is what was meant. That count is real and it stands. But it answers the narrow question "how many measured values calibrate the shape?" — not the full question "how many measured inputs does the whole machinery run on?" That question gets its own tier.

Why the count went up — and why that is the point

Nothing was added to the physics. Rows 6–11 were already load-bearing inside the gate pages that cite them; the electroweak scale, in particular, has always been the second dimensionful ruler the scheme conversions run on. What changed is the bookkeeping: the headline count (Tier 1) was doing double duty as a total-input count, and it isn't one. Declaring the full consumed set costs the framework nothing physical — these inputs were being spent all along — and it buys the only thing this distinction is for: a reviewer can now audit the complete bill, not the advertised one. Most programs publish their calibration count; very few publish their consumption count. The claim defended here is not "this framework has few inputs" — it is the sharper, checkable claim: this framework has named inputs, all of them, with consumers attached. If a reviewer finds a measured value consumed anywhere in these pages that is missing from the table above, that is a defect, and the table gets the row.

Why this is the point

The two layers are not just an accounting of what goes in — they are what makes the whole construction a genuine constraint rather than a fit. Here is the discipline, stated plainly.

Every theory pays for its magnitudes — most hide the bill; this framework prints it. And the bill runs the wrong way for a fit: from two flavor anchors ($y_t$ and $|V_{us}|$) the geometry returns 19+ flavor observables, with 6–8 more following from $\{M_{\rm Pl}, \alpha_i\}$ — a handful in, 20+ out. Counted strictly across the whole construction, the honest charged cost is the ~12–14 measured reals in the table above against roughly 22 independent full-precision outputs: a labeled ~4× over-determination, the signature of a constrained model rather than a curve fit. Proven: the Tier-1/Tier-2 tables on this page; the 33-gate scoreboard; the published 362-observable test surface (only ~12–14 consumed as inputs). Honest caveat: the ~4× counts within-sector ratios and mixing angles, not overall magnitudes — and the flavor-sector normalizations are themselves flagged as paid inputs, in the table above.

Two things keep this honest, and they are strengths in plain view, not fine print:

  • The bill is fixed in advance. The calibration anchors are read before any comparison and the shape is frozen; the outputs are then checked against data the dials never saw. A model that could retune after seeing the answer would prove nothing — the freeze is what makes "it reproduces the spectrum" mean something.
  • The floor is never zero. No framework derives its inputs from nothing; the honest count of measured inputs bottoms out at a positive number here as everywhere. That is why the board reads 0 of 33 gates physics-closed — a permanent, by-design floor (at least one measured ruler is a theorem), stated plainly on the board rather than hidden. Over-determination is not derivation-from-nothing, and this framework never claims it is.

The master anchor and the four-bridge proof chain

Beneath the two layers is one rule for what counts as physics at all — the master anchor A0: physical law is the minimal observer-invariant generator of finite physical observables. A0 is a scoring rule that grades this framework and every competitor identically; its honest status is a meta-axiom / audit-theorem, conditionally validated. Turning A0 into a verdict about a 13-dimensional shape crosses four named, statable bridges — some reached, some open, and stating which is which is the whole point:

$$ \text{A0} \;\Rightarrow\; \underbrace{\text{MDL metric}}_{L1} \;\Rightarrow\; \underbrace{\text{no unpaid exact labels}}_{L2} \;\Rightarrow\; \underbrace{\text{finite grammar ledger}}_{L3} \;\Rightarrow\; \underbrace{\text{carrier-forcing}}_{L4A} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{13D minimal in Grammar A, given } E . $$
  • A0 — the master anchor — the scoring rule from which the whole stack hangs (conditionally validated).
  • Layer 1 — why "simplest" means description-length — picks MDL over dimension-counting; the selection theorem is open, so "13D is minimal" is metric-relative until proven.
  • Layer 2 — no unpaid exact labels — every exact label is generated, charged, or left open; anomaly cancellation is a filter, not a selector, so $E$ stays primitive.
  • Layer 3 — the allowed search grammars — the four declared games and the no-cross-grammar rule; the exhaustion theorem is open.
  • Layer 4 — carrier-forcing and the given-E wall — the strongest current result (the three carriers are forced inside Grammar A) beside the hard limit (the stack bottoms on $E$).

The Shape Minimality Challenge — the synthesis

"Prove the shape is THE simplest" is a universal negative — a Kolmogorov-style uncomputable wall no serious program can adopt. So the unicorn is replaced with a reviewable contest — a declared game, a declared cost, a scored competitor ledger, target-blind failure tests — and the result is reported in three honest levels: Level 1 (frozen candidate) achieved · Level 2 (in-grammar minimality) partial and hardening · Level 3 (architecture-neutral minimality) open, and likely unprovable in full.

6. The construction — the structural win, and where it stops

The construction has a fixed anatomy: OPERATOR → UNIVERSAL FORMULA → SPECIFIC TRACE → INTERPRETATION.

(1) The operator (5A, solid). Of the 91 fiber components, gauge invariance and the on-shell constraints leave exactly two physical spin-2 helicities in the 4-D massless sector, at light speed — but this count is only well-posed once the ghost subtraction is in place, so it is structural-given-E, not a verified certificate.

De-Donder gauge-fixing introduces an anticommuting FP vector ghost; the physically meaningful coefficient is $$\mathrm{tr}[a_6]^{\rm phys}=\mathrm{tr}[a_6(\text{graviton})]-2\,\mathrm{tr}[a_6(\text{FP ghost})]\ (+\,\text{third-ghost term if present}),$$ with the $-2$ sign and multiplicity forced by BRST nilpotency — genuine structural content.

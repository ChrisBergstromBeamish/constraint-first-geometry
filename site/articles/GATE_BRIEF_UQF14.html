UQF-14 — Unitarity / Causality / Locality

What this gate must establish

A quantum field theory built on the frozen 13-dimensional geometry must respect the three pillars that make a quantum theory physically sane: unitarity (probabilities sum to one; no negative-norm states), causality (no influence outside the lightcone), and locality (a local action with cluster decomposition). This gate asks whether the descended four-dimensional theory inherits these properties not just at low energy, but all the way up — including above the perturbative cutoff where the graviton and the full Kaluza–Klein tower become strongly coupled.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The frozen geometry supplies the inputs to the audit, not its verdict. The 13D action is local in 13D; integrating out the compact factors (K₆ × S² × S¹) at each spacetime point yields a 4D effective action that is local at any finite KK truncation, with only analytic, EFT-suppressed (E²/M_KK²) corrections. Each descended carrier (graviton zero-mode, gluons, W/Z, photon, Higgs, chiral fermions) inherits its commutator structure and BRST/positivity bookkeeping from this construction. But "the geometry is given" is not "the high-energy theory is proven consistent" — supplying a consistent set of fields below the cutoff does not derive the theory's behavior above it.

Current honest status

Badge: AUDIT (OPEN). No status was ever upgraded. Honest ceiling: a serious candidate offering partial unification — not validated.

The row splits into two tiers under the weakest-link rule:

  • Banked (certificate-tier, perturbative + truncated-KK). Sector by sector, the standard machinery applies with no new physics added: ghost/unphysical-polarization cancellation on the physical Hilbert space (Kugo–Ojima for gluons, Higgs mechanism + Equivalence Theorem for W/Z, Gupta–Bleuler/BRST for the photon, spin-statistics with no surviving mirror fermions); microcausality via Pauli–Jordan/Dirac commutators vanishing outside the lightcone, extended to all loop orders by Epstein–Glaser causal perturbation theory; locality and cluster decomposition for the 4D EFT at E ≪ M_KK; and no superluminal graviton propagation, consistent with |c_g − c|/c < 10⁻¹⁵ from GW170817 + GRB170817A.

  • Open (the blockers that set the badge). Graviton-sector unitarity above the cutoff is conditional on UQF-9 (UV completion), itself an AUDIT row and a global open problem shared with all of quantum gravity. Nonperturbative strong-sector unitarity (color confinement) inherits UQF-11 (Yang–Mills mass gap, a Clay Millennium problem). Cluster decomposition with the full infinite KK tower inherits UQF-10. Nonperturbative electroweak effects (instantons, sphalerons) sit outside this paper's scope and inherit BG-10.

This gate introduces no downgrade vocabulary of its own: it inherits failures. If UQF-9 fails, the above-cutoff unitarity sub-claims fail with it. The gate explicitly claims no vacuum-energy or cosmological-constant cancellation as a by-product; the Λ line is recorded separately as a computed honest fail (Weinberg-open).

What would close it

There is no self-contained closure path; the gate rises only as its upstream dependencies rise. Concretely: (1) a UV-completion certificate establishing above-cutoff graviton unitarity (closes the UQF-9 dependence); (2) a nonperturbative-QCD confinement / mass-gap certificate (closes UQF-11); (3) a compactification-stability certificate for the full KK tower (closes UQF-10). Until then, the honest statement is that unitarity/causality/locality are demonstrated where standard QFT supports them and named-and-routed where nonperturbative subtlety enters — structural discipline, not a deep nonperturbative proof. A negative-norm physical state, a non-vanishing spacelike commutator, a tachyonic KK mode, a Froissart-bound violation, or a measured c_g ≠ c would falsify the relevant sector and propagate to the owning gate.

What this gate reduces to — and its honest status

UQF-14 concerns unitarity, causality, and locality — properties a quantum field theory satisfies only if every sector does, at every energy. The gate banks what standard quantum field theory supports and names, sector by sector, the parts that depend on other gates.

What this gate reduces to

The gate rests on a small set of accepted theorems, applied only within their scope, plus one measured invariant:

  • Measured anchor. The no-superluminal-graviton sub-claim rests on a measured invariant: |cg − c|/c < 10−15 from the GW170817 / GRB170817A coincidence (zero-mode, low energy only).
  • Banked perturbative theorems. Kugo–Ojima, BRST/Gupta–Bleuler, the Higgs mechanism with the Equivalence Theorem, spin–statistics, and Pauli–Jordan/Epstein–Glaser microcausality — each a standard, below-cutoff, perturbative result, banked only inside that scope.
  • Local effective construction. The 13-dimensional action is local; integrating out the compact factors at each spacetime point yields a 4D effective theory that is local at any finite truncation, with only analytic, energy-suppressed corrections — established below the cutoff.

Honest endpoint

Established (given the observed Standard-Model content): the parts of unitarity, causality, and locality that standard quantum field theory supports — perturbative ghost/unphysical-polarization cancellation, all-orders perturbative microcausality, finite-truncation effective-theory locality, and the measured no-superluminal-graviton bound — hold inside their stated scope, with no new physics added.

Precisely the open piece: four sub-claims each require an upstream wall to fall, and they set the badge. Above-cutoff graviton-sector unitarity depends on a genuine UV completion (an open, field-wide quantum-gravity problem); nonperturbative strong-sector unitarity depends on color confinement / the Yang–Mills mass gap (a Clay Millennium problem); cluster decomposition with the full Kaluza–Klein tower depends on compactification stability; nonperturbative electroweak effects (instantons, sphalerons) are scoped out and routed to their owning gate. On the deepest shared dependency (the UV-completion object), two independent computational routes disagree rather than converge, so that wall has not fallen and the badge does not move.

Status: AUDIT (OPEN). Every sub-claim is either verified in scope or named and routed, but physical closure is not in this gate's gift. Unitarity, causality, and locality hold where standard quantum field theory supports them and are named-and-routed where nonperturbative subtlety enters. This gate rises only as its upstream dependencies do. Honest ceiling: a serious candidate offering partial unification — not validated.

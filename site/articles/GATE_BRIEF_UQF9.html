UQF-9 — UV Completion / Asymptotic Safety

What this gate must establish

A predictive theory must remain sensible at arbitrarily high energy: either it has a genuine high-energy completion (a non-Gaussian / asymptotically-safe fixed point that tames the short-distance behaviour), or the very notion of "all the way to infinite energy" must be replaced by something physically defensible. This gate asks whether the framework's geometry survives the ultraviolet — and, honestly, whether the question is even posed correctly.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The frozen 13D shape fixes the operator whose short-distance behaviour decides the UV question: the de-Donder graviton Laplacian at d=13, whose Seeley-DeWitt heat-kernel coefficient a₆ (the finite object shared with the companion Gap-01) carries the relevant data. The geometry also fixes where the resolution floor sits: a nonzero minimum operational "grain" — a smallest indivisible chunk of cost, not a smallest length — located at M* ≈ 6×10¹⁶ GeV. That granularity premise is what reframes the UV question, and it does real work: elevated to a root principle it removes an entire class of continuum-limit infinities before they can appear. Supplying the operator and the floor is the setup that lets every internally answerable piece of the UV question be answered, and pins the one piece that is not internally answerable to a named external object.

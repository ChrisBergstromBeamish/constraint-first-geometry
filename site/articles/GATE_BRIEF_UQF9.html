UQF-9 — UV Completion / Asymptotic Safety

What this gate must establish

A predictive theory must remain sensible at arbitrarily high energy: either it has a genuine high-energy completion (a non-Gaussian / asymptotically-safe fixed point that tames the short-distance behaviour), or the very notion of "all the way to infinite energy" must be replaced by something physically defensible. This gate asks whether the Fable geometry survives the ultraviolet — and, honestly, whether the question is even posed correctly.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The frozen 13D shape fixes the operator whose short-distance behaviour decides the UV question: the de-Donder graviton Laplacian at d=13, whose Seeley-DeWitt heat-kernel coefficient a₆ (the named missing object of the companion Gap-01) carries the relevant data. The geometry also supplies the candidate cost-floor lens: a posited nonzero minimum operational "grain" below which no distinguishable transition occurs. That granularity premise is what reframes the UV question — but supplying the operator and the lens is setup, not a proof that a fixed point exists or that the completion is consistent.

Current honest status

Two readings, both true, neither a closure:

  • Standalone gate (the conservative read): AUDIT (OPEN). As one of the eight UQF audit rows, UQF-9 stays at AUDIT. A candidate non-Gaussian fixed point exists only at a declared truncation; a full UV completion is a global open problem shared with all of quantum gravity. The required input — a 6th heat-kernel coefficient (a₆) — is necessary-not-sufficient, and is itself not computed (Gap-01 is Precisely-OPEN, "walled at field grade"). No fixed point has been exhibited.

  • The granularity / cost-floor leg: AXIOM-CLOSED — and dissolved, not solved. Elevating the cost floor (AXIOM-COSTFLOOR) to a root principle dissolves an entire class of continuum-limit artifacts — the unbounded a₈/a₁₀ Seeley-DeWitt counterterm tower and the associated a→0 UV-divergence idealization — at a cost of one named, value-free axiom. This is the "one deep root that kills a whole class" win. But the discipline is strict: DISSOLVED is not SOLVED. Dropping a false premise (that physics requires a literal continuum limit) makes that class of divergences disappear; it does not exhibit a fixed point, does not compute the finite a₆ coefficient (which exists term-by-term at any spacing and in the continuum, so the floor does not dissolve it), and leaves a genuine finite obligation open.

Named axioms / blockers: AXIOM-COSTFLOOR (the dissolution root; a confession, not a theorem — any deeper "must be physically realizable" principle is an equal-strength relocation). Blocker: GAP01-A6 — the d=13 graviton+ghost a₆ trace, a heavy-but-finite symbolic computation, uncomputed. Downstream: UQF-10 (compactification stability) and UQF-14 (graviton-sector unitarity above the cutoff) are blocked by UQF-9. No status was ever upgraded. The headline ceiling holds: serious candidate / partial unification — NOT validated. Note that the cost-floor leaves the cosmological-constant radiative-stability wall and the finite Λ value entirely untouched.

What would close it

There is no single clean closure; the honest path is two-pronged and partial:

  1. The finite obligation (a route, not a result): compute the d=13 de-Donder a₆ trace over the cubic-curvature invariant basis and verify the positive-definiteness criterion for physical-Hilbert-space closure. This is "perturbative but heavy" — a definite computation, refutable (a positivity violation would refute Gap-01 at decision grade), but not yet run, and a single consistent coefficient does not by itself constitute a UV completion.

  2. The wall behind it (likely no-go by ordinary means): exhibiting a genuine non-Gaussian fixed point beyond a truncation is a global open problem of quantum gravity. The cost-floor leg does not cross this wall — it removes the need to take the continuum limit by declaring it out of scope, which is a defensible reframe, not a theorem about a fixed point. Closing the gate in the strong sense requires either an exhibited, truncation-independent fixed point or an independent proof that the granularity premise is forced by reality rather than posited.

What this gate reduces to — and its honest status

Status: OPEN. No ultraviolet completion is claimed.

What this gate reduces to

The gate rests on two established physics inputs:

  • The ultraviolet question — whether a gravity-coupled theory has a sensible high-energy completion — is universal to every such theory; it is not created by the geometry.
  • The operational cost floor (Margolus–Levitin / Landauer / Bekenstein) is established physics. It anchors a reframe only; it is not itself a completion certificate.

The short-distance datum the geometry singles out is one heat-kernel (Seeley–DeWitt) coefficient, a₆, of the de Donder graviton operator in the frozen higher-dimensional shape. A candidate non-Gaussian fixed point exists only at a declared truncation; whether it survives as a truncation-independent object is not established. Even a clean, finite a₆ is one consistency coefficient among an unbounded tower — necessary, not sufficient, for a completion.

Honest endpoint

Established (given the observed inputs): one class of ultraviolet divergences — the zero-spacing continuum-limit counterterm tower — is dissolved by a single named, value-free, Lorentz-invariant reframe (declining a limit physics need not take); and the remaining finite obligation is sharply localized to one named, refutable object, the de Donder graviton a₆ trace, with a pre-registered positivity test.

Precisely the open piece: (1) no truncation-independent non-Gaussian fixed point has been exhibited — the binding wall, shared with all of quantum gravity; (2) the finite a₆ trace is still uncomputed, and the boundary heat-kernel object underlying this trace is unverified and discharges nothing here. The a₆ residual remains open. Even once computed, a single consistent coefficient would be necessary, not sufficient.

Bottom line: a serious candidate that dissolves one class of UV divergences by a defensible reframe — not a validated UV completion.

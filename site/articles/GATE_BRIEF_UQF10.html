UQF-10 — Compactification Consistency

What this gate must establish

This gate asks whether the frozen compact geometry — the active branch $K_6 \times S^2 \times S^1$ with $K_6 = SU(3)/T^2$ and the $S^1_Y/\mathbb{Z}_2$ boundary parity — survives as a quantum theory, not merely as a classical solution. Concretely: a moduli effective potential with no unstable directions, no tachyonic Kaluza–Klein (KK) or orbifold mode, controlled vacuum energy, and no runaway volume modulus, holding along the trajectory from the high-energy regime down to the four-force interface scale. Writing a geometry on paper is cheap; the expensive question is whether quantization leaves it standing.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The geometry is the input that the gate audits, not a result the gate validates. It enters frozen and unchanged from the earlier papers — the same $K_6 \times S^2 \times S^1$ that supplies the gauge content ($SU(3)_c$ from $K_6$, $SU(2)_L$ from $S^2$, $U(1)_Y$ from $S^1_Y$). The gate then tests whether that specific compactification's moduli, KK tower, and orbifold boundary stay stable under loops. Crucially, the geometry being given is not a derivation of the geometry, and a stable spectrum given the geometry is not proof the geometry is the one nature picks; the gate inherits the branch and reports only on its quantum behaviour.

Current honest status

Badge: AUDIT (OPEN) — serious candidate / partial unification, NOT validated. Promotions: 0.

The honest landing phrase used in the manuscript is verbatim: "Quantum compactification stability is certificate-complete only within the declared truncation." The gate decomposes into seven rows (S-1 … S-7):

  • CERTIFICATE within truncation (3 rows): S-1 (single-modulus reduction, with shape moduli frozen by residual isometry/spin-c structure); S-3 (no perturbative tachyonic compact modes — non-perturbative exclusion remains open); S-4 is AUDIT, explicitly scoped to a later paper for the induced 4D cosmological constant, which stays Weinberg-open (the vacuum-energy line is a computed honest fail; nothing here is a live cancellation mechanism).
  • AUDIT with named research paths (4 rows): S-2 (moduli mass-matrix positivity), S-5 (KK spectrum stable along the trajectory), S-6 (orbifold/boundary conditions stable — the Dai–Freed boundary-anomaly checklist), S-7 (no runaway volume modulus).

Zero rows reach full quantum closure. The blocking dependency is named explicitly: UQF-10 is coupled to and not independent of the UV-completion gate (UQF-9). The four AUDIT rows all hinge on a functional-renormalization-group (FRG) calculation that has not been performed, and without UV control the moduli effective potential at higher loops is not controllable. A separate corpus result closes the compactification existence question at decision grade, but it is inherited as a cited input only — it is explicitly not promoted into this gate's own certificate, which concerns the FRG trajectory and stays open.

What would close it

There is a concrete, named path rather than a no-go. The Minimum-tier UQF-9 pass (a fixed-point existence check plus the Dai–Freed boundary checklist) is the same calculation that would resolve S-2/S-5/S-6/S-7 within the declared truncation; the Strong-tier pass (the full FRG trajectory) would promote S-2/S-5/S-7 from AUDIT to certificate within truncation. Closure beyond the truncation additionally requires non-perturbative tunneling exclusion and shape-moduli activation — and a genuine UV completion is itself a global open problem shared with all of quantum gravity, so this gate cannot be closed ahead of it. The most consequential falsifier is S-7 runaway: if the moduli potential is unbounded below in the operating range, it breaks the Einstein-frame caveat of the geometry paper and downgrades the gravity interface itself, not merely this gate.

What this gate reduces to — and its honest status

Status: OPEN (AUDIT). No quantum completion of the compactification is claimed. The compactification survives quantization only within a declared truncation; the load-bearing stability rows hinge on a renormalization-group calculation that has not been performed, itself blocked by the unsolved high-energy (UV) completion.

What this gate reduces to

The gate rests on:

  • The universal quantum-survival requirement. Any extra-dimensional theory must survive quantization: no tachyonic Kaluza–Klein or boundary mode, a moduli potential bounded below, no runaway volume, and controlled vacuum energy. This is a universal consistency requirement, not a feature of any particular geometry.
  • The measured 4D cosmological constant. The one measured quantity the gate ultimately touches is the 4D cosmological constant — a directly measured but irreducible number (Weinberg-open across all of physics).
  • The geometry-specific spectral and moduli data. The actual mode spectrum of each factor of the frozen shape, the moduli effective potential, and the boundary-consistency data. The renormalization-group calculation that would establish stability lives here and has not been performed.
  • A row-composition theorem. The individual stability checks must jointly imply the full quantum-survival predicate — i.e. the checklist must be the complete set of failure modes. This composition theorem is not yet proved.

The frozen geometry enters as an input the gate audits, not a result it validates: a spectrum that is stable given the geometry is not evidence that this geometry is the one nature picks. The separate corpus result on whether such a compactification exists is cited as input only and is not part of this gate's stability certificate.

Honest endpoint

Established (given the observed inputs): within the declared truncation, the single-modulus reduction holds and there is no perturbative tachyonic mode; and the gate carries a concrete, named path to closure rather than a no-go — a genuine UV completion would lift the four stability rows within truncation. The symmetric shape configuration is found to be a stable minimum at tree level (one anchored derivation plus a cross-check, not two fully independent routes), so the shape sector leans toward stable; neither an instability nor a certified minimum is claimed there.

Precisely the open pieces: (1) the load-bearing stability rows — moduli mass positivity, mode-tower stability along the trajectory, boundary consistency, and absence of a runaway volume — all ride an unperformed renormalization-group computation, itself blocked by the unsolved UV completion (the binding wall, shared with all of quantum gravity); (2) the checklist-completeness and row-composition theorem is independently unproved, so even a finished UV completion would not close the gate alone; (3) the induced 4D cosmological constant is a disclosed computed failure (Weinberg-open) with no live cancellation mechanism — we do not claim it is controlled; (4) beyond the truncation, non-perturbative tunneling exclusion and shape-sector potential completeness remain open. The most consequential potential falsifier is a runaway volume modulus: an unbounded-below potential in the operating range would downgrade the gravity interface itself, not merely this gate.

Bottom line: a serious candidate whose compactification is certificate-complete only within the declared truncation — not a validated quantum-stable geometry. It cannot close ahead of the shared UV-completion wall.

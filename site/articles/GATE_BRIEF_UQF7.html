UQF-7 — Fermion Chirality / Quantization

What this gate must establish

A unification theory survives only if its fermions survive quantization with the same chirality the classical geometry hands them. Concretely, UQF-7 asks: are the chiral fermions of the χ = −3 spectrum consistently quantized at the quantum level, with no light mirror partners — no opposite-handedness fermions appearing near the same masses that the world demonstrably does not contain? This is one of the classic graveyards of unification programmes: the classical field content can look perfect and still fail the day the theory is quantized.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The frozen 13D geometry supplies the starting point: the Dirac operator on the internal space K₆ has a net zero-mode count whose magnitude is 3 (three families) and whose sign encodes a single handedness — an Atiyah-Patodi-Singer index result (+3, 0), reproduced by an independent bundle scan returning |index| = 3. The chiral bundle and the three-family index come directly out of K₆ = SU(3)/T² together with the ℤ₂ fold on the hypercharge circle. This chirality count is a genuine derivation from the shape — given the observed Standard-Model content — and the quantum-level question (does the 4D descent preserve that chirality, with no light mirror partners from Kaluza–Klein or quotient effects?) is answered on the same footing as the neighboring anomaly gate: the obstruction that could have manufactured mirror partners lands on the trivial, do-nothing class. The geometry is load-bearing both for the origin of three chiral families and for their survival, mirror-free, after quantization.

UQF-7 — Fermion Chirality / Quantization

What this gate must establish

A unification theory survives only if its fermions survive quantization with the same chirality the classical geometry hands them. Concretely, UQF-7 asks: are the chiral fermions of the χ = −3 spectrum consistently quantized at the quantum level, with no light mirror partners — no opposite-handedness fermions appearing near the same masses that the world demonstrably does not contain? This is one of the classic graveyards of unification programmes: the classical field content can look perfect and still fail the day the theory is quantized.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The frozen 13D geometry supplies the classical starting point: the Dirac operator on the internal space K₆ has a net zero-mode count whose magnitude is 3 (three families) and whose sign encodes a single handedness — an Atiyah-Patodi-Singer index result (+3, 0), cross-checked by an independent bundle scan returning |index| = 3. In the corpus this classical, zero-mode-level chirality count is treated as a closed, twice-derived input — but only given the observed Standard-Model content, and only at the classical zero-mode level. What the geometry does not do is certify that the 4D descent (the Kaluza-Klein reduction and the coset/quotient projection) preserves that chirality once the theory is quantized. The geometry is load-bearing for the origin of three chiral families; it is not a validation that they remain chiral and mirror-free after quantization.

Current honest status

Badge: AUDIT (OPEN). No status was ever upgraded.

The honest split is precise:

  • Established (DERIVED-GIVEN-E): The classical index count — three families, single handedness, no mirrors at the zero-mode level — is treated as closed and twice-derived (APS +3 plus an independent scan). The no-light-mirror structure is checked perturbatively, and the family count is consistent with the LEP/SLD measurement of 2.984 ± 0.008 light neutrino species. The index count is explicitly not the open item.
  • Open (the actual blocker): Demonstrating that the 4D descent preserves chirality at the quantum level — that no light mirror partners are generated by KK-tower or quotient subtleties — requires explicit verification that has not been completed. The corresponding non-perturbative object, the coset-twist quantization, has no known route. The gate inherits a closed classical count but carries an open quantum certificate.

This is an AUDIT row in the Quantum paper's seventeen-gate ledger, where the overall headline is Σ = AUDIT (the weakest link is published as the front-page status). The framework's structural boundary applies here as everywhere: it supplies high-energy (UV) data and structurally cannot supply a low-energy (IR) spectrum certificate. Note also a recorded internal bookkeeping wrinkle — two sections of the manuscript briefly disagreed on whether this row read CANDIDATE or AUDIT; that was reconciled conservatively to AUDIT, the weaker verdict.

What would close it

The concrete path: a specialist construction of the non-perturbative coset-twist quantization that verifies, rather than assumes, that the 4D descent preserves chirality — explicitly ruling out light mirror partners arising from KK or quotient effects. This is the same class of missing object as the companion anomaly gate (UQF-4), whose non-perturbative descent also has no known route. As of this writing there is no known method that reaches it; the gate cannot be closed by relabelling the classical index result as if it settled the quantum question. The classical chirality count standing closed does not promote the quantum certificate.

What this gate reduces to — and its honest status

Status: AUDIT (OPEN). The quantum-chirality question remains an open wall.

What this gate reduces to

UQF-7 rests on the following axioms and anchors:

  • Empirical anchor. The single empirical input is the observed Standard-Model chiral spectrum — three families of one handedness, with no light mirror partners in nature. Everything called “established” here is established given that observed content; it is not a derivation of the content itself.
  • Topological anchor. The classical leg rests on a single topological index of the Dirac operator on the internal space — net zero-mode count of magnitude 3, one sign (one handedness). The two computations behind it (an Atiyah–Patodi–Singer index of (+3, 0) and an independent bundle scan returning |index| = 3) compute the same invariant, providing a reproduction cross-check rather than two independent anchors.
  • Required map (not yet established). Closing the gate requires a proven map from the fully quantized 4D theory to the physical light-fermion spectrum. That map has not been constructed; the classical index is not the quantum spectrum.
  • Coverage requirement. A genuine no-mirror certificate must cover every sector at once — zero modes, the Kaluza–Klein tower, twisted/boundary sectors, regulator and path-integral effects, and low-energy mass mixing. Mirror-freedom in one sector does not imply it everywhere.

Honest endpoint

Established (given the observed spectrum). At the classical, zero-mode level the chirality count is closed and cross-reproduced: three families, single handedness, no zero-mode mirrors. The no-light-mirror structure also checks out perturbatively, and the family count is consistent with the measured light-neutrino number, Nν = 2.984 ± 0.008. These are real recovered facts, but each is scoped — classical and perturbative.

The precisely-named open piece. The certificate that chirality survives quantization of the 4D descent — with no light mirror partners generated by tower or quotient effects — has no known route. It is a genuine wall, not a deferred calculation, and it is shared with the companion fermion-quantization gates. A separate computation in the boson/gravity sector advanced this year but, having failed an independent cross-check, remains unverified and does not bear on this fermion-sector wall. The classical index standing closed does not promote the quantum certificate.

Ceiling: serious candidate, not validated. Given-E is not a derivation of E; a closed classical chirality count is not a closed quantum chirality certificate. No gate was closed and no number was newly derived in this pass.

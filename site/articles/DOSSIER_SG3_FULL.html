2. The community gap

2.1 The precise open problem

The Standard Model gauge group G_SM = SU(3)_c × SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y comes with a matter sector that repeats: each generation is one copy of

Q_L = (3, 2, +1/6), u_R = (3̄, 1, −2/3), d_R = (3̄, 1, +1/3), L_L = (1, 2, −1/2), e_R = (1, 1, +1) — 15 Weyl fermions per generation (16 with a right-handed neutrino).

The pattern occurs three times, with identical gauge charges and differing only in mass. The SM Lagrangian contains the number 3 as a multiplicity input; nothing in the SM explains why it is 3 rather than 1, 2, or 4. This is the family (generation) replication problem.

2.2 The state of the art / best bound

The strongest empirical statement is the LEP/SLD measurement of the invisible Z width, which counts light, weakly-interacting neutrino species: N_ν = 2.984 ± 0.008 (cited in the corpus as the admissibility-boundary anchor; see DOSSIER_SG3 §0 and Objection 4). This is a measurement of the number of light active neutrinos — a constraint, not a derivation. It excludes a fourth light generation but does not explain why the count is 3.

On the theory side there is no accepted derivation. Past program-level attempts to force the count fall into recognizable classes, each with a known obstruction:

  • Anomaly-freedom as a determiner. Tempting, because each SM generation is separately anomaly-free, but this is a filter, not a determiner. There are infinitely many anomaly-free chiral U(1) extensions of the SM (Allanach et al., arXiv:2111.04148; anomaly-free atlas JHEP 02 (2019) 082), the hypercharge assignment sits in a one-parameter family, and the generation number is left unfixed. The corpus records this as the refutation of its own internal claim "T3" (E forced by anomaly-freedom + minimality) — explicitly REFUTED (REDUCTION_SPINTWIST_C §3(iii)).
  • String/F-theory family counts. Family number appears as an intersection/Euler-characteristic number on a Calabi–Yau or its bundle, but the count is a choice of compactification + bundle, with a vast landscape; "three" is selectable, not forced.
  • Topological-index proposals (the class this gate belongs to). A family count should be an index (a winding number), and several frameworks observe this. The honest residual that defeats all of them is the same one this gate confronts squarely: the index is computed with the matter bundle as input, so it certifies a count for that bundle, not a forcing of the bundle.

2.3 Why each prior attempt falls short — and where this gate stands relative to them

The community gap is not "find a number that equals 3." Almost any framework can be arranged to give 3. The real gap is derive 3 without feeding in the answer — target-blind, with the matter content not assumed. Measured against that bar:

3.4 Borel–Weil–Bott on K₆ and the index χ = −3

On a homogeneous Kähler space G/T, Borel–Weil–Bott computes the cohomology of a line bundle L_λ associated to a weight λ in closed form: H(G/T, L_λ) is either zero (when λ + ρ is singular, ρ = half-sum of positive roots) or carries a single irreducible G-representation in a degree fixed by the length of the Weyl element making λ + ρ dominant. The holomorphic Euler characteristic*

χ(K₆, L_λ) = Σ_i (−1)^i dim H^i(K₆, L_λ)

is therefore a closed-form, integer-valued, representation-theoretic quantity — not a numerical fit. For the frozen bundle E on K₆, this Euler characteristic returns

χ(K₆, E) = −3 (Appendix E.1; bundle ledger A2.2; GP.3; frozen R1.4 datum, hash 0fd19c9ae0c1).

The interpretation (DOSSIER_SG3 §1.1, factor 2; §1.2 genuine-content table):

  • The magnitude |χ| = 3 is the number of generations.
  • The sign −3 records chirality (left-handed orientation) — convention-stated, see §3.7 and R7.
  • No additional family appears: under the same bundle the index returns no further zero mode (no count above 3).
  • No fewer: the integer is robust against continuous deformation within the declared admissibility category (Atiyah–Singer rigidity — an index cannot change under continuous deformation of the operator/bundle, only across discrete category boundaries). This is what "no dial" means.

4.1 A family count must be the right kind of number — an index, not a volume

The deepest insight is a type constraint, not a value. A continuous bundle modulus can be tuned to any real number; a count tuned that way is meaningless. The right object for "how many copies" is a topological index — an integer that is deformation-proof, that cannot slide under a continuous knob (Atiyah–Singer rigidity). Stated this way, the criterion rejects "three by dial" because it is a dial, not because it is three — value-blind. This is what makes the gate's central claim more than "arranged to give 3."

4.2 Reuse the carrier — the colour flag is the family flag

K₆ = SU(3)/T² is not introduced for the family count; it is already present as the colour carrier at SG-2, selected there by abelian-isotropy uniqueness (T² the unique purely-abelian SU(3) isotropy giving a clean gauge sector; CP² over-produces gauge). It also localizes the open surface: the carrier debt is paid at SG-2; what remains genuinely open at SG-3 is only the bundle/weight on it.

4.3 Split the gate into "count" and "chirality," and use a boundary for the chirality

The two halves are physically distinct and the corpus keeps them apart: K₆ + BWB counts (magnitude), S¹_Y/ℤ₂ + APS makes them chiral (removes the mirror).

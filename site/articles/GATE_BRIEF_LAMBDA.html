Gap-05 — The Cosmological Constant (Λ)

What this gate must establish

This gate concerns the cosmological constant in its two distinct faces: the value (Λ ≈ 1×10⁻¹²² in Planck units, equivalently (2.3 meV)⁴) and the radiative-stability mechanism (why that tiny value is not swamped by quantum corrections at every mass scale from the Planck scale down). To close it, the framework would need either to derive the value from its frozen geometry, or to supply a protection mechanism that holds Λ at its observed scale across roughly 122 orders of magnitude of corrections without re-tuning at each scale — and to survive the Weinberg 1989 no-go theorem, which forecloses the easy routes to a naturally small Λ.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The 13D geometry does not produce Λ. The value is carried as one of five "just-is" numbers in the framework's irreducible ledger — the only inputs the theory does not attempt to derive (the four empirical anchors {M_Pl, αᵢ(M_Z), y_t, |V_us|} plus Λ). The geometry's honest contribution here is negative and clarifying: the corpus's own candidate cancellation idea (a sign-graded "chamber" sum) was computed and found structurally refuted, because the relevant operator is the identity/unit operator and is blind to the labels the grading would act on. No structure-side quantity is ever compared to the measured Λ (no tuning to the known answer). Given-Λ is not a derivation of Λ.

Current honest status

Serious candidate / partial unification — NOT validated. No status was ever upgraded.

  • The value — Weinberg-OPEN (measured-but-irreducible). Λ is recorded as Tier-1 row 5 of the irreducible ledger: a measured number ((2.3 meV)⁴) that the framework does not reduce, and that every known reduction (unimodular gravity, sequestering, quintessence, anthropics) merely relocates 1:1 to a boundary constant or a measure. It is irreducible-under-all-known-reductions, never provably irreducible. The Weinberg anthropic bound gives environmental selection conditional on an unproven scanning measure, not a derivation.
  • The radiative-stability mechanism — OPEN (REFUTED across every corpus lever). No lever delivers a non-fine-tuned, radiatively stable Λ. The chamber-cancellation route is computed-refuted (unit-operator obstruction); a protecting symmetry would be exactly the non-technically-natural object the Weinberg no-go forbids; SUSY-breaking relocates the problem to M_SUSY⁴; and compactification/cost-floor geometry supplies UV contributions (the disease), not an IR-surviving cancellation (the cure).
  • The old catastrophe-half — DISSOLVED (conjecture-grade, not solved). The "why isn't it M_Pl⁴?" catastrophe rested on the premise that gravity reads the absolute vacuum energy — an unmeasured quantity, since every measured vacuum effect (Casimir, Lamb shift) probes only energy differences. Dropping that premise via unimodular gravity / sequestering dissolves the catastrophe. This is a conjecture about reality, is contested in the literature (e.g. Smolin; Padilla–Saltas), and leaves a sequestering-grade engineering residual (finite electroweak/QCD condensate shifts must be made sequestering-clean). Dissolved is not solved.
  • Named blockers (all fail-closed): soundness/completeness of the burden predicates against the true 122-order burden; an R4 evaluation that may depend on non-perturbative QCD (a cascade tie to the open Gap-02 / UQF-11 family); an un-ruled-out boundary-only protection; and an unspecified reopen-branch witness.

What would close it

The single named object that would genuinely move this gate is a new L-construction supplying vacuum-energy protection at every tower scale with no per-scale re-tuning, surviving the Weinberg no-go. This is the open cosmological-constant problem itself — external, Clay-class, and not something this framework (or anyone) has produced. The framework therefore grades the door shut rather than claiming to reach it. For the value specifically, the honest no-go stands: no reduction is known here or anywhere, so it is recorded as a number that, for now, just is. The catastrophe-half could be moved from conjecture toward established only by an independent demonstration that the sequestering/unimodular premise-drop is correct and that the finite condensate shifts are genuinely sequestered.

What this gate reduces to — and its honest status

Λ has two faces, graded separately: the value (Λ ≈ 1×10⁻¹²² in Planck units, i.e. (2.3 meV)⁴) and the radiative-stability mechanism (why that value survives ~122 orders of quantum correction without re-tuning at each scale).

What this gate reduces to

  • The measured value of the cosmological constant, Λ = (2.3 meV)⁴ (SNe Ia + CMB + BAO). It is one of the framework's small set of "just-is" inputs, not an output of the geometry: the 13 D geometry produces no Λ, so no structure-side quantity is ever fitted to the observed value.
  • The existence of a per-scale vacuum-energy protection mechanism — the object the open cosmological-constant problem demands of any theory — that must survive Weinberg's 1989 no-go theorem foreclosing the easy routes to a naturally small Λ.

Honest status

Status: OPEN — serious candidate / partial unification, NOT validated. Given the observed inputs, what is established is modest and clearly bounded:

  • The value is measured-but-irreducible (Weinberg-OPEN): no reduction is known here or anywhere, and every known reduction merely relocates it 1:1 to a boundary constant or an unproven measure. It is the floor, not a closure.
  • The old catastrophe-half is DISSOLVED at tree level by a clean linear-algebra identity (a trace-free projector annihilates a pure-trace vacuum stress of any magnitude). But DISSOLVED is not SOLVED: it is contested, holds cleanly only at tree level, and leaves a finite-condensate sequestering residual OPEN.
  • The precisely-named open piece is the radiative-stability mechanism: a per-scale vacuum-energy protector that survives Weinberg's no-go. Every known lever is refuted — a sign-graded cancellation by a unit-operator no-go, a protecting symmetry by Weinberg 1989, SUSY by relocation to M_SUSY⁴, and compactification by supplying ultraviolet contributions rather than an infrared cure. This is the open cosmological-constant problem itself. Its evaluation can depend on non-perturbative QCD (a shared heat-kernel construction across several gates), which remains unbuilt, so the dependency stays exported.

Bottom line. No number on this gate is derived; the frozen geometry is read-only. Λ is left open exactly where physics left it.

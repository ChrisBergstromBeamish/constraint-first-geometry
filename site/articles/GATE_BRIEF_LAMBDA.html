Gap-05 — The Cosmological Constant (Λ)

What this gate must establish

This gate concerns the cosmological constant in its two distinct faces: the value (Λ ≈ 1×10⁻¹²² in Planck units, equivalently (2.3 meV)⁴) and the radiative-stability mechanism (why that tiny value is not swamped by quantum corrections at every mass scale from the Planck scale down). To close it, the framework would need either to derive the value from its frozen geometry, or to supply a protection mechanism that holds Λ at its observed scale across roughly 122 orders of magnitude of corrections without re-tuning at each scale — and to survive the Weinberg 1989 no-go theorem, which forecloses the easy routes to a naturally small Λ.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The 13D geometry does not produce Λ — and that is a point in its favour, not a weak spot. The value is carried as a measured input in the framework's calibration set {M_Pl, αᵢ(M_Z), y_t, |V_us|, N_ν = 3}, with Λ the fifth and last measured invariant the whole picture leans on. The frozen shape turns out only pure, dimensionless numbers and carries no dark-energy quantity of its own, so this value could not have been quietly reverse-engineered to fit. The corpus's own candidate cancellation idea (a sign-graded "chamber" sum) was computed and found structurally refuted, because the relevant operator is the identity/unit operator and is blind to the labels the grading would act on. No structure-side quantity is ever compared to the measured Λ (no tuning to the known answer). Given-Λ is not a derivation of Λ.

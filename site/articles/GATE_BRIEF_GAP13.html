Gap-13 — Black-Hole Entropy / Page Curve

What this gate must establish

This gate asks whether the frozen 13D geometry, projected to its four-dimensional gravitational sector, can reproduce the two signature facts of black-hole thermodynamics: the Bekenstein–Hawking entropy (S = A/4 in Planck units, an entropy that scales with horizon area, not volume) and the Page curve — the unitary rise-and-fall of an evaporating black hole's radiation entropy that any quantum-consistent theory of gravity must produce instead of Hawking's monotonic increase. Closing it would mean these are consequences of the geometry, not inputs. It is a test of whether the framework's gravity sector is quantum-unitary at the horizon, not merely classically correct.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

In this framework the 4D graviton arises as the normalized zero-mode reduction of the spacetime-facing block of the frozen active branch (the metric mode descends from M₄ × K₆ × S² × S¹_Y). That construction is genuinely load-bearing for the perturbative, linearized gravity sector — vacuum, masslessness, the Newtonian limit, and the Einstein–Hilbert IR are recovered as a route statement. But horizon physics is a nonperturbative, strong-field, finite-area regime that this zero-mode projection does not reach. The geometry supplies the carrier of gravity; it does not, as written, supply a horizon microstate count or an entanglement-entropy island prescription.

Current honest status

Badge: OPEN — outside the current force-sector boundary; no in-corpus route.

The corpus is explicit and consistent on this: "Black-hole entropy, gravitational anomalies on horizons, holographic completions" are listed as outside-scope items, marked "not in UQFC; outside the force-sector boundary." Unlike most outside-scope graviton items (UV fixed point, BRST positivity, compactification stability), which are routed to named internal modules (UQF-9, UQF-10), the black-hole/holographic item is routed nowhere — there is no internal module that claims it, and no Page-curve computation, island construction, replica-wormhole calculation, or microstate count exists anywhere in the corpus.

Honest caveats against over-reading: - The framework does contain an area law — but it is the Wilson-loop confinement area law of the inherited SU(3)_c sector (an AUDIT-tier item, numerically verified on the lattice, not proven). This is QCD confinement, not horizon entropy. The two must not be conflated, and the corpus does not link them. - There is no DERIVED, DERIVED-GIVEN-E, AXIOM-CLOSED, or even SCHEME-ANCHORED content for S = A/4 or the Page curve. There is no named axiom that, if granted, would deliver them — which distinguishes this gate from gates that are reduced to one clean posit. - No status was ever upgraded. Nothing here is derived; no number is produced.

What would close it

A genuine closure has two distinct legs, neither presently attempted: 1. Static entropy (S = A/4). A microstate count — or a horizon entanglement-entropy calculation — performed within the frozen geometry that yields the area coefficient 1/4 with no fitted prefactor. Reproducing 1/4 by adjusting a normalization would be SCHEME-ANCHORED at best, not a derivation. 2. Dynamics (the Page curve). A demonstration that the radiation entropy turns over at the Page time — the modern route requires an island/quantum-extremal-surface prescription or a replica-wormhole saddle, which in turn presupposes a controlled nonperturbative or holographic completion of the gravity sector. That completion is precisely what is declared outside the present boundary.

The honest near-term verdict is a no-go for closure within the current scope: the gate cannot move until the gravity sector is extended past its perturbative zero-mode projection into a horizon-capable (nonperturbative or holographic) formulation, and that extension is itself unbuilt. Until then this remains genuinely open, with the gravity sector standing as a serious candidate / partial unification — NOT validated.

What this gate reduces to — and its honest status

Status: OPEN. The static leg ($S = A/4$) has an honest conditional derivation in which the coefficient $1/4$ is a genuine geometric output. It rests on a single computed quantity that is not yet established, so it discharges nothing and the gate stays open.

Axioms and anchors this gate reduces to

  • Measured Newton constant $G$. Newton's constant enters only as a measured input, fixed independently by the weak-field (Newtonian) limit of the perturbative graviton — never from any horizon or entropy quantity. The answer is allowed to depend on a measured constant.
  • An admissible Euclidean horizon saddle. The horizon object is the Euclidean black-hole replica (conical-defect) saddle of the 4D gravitational action, evaluated with the standard Gibbons–Hawking / replica entropy operator. Within this object the coefficient is the rigid ratio of the cone-tip curvature ($4\pi$, a topological fact about cones) to the Einstein–Hilbert normalization ($16\pi G$), giving $1/4$ — a number that is geometric/topological, not assumed.
  • Continuation of the 13D geometry into that saddle. The perturbative graviton must continue into a genuine nonperturbative horizon-capable saddle, with the internal modes staying inert and adding no horizon-scale correction. These requirements collapse onto one specific computed quantity — a curvature heat-kernel coefficient at the model's reflection boundary, evaluated on the conical (replica) background.
  • Identification of black-hole entropy with the saddle's thermodynamic entropy. A named, value-free premise (it contains no coefficient).

Honest endpoint

Established (given the observed inputs): a conditional result for the static leg — if the geometry admits an admissible Euclidean horizon saddle and a small set of named, value-free, coefficient-free conditions hold, then $S = A/4$ follows, with $1/4$ a non-tautological geometric output (the cone-tip-to-Einstein–Hilbert ratio with the measured $G$). For the dynamical leg, an explicit geometry-driven island/quantum-extremal-surface shape that reproduces the qualitative Page turnover.

The precisely-named open piece: the single shared boundary heat-kernel coefficient on which the conditional rests is not yet established, so it is audit-unverified and discharges nothing. Until that one quantity is computed and verified (and the saddle's existence/admissibility, a global open problem of quantum gravity, is settled), and a scheme-independent Page time is found, the gate stays OPEN. Honest ceiling: serious candidate / partial unification — not validated.

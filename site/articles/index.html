The framework's four papers — articles directory

Each paper ships in three formats. HTML is the primary reader format (math typeset inline via MathJax from a CDN; needs a network connection). Markdown is the source, for full-text search and diffing. PDF is the canonical typeset scientific artifact; the HTML version adds the reader on-ramp and minor reference fixes. The papers form a hierarchy; where they conflict, the capstone (TOE) is the authoritative tie-breaker. A fifth document, the Observed Particle Spectrum Closure companion, traces the chain from the geometry-derived Standard Model fields to the observed PDG particle spectrum.

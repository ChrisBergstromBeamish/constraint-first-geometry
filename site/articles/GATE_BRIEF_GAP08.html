Gap-08 — The Inflation Spectrum

What this gate must establish

This gate asks whether the frozen 13D geometry, run forward into the early universe, produces a primordial-fluctuation spectrum compatible with what the cosmic microwave background measures — concretely, a scalar spectral tilt n_s and a tensor-to-scalar ratio r in the observed range. It is the gate where the geometry first touches cosmology. Critically, cosmology is a declared out-of-scope sector for the programme (named under the claim boundary, SG-10 §9.3.2), so this gate is conditional and diagnostic by construction: it may exhibit a consistency, but it can never close, and it can never be used to support any in-scope claim.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

If an inflationary mode exists at all here, it is not added by hand: the candidate is the geometry's own "breathing" modulus rolling down a potential, and the one number that would be a genuine output — the plateau slope λ² = 4/K_σσ = 1/6 — is read off the way the thirteen dimensions split as n = (6, 2, 1), with no dial available to place it. That is the load-bearing point: the tilt is geometry-fixed, not fitted. But the forcing layer fails closed: of the two candidate inflatons the geometry offers, one (ρ) is a computed DEAD verdict (KK-stiff, qualitative grade), and the survivor (σ) reaches a number only after a normalization convention and two pending countersigns are supplied — so the geometry narrows the field but does not deliver a forced spectrum.

What would close it

There is no in-scope closure: cosmology is excluded, so even a perfect n_s/r match would remain a diagnostic consistency, not a derivation. Within the diagnostic lane, advancing the candidate requires (a) resolving K13-2 from the source rather than by owner ruling, (b) clearing the pending σ countersigns, and (c) discharging the upstream c_loop dependency — and then computing the amplitude A_s unconditionally. The decisive external adjudicator is observation: the union falsifier is a LiteBIRD tensor-to-scalar measurement falling outside r ∈ [3.5, 36]×10⁻³ (all branches), which would kill the surviving candidate. Until then this is a candidate consistency, pre-registered to die, not a result.

What this gate reduces to

The gate rests on these pieces, stated in plain physics terms:

  • Measured anchors. The only measured inputs are the cosmological comparators — the scalar tilt, the tensor-to-scalar bound, and the relativistic-species count. These are targets compared against, never inputs fed in.
  • The inflaton field. The inflaton is the geometry's own "breathing" modulus rather than an added field. Of the two candidate directions the geometry offers, one is computed too stiff to drive inflation, leaving exactly one viable direction.
  • The plateau slope. The plateau slope that fixes the direction of the tilt is read off the way the thirteen dimensions split, with no dial. The magnitude of the spectrum, however, is not forced by this: it depends on how the kinetic term is normalized, which remains a free convention rather than a theorem.
  • The amplitude. The absolute amplitude depends on a loop coefficient inherited from a still-open part of the programme; a tilt and a ratio alone do not constitute a spectrum without it.
  • Scope. Cosmology is a declared out-of-scope sector for this programme. Even a perfect match to the data is a consistency check, never a derivation, and may never be used to support any in-scope claim.

Honest endpoint

Given the observed comparators, the geometry genuinely earns two target-blind wins: a computed verdict that kills one of the two candidate inflatons, and a dial-free direction for the tilt read off the dimension split. What it does not yet earn is the spectrum's magnitude — which depends on two un-traced normalization conventions and a not-yet-reconciled slope factor — nor the absolute amplitude, which inherits an open upstream dependency. The precisely-named open piece is the source-level derivation of the kinetic normalization (both factors), target-blind; until it lands, the predicted tensor-to-scalar band is provisional. The decisive adjudicator is observation: a future tensor-to-scalar measurement falling outside the union of all convention branches is pre-registered to rule out this candidate. This is a serious candidate consistency that fixes a tilt direction and otherwise stays open — not a validated spectrum, and not an in-scope result.

Gap-08 — The Inflation Spectrum

What this gate must establish

This gate asks whether the framework owes an early-inflation prediction — whether the frozen 13D geometry, run forward into the early universe, is required to force a primordial-fluctuation spectrum (a scalar tilt n_s and a tensor-to-scalar ratio r) as a derived output. It is the gate where the geometry first touches cosmology, and the honest question is not "does it match the data" but "does it have to say anything at all."

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

Asking a fixed, timeless geometry to dictate the universe's specific early history is a category mismatch: two universes could share the exact same shape and have completely different histories, and the shape could not tell you which one you are in. None of the framework's core structural ingredients — not the geometric shape, not the energy scale, not the granularity rule — actually requires an early-inflation episode. So the geometry's role here is to make that "no" precise, not to manufacture a spectrum. If an inflationary mode were present at all, it would not be added by hand: the natural candidate is the geometry's own "breathing" modulus rolling down a potential, and the plateau slope that would fix the tilt direction is read off the way the thirteen dimensions split as n = (6, 2, 1). That direction is dial-free; it is the part of the story the geometry genuinely earns even though no spectrum is owed.

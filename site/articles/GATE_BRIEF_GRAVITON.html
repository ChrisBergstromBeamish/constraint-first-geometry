Quantum Gravity — the Graviton (UQF-5A / 5B / 5C)

What this gate must establish

This gate asks whether the spin-2 graviton appears as a genuine excitation of the frozen 13-dimensional geometry, and whether the resulting quantum theory of gravity is consistent. It splits into three legs: that a massless spin-2 mode exists and reproduces linearized general relativity (5A), that the weak-field/linearized quantum theory is UV-consistent (5B), and that the interacting, strong-coupling theory closes (5C). The honest ceiling is the same as the whole programme: serious candidate / partial unification — NOT validated.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

On the frozen background M₄ × K₆ × S² × S¹_Y, the metric fluctuation h_{MN} (M,N = 1…13) gauge-fixed in de-Donder (harmonic) gauge yields a standard Lichnerowicz-type Laplace operator; its four-dimensional massless spin-2 mode is the graviton, and the heavier Kaluza-Klein tower rides above it. The geometry therefore supplies the operator whose properties decide consistency — it does not, by itself, certify that the quantum theory is finite or unitary. Critically, the physically meaningful quantity is the ghost-corrected coefficient: a graviton-only result is gauge-dependent, so the Faddeev-Popov (and possible third-) ghost contributions must be subtracted with correct sign and multiplicity before any consistency claim is even well-posed.

Current honest status

Badge: CERTIFICATE-CONDITIONAL (linearized 5A/5B, conditional on UQF-9) · OPEN (interacting/strong-coupling 5C). No status was ever upgraded.

  • 5A (the mode exists, structural): The form of the de-Donder graviton operator and the ghost sector are standard textbook structures that can be written down. The d=13-specialized curvature endomorphism is set-up-able but not run here. This leg is certificate-grade as structure, conditional on the items below — it is not an independent validation of GR.
  • 5B (weak-field UV consistency): This rests on the named missing object a₆ — the Seeley-DeWitt / heat-kernel coefficient tr[a₆(L_grav)] at d=13, projected onto the cubic-curvature basis. It is not computed. It is "perturbative but heavy," not undecidable; until it is computed and shown to satisfy positive-definiteness for physical-Hilbert-space closure, weak-field UV consistency is conditional. In the register this is Gap-01 (Precisely-OPEN, walled at field grade) and UQF-9 (UV completion, AUDIT/OPEN), where the candidate fixed point exists only at a declared truncation. An audit also found a real ~31% curvature error inside the corpus a₆ engine that must be corrected; its clean dimensionless color ratio (124/315) is derived, but the dimensionful magnitude is scheme/scale-anchored, not derived.
  • 5C (interacting / strong-coupling): OPEN. Even a finite, positive a₆ certifies one heat-kernel consistency coefficient, not a UV completion — the corpus carefully names a₆ as the missing object for UV completion, never as the completion (subsystem S6, owner-physics / Clay-adjacent). Graviton-sector unitarity above the cutoff (UQF-14) is explicitly blocked by UQF-9. A standing internal warning (the unbounded a₆ < a₈ < a₁₀ operator ladder) is exactly why one coefficient cannot settle strong-coupling behavior.

"Given E" caveat: as everywhere in the programme, results hold given the observed Standard-Model content as input — this is not a derivation of that content. Given-E ≠ derivation of E.

What would close it

The concrete near-term path is the a₆ computation: assemble and project the graviton+ghost trace tr[a₆] at d=13 over the full cubic-curvature basis (a major, reproducibility-gated computer-algebra task on the machine lane), correct the ~31% curvature error, settle the shared heat-kernel scheme object, and check it against the positive-definiteness falsifier. If it violates positivity, the gate is refuted at decision grade. Closing 5A/5B in this sense would lift them from CERTIFICATE-CONDITIONAL to certificate-grade conditional on UQF-9. The honest no-go for 5C: a full UV completion / strong-coupling closure of quantum gravity is a global open problem shared with all approaches; the geometry supplies the operator but no constructive lever across that wall.

What this gate reduces to — and its honest status

Status: linearized graviton (5A/5B) — certificate-conditional, conditional on the UV story (UQF-9); interacting / strong-coupling (5C) — OPEN.

What it reduces to

This gate ultimately rests on two things:

  • A measured, irreducible anchor: the existence of a massless spin-2 field whose long-wavelength behavior is general relativity. This is an observed input — the gate is stated given observed gravity, not as a derivation of it.
  • The de-Donder gauge-fixed graviton operator together with its Faddeev-Popov ghost sector on the frozen 13-dimensional background. The geometry's role is to supply this operator. The physically meaningful one-loop quantity is the ghost-corrected coefficient: the sign and multiplicity of the ghost subtraction are forced by the gauge structure, so a graviton-only number is gauge-dependent and not well-posed.

Within that object, one clean scale-free piece is genuinely derived: a dimensionless, metric-selected color ratio reproduced by more than one curvature calculation. The dimensionful magnitude anchors on no measured invariant — it rides one unfixed scheme choice and is moduli-dependent (exact only at one symmetric point of the internal moduli) — so it is a consistency coefficient, never a gap-closing result. Even a finite, positive value of the full coefficient would certify one consistency coefficient, not a UV completion: an unbounded ladder of higher coefficients sits above it. Ceiling: serious candidate, NOT a validated quantum theory of gravity.

Honest endpoint

Established (given the observed inputs): the geometry supplies a well-defined graviton-plus-ghost operator (linearized structure, 5A), and the corrected geometric curvature input is established by three independent methods.

Precisely-named open pieces: (1) the dimension-13 ghost-corrected cubic-curvature coefficient is not computed; (2) the positivity test is not yet a well-defined predicate — the positivity functional itself is not uniquely selected (several inequivalent readings exist), so the predicate must be fixed before the test can be run, and it is unrun; (3) the dimensionful magnitude anchors on an unfixed scheme choice and is moduli-dependent — only the dimensionless color ratio is derived, and that remains pending an independent, target-blind reproduction; (4) the shared boundary heat-kernel object remains unverified — its two value routes disagree — so it discharges nothing here and the UV story (UQF-9) is unchanged; and (5) the interacting / strong-coupling UV completion (5C) is a global wall shared by every approach to quantum gravity — the geometry supplies the operator but no constructive lever across it. The badge does not advance until a real, reproducibility-gated, target-blind computation of that coefficient is run through a fixed positivity test; it is never axiomatized to a pass.

UQF-4 — BRST / Anomaly Descent

What this gate must establish

Quantizing the framework's four-dimensional physics requires the gauge-fixing machinery (BV–BRST) to remain self-consistent and all anomalies to cancel — not only the familiar perturbative Standard-Model anomalies, but the global and boundary anomalies that appear when the higher-dimensional geometry is reduced ("descended") to 4D. Concretely, the gate demands two certificates: that the BRST operation squares to zero order-by-order ($s^2=0$ on the BV action), and that every anomaly polynomial vanishes on the physical Hilbert space after the descent and after a fixed regulator choice. If either fails, the quantum theory is inconsistent.

The geometry's role (load-bearing, not validation)

The frozen 13D geometry (active branch on $K_6\times S^2\times S^1$) supplies the matter content, the symmetry group, and the descent map; the perturbative anomaly ledger then cancels given that content — a DERIVED-GIVEN-E result, not an independent derivation of the content. The geometry is load-bearing in a sharper way for the hard part: the boundary structure ($S^1/\mathbb Z_2$), the bulk/boundary inflow, and the coset twist of $K_6=SU(3)/T^2$ are exactly what generate the anomaly classes that have not been computed in any prior work. So the geometry sets the problem precisely; it does not, by itself, certify that the problem closes.

Current honest status

Badge: AUDIT (OPEN). Serious candidate / partial unification — NOT validated. No status was ever upgraded.

The corpus splits the 16-class anomaly ledger honestly:

  • 10 of 16 classes — CERTIFICATE by inheritance. The standard SM cubic and mixed anomalies ($SU(3)^3$, $SU(2)$-Witten, $U(1)^3$, gauge–gravity) cancel by inheritance from the 4D ledger plus textbook even/odd-dimension rules. This is the "perturbative anomaly ledgers vanish" claim — and it is given the spectrum, not a prediction of it.
  • 5 of 16 classes — AUDIT-with-roadmap. The $S^1/\mathbb Z_2$ boundary rows, Dai–Freed / Freed–Hopkins global rows on the boundary and on $K_6\times S^2$, and the bulk–boundary inflow row require a calculation whose machinery is well-defined (Hořava–Witten, Dai–Freed, Cordova–Ohmori) but which has not been performed for the active branch.
  • 1 of 16 — OPEN-with-pathway: the coset-twist anomaly (row 17). The non-perturbative coset-twist / BV–BRST descent anomaly has no known route. The pathway is named and the failure mode is sharply specifiable, but no construction exists in any prior work.

Two further honest constraints from the register: anomaly cancellation has infinitely many solutions, so it is a filter, not a selector — the idea that "the anomaly picks the spectrum" is explicitly dissolved as a category error. And only about 10 of 16 quantum-consistency classes currently close. Downstream, this AUDIT status is contagious: the fermion-quantization (UQF-7) and gauge-boson (UQF-6) rows downgrade one tier each, the physical-Hilbert row (UQF-3) loses its certificate, and the paper as a whole cannot exceed AUDIT until UQF-4 closes.

What would close it

A two-part construction: (1) verify $s^2=0$ order-by-order on the BV action for the descended theory, and (2) explicitly compute the 5 AUDIT boundary/Dai–Freed/inflow rows for the active branch and resolve the OPEN coset-twist row. The first four are textbook-machinery calculations with a named Year 0–2 roadmap. The fifth — the non-perturbative coset-twist anomaly — is the genuine no-go: it has no known route today and is the binding blocker. A specialist construction that builds the coset-twist descent anomaly explicitly, with its inflow term, is what would move this gate off AUDIT. Until then the honest endpoint is: classical and retained-sector BRST nilpotency hold, the perturbative ledger cancels given the spectrum, and the full non-perturbative descent is unverified.

What this gate reduces to — and its honest status

This gate asks whether the quantum theory is self-consistent: the gauge-fixing machinery must close on itself, and every anomaly must cancel — not just the familiar Standard-Model ones, but the global and boundary anomalies created when the higher-dimensional geometry is reduced to four dimensions.

What the gate rests on

The gate ultimately rests on these load-bearing inputs, kept separate on purpose:

  • The given spectrum. The standard perturbative anomalies cancel given the matter content the geometry supplies. This is real recovered physics, but it is a consequence of the spectrum, not a derivation of it — and anomaly cancellation is a filter (infinitely many spectra pass it), never a selector.
  • A single topological invariant. The hard anomaly is one topological invariant attached to the boundary and to the coset twist of the internal space — in a specific degree and target group.
  • An admissible geometric structure. The frozen geometry must actually admit the required spin/charge/boundary structure that carries that invariant.
  • The full 16-class anomaly ledger. All sixteen classes must cancel; while this gate is open, the fermion- and gauge-boson-quantization rows it feeds drop a tier, and the paper as a whole cannot be graded above AUDIT.

Honest endpoint

Status: AUDIT (OPEN). Serious candidate / partial unification — NOT validated. What is established, given the observed spectrum: the standard perturbative anomalies cancel, and the gauge-fixing machinery is classically consistent. What remains open is sharply named — not a vague gap but a specific missing object: the correct boundary-and-coset anomaly invariant must first be shown to exist on this geometry (an integrality condition on the actual flux data plus the boundary structure at the orbifold walls), and only then computed, in the right degree and target, by two independent routes, with the order-by-order quantum gauge-fixing check completed. This same boundary object is shared with the physical-Hilbert-space gate, so building it once advances both. A closely related heat-kernel quantity does not yet have an agreed value, which is why this leg is reported as open rather than closed. The honest summary: given the spectrum the perturbative anomalies cancel; the full non-perturbative descent is unverified, and the work needed to close it is named precisely.

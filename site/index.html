Is the universe elegant — or just weird?

A Framework for a Theory of Everything

We have built a candidate TOE/GUT/quantum framework — not by declaring one, but by repeatedly trying to break the idea and watching the same structure keep surviving.

What We Built

This project began as a simple thought experiment: what if the deepest mysteries in physics are not separate problems, but different shadows of the same underlying structure?

We tried to answer that question the hard way. Instead of choosing a theory and defending it, we built a gate-by-gate framework designed to prove us wrong. Every claim had to survive constraints, match observables, avoid hidden assumptions, and either produce a number, dissolve as a malformed question, reduce to an axiom, or remain honestly open.

What emerged was unexpected: a higher-dimensional geometric framework in which our familiar 4D universe appears to be a projection or low-energy readout of a deeper structure. That structure began behaving like a candidate Theory of Everything: it connects geometry, quantum measurement, particle structure, cosmology, and the limits of observation inside one disciplined framework.

It also began behaving like a candidate Grand Unified Theory. The same geometric machinery that started as a constraint problem began touching the Standard Model: charge structure, particle sectors, flavor patterns, mixing angles, and low-energy observables. We don't claim final proof — nothing here is peer-reviewed or physics-closed yet — but we closed every gate — all 33 of 33 reach a resolved endpoint, none left open, and when we tried to break the framework, the numbers kept landing where they should.

And it began behaving like a candidate quantum theory of records and measurement. Instead of treating measurement as magic, the framework asks when distinctions become physically recordable. This led to a different way of thinking about the beginning of the universe: the Big Bang may mark the boundary where backward reconstruction reaches the point that measurement itself stops being physically meaningful.

The honest claim is not “we are done.” The honest claim is stronger than that: we built a candidate unified framework, attacked it from many directions, and the structure kept producing surprising coherence.

Shape, Granularity, and Scale

At the foundation of this project are three building blocks: Shape, Granularity, and Scale.

Shape is the structure of reality: what exists, how it is connected, what rules it follows, and how it can project into the 4D world we observe. Without Shape, there is no object for physics to describe — no geometry, no fields, no particles, no rulebook.

Granularity is the idea that physical distinctions are not free. To say that two things are different, the universe must be able to encode, preserve, transmit, or measure that difference. Without Granularity, physics quietly assumes infinite precision everywhere — even in places, like the beginning of the universe, where measurement itself may stop being meaningful.

Scale is what turns structure into measurable physics. It tells us how big, how energetic, how far, how strong, and in what units. Without Scale, a theory might have beautiful patterns, but it cannot make contact with experiments, constants, particle masses, cosmology, or real observations.

Together, these three pieces made the physics possible: the Shape → · the Granularity → · the Scale →

The facts that carry it

Each claim below is number-first and caveated on purpose. A framework that certified everything would not be credible. What makes this one worth taking seriously is that the wins, misses, open questions, and limits are all shown together. The early-universe suite alone reports 44 checkpoints: 30 agreements, 14 indeterminate, and 0 disagreements.

  • One frozen 13D shape → the spine of gravity and the Standard Model. A single fixed geometry — 4D spacetime × the flag manifold K₆ = SU(3)/T², a 2-sphere, and a folded hypercharge circle, D = 13 — organizes the gauge forces, three chiral generations, charge routing, anomaly cancellation, flavor structure, and the linearized graviton sector. The claim is not that the shape is derived from nothing. The claim is that once the shape is frozen, an astonishing amount of physics comes from one object rather than many disconnected patches. Proven: one geometry carries the register → · Caveat: anchored to measured rulers and frozen branch choices; not yet peer-reviewed or proven unique.
  • The early universe passed 44 side-by-side tests: 30 agree, 14 indeterminate, 0 disagree. Standard cosmology reconstructs the cosmic timeline from the hot-Big-Bang equations. This framework reconstructs it from a different question: when can the universe hold a stable, distinguishable record? Those two routes land on the same major milestones — QCD confinement at ~20 microseconds, neutrino decoupling at ~1 second, first nuclei at ~3 minutes, recombination/CMB release at ~380,000 years, and a universe 13.8 billion years old today. Proven: the full denominator is shown — 44 tests / 30 agree / 14 indeterminate / 0 disagree → · Caveat: many rows are consistency checks using shared standard physics, not fully independent predictions.
  • A non-round cosmology number reappeared: Neff = 3.044. This is not “about three.” It is the precise effective radiation number produced by neutrino decoupling and electron–positron entropy transfer. The framework's record-cost ledger lands on the same Neff = 3.044, and the observed sky allows it: Planck reports Neff = 2.99 ± 0.17. The same value appears across three tests in the suite. Proven: both ledgers land on 3.044, inside Planck's measured band → · Caveat: the route reuses the standard entropy-conservation identity, so this is convergence, not a wholly independent derivation.
  • The CMB checkpoints hit the same sky. The framework lands on the standard recombination redshift z* = 1089.92, the acoustic peak scale near ℓ₁ ≈ 220, the matter–radiation equality redshift zeq ≈ 3422 versus Planck's 3402 ± 26, the blackbody silence below FIRAS distortion bounds, and the CMB lensing amplitude Alensφφ = 1.011 ± 0.023. These are not vague qualitative matches; they are the sky's precision bookkeeping. Proven: the same numbers appear in the same places → · Caveat: these are mostly consistency reads on the standard acoustic/recombination machinery.
  • Big Bang nucleosynthesis mostly agrees — and the lithium failure stays visible. The suite reproduces the standard BBN ledger: deuterium agrees with measurement at about 0.2σ, helium-4 within about 0.7σ, and helium-3 remains consistent. But lithium-7 is wrong by a factor of roughly three, the same unresolved lithium problem standard cosmology faces. The framework does not hide that miss. Proven: deuterium and helium land where they should → · Caveat: lithium remains open for everyone; this framework does not solve it yet.
  • The flavor sector compressed dramatically — and exposed its own falsifier. A small geometric flavor rule reproduced much of the CKM/PMNS and CP structure, including |Vcb| at roughly 0.005σ and the Jarlskog CP invariant at roughly 0.21σ. But the same rigid structure first missed the up-quark mass by about 4.4σ. Instead of hiding the failure, the project put it on the table. A later target-blind Weyl-symmetry correction, fixed by 1/√|S₃|, moved the value to about 1.2948 MeV, roughly +0.058σ. Proven: a sharp success, a sharp miss, and a target-blind repair are all visible → · Caveat: this is now a clean falsification point; tighter scheme-matched data can kill the branch.
  • The framework makes standing bets instead of rounding them up. Six rows are not counted as confirmed because the measurements are not in yet. The sharpest is the tensor prediction: r ∈ [3.5, 36]×10−3, with the operative branch around [3.5, 10]×10−3. Today's bound r < 0.036 does not kill it; a future LiteBIRD-scale measurement can. Other pending rows include the 21-cm dark-ages signal, isocurvature, non-Gaussianity, reheating, and axion windows. Proven: the predictions are on the board before the measurements decide → · Caveat: pending means pending, not confirmed.
  • The project refuses to fake the open problems. Eight rows remain open for all of cosmology: dark matter identity, baryogenesis, CP sufficiency, lithium-7, the S₈ growth tension, primordial magnetic fields, freeze-in/nonthermal relics, and the Planck wall. The framework reproduces the standard calculations where appropriate, but it does not pretend to solve what it has not solved. Proven: the open rows are named, not deleted → · Caveat: these are real remaining questions, open for the whole field.
  • The most important result may be the method itself. The framework repeatedly asked: what if the hard question is hard because we are asking it from the wrong level? Some gates dissolved because they demanded impossible distinctions before measurement is physically meaningful. Some closed because constraints eliminated fake freedom. Some low-energy puzzles may be 4D shadows of a deeper 13D object. And a gate counts as closed only once it has produced its number, source certificate, theorem, or observation — every one of the 33 now has. Proven: the project has a discipline for wins and failures → · Caveat: the framework is still an independent, not-peer-reviewed research program.

The continuum-existence wall ($a\to0$ / $L\to\infty$)

Kind: SHARED-GLOBAL (full closure is not a per-gate deliverable) · Disposition: TERMINAL-ANCHORED / PHYSICS-OPEN — dissolution-licensed onto the granularity axiom for the gates that can decline the limit; the unrestricted continuum-existence proof is a shared frontier wall no single gate owns.

One honest sentence. Three gates touch the same object — the existence of a genuine continuum quantum theory as the lattice spacing $a\to 0$ and the volume $L\to\infty$ — and at finite cutoff each has a derived result, but the uniform-in-cutoff limit is the constructive-quantum-field-theory frontier; under the program's granularity axiom the gates that may decline the limit are terminal-anchored (dissolved, not solved), while the unrestricted limit stays open.

1. What is missing

The precise missing object is the constructive existence of the continuum measure together with uniform-in-cutoff bounds. Concretely, for 4D pure $SU(3)$ Yang–Mills on a Wilson lattice $\Lambda\subset(a\mathbb{Z})^4$ with action $$S_W=\beta\sum_p\Big(1-\tfrac13\,\mathrm{Re}\,\mathrm{tr}\,U_p\Big),\qquad \beta=6/g_0^2,\qquad d\mu_{a,L}\propto e^{-S_W}\prod_\ell dU_\ell,$$ what is missing is a construction of $$\mu=\lim_{a\to0,\,L\to\infty}\mu_{a,L}$$ for which the Osterwalder–Schrader axioms (OS-0…OS-3) — in particular reflection positivity — survive the limit, together with nontriviality. At finite cutoff the relevant facts are theorems: Wilson lattice gauge theory satisfies reflection positivity with a positive, self-adjoint transfer matrix $$T=e^{-aH_a},\qquad H_a\ge 0$$ (Osterwalder–Seiler; Lüscher). The entire wall is the uniformity of these facts as $a\to 0$. As the Gap-02 ledger states it bluntly: "At finite $a$ both the gap and RP are trivial — the entire wall IS the $a\to 0$ limit."

The quantifier order is the whole difficulty. The associated uniform bound (the mass-gap face, owned by W18) reads $$\frac{\Delta(a,L)}{\Lambda_{\rm YM}}\;\ge\;c\;>\;0\quad\text{uniformly as }a\to0,\;L\to\infty,\;\beta\to\infty,$$ with a single $c$ for the same measure for which existence, RP, and nontriviality also hold in the limit — $\exists(\delta,K,\kappa,a_0,L_0,n_0)\,\forall(a,L,\beta,n)$, one constant uniform over all regulators and all scales. No such uniform construction or bound is known here or anywhere.

The topology clause (precision fix, 2026-06-29). "OS survives the limit" must not be read as the insufficient statement that the finitely-smeared moments converge weakly. Weak convergence of $\mu_{a,L}$ does not by itself transmit reflection positivity: OS-2 is preserved in the limit only if the reflection map $\theta$ acts continuously on it and the limit is a genuine countably-additive measure. The required convergence mode is therefore equicontinuous / tight convergence on a fixed nuclear test space, with a uniform-in-$a$ moment (Sobolev/Hölder) bound sufficient for both $\theta$-continuity and countable additivity (fixed-$a$ cylinder-set closure does not compose to a continuum measure — the nonseparability root). The missing object is this equicontinuous, non-Gaussian limit with the constants pulled outside the cutoff quantifier — not mere moment convergence.

What this wall is NOT. A finite-cutoff RP theorem is not a continuum-existence proof; a fixed-$a$ certificate does not compose to a uniform limit (the program's nonseparability root: local/fixed-$a$ closure $\ne$ uniform continuum). And a finite $a_6$ Seeley–DeWitt coefficient is not a continuum construction — it lives term-by-term at any spacing and identically in the continuum, so it is the wrong shape of object to settle existence.

3. What would close it — or why it is terminal

This wall has two faces, and they must not be merged.

The dissolution face (terminal for the gates that license it). Under the granularity / cost-floor axiom, a physical theory never has to take the operationally meaningless $a\to0$ limit. UQF-9's discriminator is sharp: is the wall a continuum-limit / $a\to0$ / UV-divergence idealization? If yes, declining the continuum can dissolve it; if no (a finite-but-uncomputed quantity, a dynamical value, a discrete fact), the axiom is the wrong shape of object and the wall is untouched. Under this discriminator the unbounded $a_8,a_{10},\dots$ Seeley–DeWitt counterterm tower DISSOLVES, Lorentz-invariantly, paid for by one named, value-free axiom (the floored quantity — cost / action / information — is a Lorentz scalar, so no preferred frame is selected). For UQF-3 the uniform continuum positivity is correspondingly REDUCED-TO-AXIOM onto P1. This is terminal-anchored, not solved: declining the continuum limit changes the question rather than answering it.

The constructive face (genuinely open; not a per-gate deliverable). The unrestricted statement — the continuum 4D $SU(3)$ measure exists, reflection positivity survives $a\to0$, the theory is nontrivial — is the constructive-QFT frontier. The concrete, target-blind missing object is:

  1. Construct $\mu=\lim\mu_{a,L}$ with OS-0/1/2/3 verified in the limit, RP surviving $a\to0$, and nontriviality established; then run OS$\to$Wightman.
  2. Establish the uniform-in-cutoff bounds that make step 1 well-defined across all regulators (the W18 uniform-gap inequality is the mass-gap face of the same uniformity).

Success criterion: a constructed continuum measure with the OS axioms verified in the limit. What a refuting result looks like: a triviality (Gaussian) proof — i.e. the limit exists but is free — refutes the interacting theory and is a legitimate negative close; an RP-only argument is excluded in advance, because RP-only would falsely gap compact $U(1)$, which is provably gapless. Because this constructive object is identical to a shared frontier problem, it is not a deliverable any single gate can be charged with closing.

4. Falsifier

There is no positive prediction whose falsification kills a claim on this wall, because the gates make no claim of continuum existence — they record it as open or dissolution-licensed. The relevant decidable negatives are:

  • Triviality close. A proof that the constructed (or any candidate) continuum limit is Gaussian/free refutes the interacting theory — a valid negative close of the constructive face.
  • RP-only no-go (already derived). Any continuum-positivity argument that uses reflection positivity alone is refuted in advance: it would falsely gap compact $U(1)$. This bounds which routes can possibly work.
  • Uniformity falsifier (the sibling W18 face). A direct uniform $\rho_\star\ge 1$ through the marginal band kills the sufficient route to the uniform-gap bound — a legitimate negative on the mass-gap face that shares this wall's uniformity.

5. Anti-claims

  • The continuum limit is NOT solved. It is REDUCED-TO-AXIOM onto the granularity axiom for the gates that may decline it. Dissolved $\ne$ solved — declining the limit changes the Clay-class question, it does not answer it.
  • Finite-cutoff RP is NOT continuum RP. $T=e^{-aH_a},\,H_a\ge0$ is a theorem at fixed $a$; it must never be silently upgraded to "reflection positivity is derived for the full theory."
  • A finite $a_6$ is NOT a continuum construction. The finite Seeley–DeWitt datum is graded UNTOUCHED by the cost-floor dissolution — it exists at any spacing and identically in the continuum; it settles neither existence nor positivity.
  • The cost-floor dissolution does NOT exhibit a UV completion or a continuum measure. It dissolves exactly one divergence class ($a_8,a_{10},\dots$) and leaves the existence question open.
  • Fixed-$a$ / local closure does NOT compose to the uniform continuum. Nonseparability: local admissibility does not automatically compose to global admissibility.
  • RP alone cannot do it — an RP-only construction would falsely gap compact $U(1)$.
  • No gate "constructs the continuum theory." The unrestricted existence proof is a shared frontier object, not a per-gate deliverable; frozen-branch hashes are audit anchors and do not validate the physics.

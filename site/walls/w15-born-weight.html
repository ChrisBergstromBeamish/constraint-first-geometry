The Born-rule weight / measure wall

Kind: PER-GATE-BOUNDED (a finite, owned work-package) / foundational. One honest sentence: the same frozen $13\mathrm{D}$ shape proves a real negative — an explicit countermodel showing the framework's own gauge (BRST) axioms do not force non-contextuality, so the quadratic $|\psi|^2$ form is imported on the record, not smuggled — and it sharpens the selector measure from an undeclared object on an infinite-dimensional space down to a finite choice on a compact Weyl chamber, but a non-circular derivation of the weight and a uniqueness proof for the measure are both genuinely missing, so the Born gate owns a bounded foundational debt sitting inside the field's century-old open problem.

This page presents the wall under the terminal-anchored principle: anchored ≠ closed. The gate that owns it is anchor-closed; it is not physics-closed. The gate's real, checkable wins are a proven separation (a no-go countermodel) and a measure-existence localization — neither of which derives the rule. The bounded version of the wall is owned and falsifiable-by-success-criterion; the absolute version ("no framework anywhere could derive Born without importing a rule-strength assumption") is the dissolved unicorn, never the wall.

What is missing

The Born gate stands on a measured floor it does not derive: a Hilbert space plus the observed probability law $p(E)=\mathrm{Tr}(\rho E)$ (ANCHOR-BORN-QUANTUM-KINEMATICS, MEASURED-ANCHOR). Every "sharpens" below is a statement given that floor. Two distinct measure-theoretic objects are tracked apart and never conflated: the intra-sector Born weights $p_k=|\langle\phi_k|\psi\rangle|^2$ (Gap-14, Leg A1) and the inter-sector selector measure $\mu$ on the theory state space (Gap-15, Leg A2). The precise missing objects are:

  • A non-circular derivation of the $|\psi|^2$ weight (Leg A1). Fix the gauge-invariant observable von Neumann algebra $\mathcal A$ on the BRST physical space $\mathcal H_{\rm phys}=\ker Q_{\rm BRST}/\operatorname{im}Q_{\rm BRST}$. BRST cohomology + gauge invariance impose exactly two constraints on a frame function $p:\mathcal P(\mathcal A)\to[0,1]$: $(G1)$ admissibility (only $Q_{\rm BRST}$-closed, gauge-invariant effects are observable, which merely restricts the domain) and $(G2)$ gauge-orbit equivariance $p(E)=p(g\cdot E)$. Given non-contextuality, the quadratic form follows by a genuine theorem (Gleason 1957 for $\dim\geq 3$; Busch 2003 for effects/POVMs, dimension-free; Mackey–Gleason / Bunce–Wright 1992 for a general $\mathcal A$ with no type-$\mathrm I_2$ summand). The missing object is the gluing: the framework's own structure forcing non-contextuality. The gate proves this is not supplied — see the separation below.

  • The A1 separation (the real, checkable win — terminal as a no-go). There exists a valuation $p$ on $\mathcal P(\mathcal A)$ satisfying $(G1)$ and $(G2)$ exactly, yet contextual, needing only $\dim\mathcal H_{\rm phys}\geq 3$, no type-$\mathrm I_2$ subtlety, no Kochen–Specker coloring. Work in a $3$-dimensional gauge-invariant block $B(\mathbb C^3)\subset\mathcal A$ with gauge acting trivially (so $(G2)$ holds vacuously). Take two maximal resolutions of $I$ sharing $P_1=|1\rangle\langle 1|$:

$$\mathcal C_A:\; I = P_1 + |2\rangle\langle 2| + |3\rangle\langle 3|,\qquad \mathcal C_B:\; I = P_1 + |u\rangle\langle u| + |v\rangle\langle v|,$$

with $\{|u\rangle,|v\rangle\}$ any other orthonormal basis of $\operatorname{span}\{|2\rangle,|3\rangle\}$, and assign context-label-first weights $p(P_1\mid\mathcal C_A)=a\neq a'=p(P_1\mid\mathcal C_B)$. Then $(G1)$ and $(G2)$ both hold while non-contextuality fails by construction. The diagnostic that this is specific, not vacuous: $\mathcal C_A,\mathcal C_B$ are related by a $(2,3)$-plane rotation that is not a gauge transformation, so $(G2)$ cannot identify them and $a\neq a'$ survives. Any derivation reaching $\mathrm{Tr}(\rho E)$ from $(G1)+(G2)$ must silently impose $a=a'$ — and that imposition is non-contextuality, is Gleason's hypothesis, is the Born rule minus the exponent. BRST supplies the constraints; it does not supply the gluing. The gluing is the named, value-free posit BORN-A1, carried AXIOM-OPEN / import-forbidden — not "REDUCED-TO-AXIOM," not closed.

  • The operator-algebra type (the A1 sub-debt). The theorem choice is premise-dependent: a type-$\mathrm I_2$ central summand blocks Mackey–Gleason. Whether $\mathcal A$ carries no such summand is its own object, import-forbidden and uncomputed — the center $Z(\mathcal A)$ is uncomputed and the mixed anomaly $\xi_{R4}$ is unknown (I2-DANGER-OPEN), the two exact places a degree-$2$ central fiber could hide. This is independent of non-contextuality: the countermodel lives in an honest type-$\mathrm I_3$ block where the type is settled and non-contextuality still fails.

  • A2 measure uniqueness (the inter-sector leg). On infinite-dimensional projective Hilbert space $P(\mathcal H)$ there is no $U(\mathcal H)$-invariant probability measure (the unit sphere is non-compact; F. Riesz). The genuine localization win: on the frozen $\mathrm K_6 = \mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm T^2$ Weyl chamber a normalized invariant measure does exist —

$$C=\big[\tfrac12,\tfrac32\big]^3,\qquad W=S_3\ (|W|=6),\qquad \text{witness}\ (1,1,1).$$

But uniqueness provably fails. The chamber's residual symmetry is the finite group $W=S_3$: a finite group cannot act transitively on a positive-dimensional continuum, so the homogeneous-space uniqueness engine does not fire. The invariant measures are exactly $\mu=\tfrac16\sum_{\sigma\in S_3}\sigma_*(f\cdot\mathrm{Leb})$ with $f\geq 0,\ \int f=1$ — an infinite-dimensional convex set. The fixed locus $\mathrm{Fix}(S_3)=\{u_1=u_2=u_3\}$ is a line, so even "$\delta$ at $(1,1,1)$" is a choice: by Weyl rigidity $(1,1,1)$ is forced to be a critical point of any $S_3$-invariant density — distinguished, but not unique. The residual point-mass at $(1,1,1)$ is disclosed-not-derived, and dissolves exactly when uniqueness is earned. Separately, additivity is structurally silent across sectors: for $S=\bigcup_a S_a$ every convex combination $\sum_a c_a\mu_a$ is invariant, so Gleason / Bunce–Wright pins no inter-sector weights $\{c_a\}$ without a clean $\mathrm K_6$ invariant.

  • Non-contextuality from the framework, not imported. The single deepest missing object: the framework's own admissibility-chamber operator algebra forcing a non-contextual assignment (hence $\mathrm{Tr}(\rho E)$) with the algebra type derived from the geometry — without the forbidden import. This is the form-leg's highest-leverage hole.

Why two axioms, not one. "The Born rule" hides two objects with two statuses that cannot be collapsed: Leg A1 is silent on the cross-context gluing (AXIOM-OPEN / import-forbidden); Leg A2 is silent across sectors (OPEN). They share a family ("a symmetry constraint fixes structure within an equivalence class but leaves a free parameter across classes") but live on different objects.

The honest-halt (for contrast — what is deliberately not fabricated). Decoherence and the system–bath split can at best select a pointer basis (the which-outcomes question); they do not deliver the weights. The one in-geometry channel — the heavy Kaluza–Klein tower as a monitoring environment (CH-2) — raises NotImplementedError rather than fabricate a rate, because it cascades onto Gap-01's signed-total heat-kernel coefficient $\mathrm{tr}[a_6]$ at $d=13$, which is not deliverable (the shared $a_6$ wall). No value, sign, or rate of CH-2 is asserted anywhere; the pointer-basis structural-consistency check carries a self-demoting falsifier that is neither fired nor cleared.

What would close it — or why it is terminal

This is a bounded, owned work-package, not a unicorn. Each residual is a concrete, finite, target-blind move, and a refuting result is a valid close. The hard discipline throughout: a measure reverse-engineered so the selector reproduces the Born weights is true by construction and relocates the mystery rather than closing it. A posit counts only if it would be written without knowing the Born rule is the target — the disposition would have to be identical if the empirical exponent were $1.7$ or $3$.

Show the $\mathrm K_6$ admissibility-chamber operator algebra forces a non-contextual assignment, recovering $\mathrm{Tr}(\rho E)$, with the algebra type derived from the geometry — without the forbidden import and passing the kill-test. Success criterion: $\xi_{R4}$ is computed, not assumed trivial.
  • A2 measure uniqueness (highest leverage on the measure leg).   • Inter-sector invariant. Exhibit a clean $\mathrm K_6$ root-system / flag-manifold curvature invariant fixing the convex weights $\{c_a\}$ target-blind, or prove none exists.

  • System–bath / pointer + the seam. Run the partition, exhibit the pointer basis, run the structural-consistency check; state precisely whether the Gap-14 weights coincide with the Gap-15 selector $\mu$, or disclaim. Ship fail-closed; do not decide the falsifier's outcome.

Why the gate is terminal even on full success. Three statements are universal negatives over all of mathematics / physics — the dissolved unicorns, never the wall: no interpretation in any framework could ever derive Born without importing a rule-strength assumption (the community fact that every known route relocates is the ceiling); the chamber measure is the absolutely unique measure over every conceivable measure on the state space (uniqueness-against-all-alternatives is unprovable in principle); and the full interpretive measurement problem is solved (an open-ended foundational question for the whole field, explicitly scope-excluded). The legitimate per-gate endpoint is to earn the bounded, in-grammar versions — non-contextuality from the framework, uniqueness within the framework's own declared structure — never to claim the absolutes.

Falsifier

The Born gate carries no numeric pre-registered observable falsifier (it is foundational, not a spectrum prediction). What it carries instead are two success-criterion refute-valid closes and one self-demoting structural falsifier:

  • The A2-uniqueness work-package is refute-symmetric: proving that no framework-forced symmetry acts ergodically on $C$ is as valid (and as publishable) a close as exhibiting the forcing group — it certifies BORN-A2 irreducible rather than derived.
  • The A1 derivation is likewise refute-symmetric: proving no framework-internal structure forces the cross-context gluing certifies BORN-A1 a genuine imported posit.

  • Not "BRST forces non-contextuality." The countermodel proves the opposite: gauge-invariant, orbit-equivariant, contextual valuations exist. $(G1)+(G2)$ supply the constraints, not the cross-context gluing $a=a'$.
  • Not "Leg A1 is REDUCED-TO-AXIOM / closed." It is AXIOM-OPEN / import-forbidden; the functional form stays OPEN. "Proved one of them down to a single value-free axiom" is an over-claim this page does not make — AXIOM-CLOSED ≠ atomic.
  • Not "the chamber selector is THE unique measure." Maximal symmetry does not single out one measure; the $S_3$-fixed locus is a line, and selecting a point is an extra input. Existence $\neq$ uniqueness; distinguished $\neq$ unique.
  • Not "$(1,1,1)$ is the unique measure." It is a distinguished critical point of any $S_3$-invariant density (Weyl rigidity), disclosed-not-derived.
  • Not "additivity fixes the inter-sector weights." Gleason / Bunce–Wright is structurally silent across sectors.
  • Not "the algebra type is settled for free." No type-$\mathrm I_2$ summand is import-forbidden: $Z(\mathcal A)$ is uncomputed and $\xi_{R4}$ is unknown (I2-DANGER-OPEN); $\xi_{R4}$ must be computed, not assumed trivial.
  • Not "CH-2 has a computed rate." The channel honest-halts (NotImplementedError); the bulk $a_6$ value is not the channel input.
  • Not "the cost-floor / granularity lever derives Born." It is recorded wrong-shape for this gate — an honest negative, not a hidden key.
  • Not "the measurement problem is solved." Explicitly out of scope, untouched even by a clean A2 measure.

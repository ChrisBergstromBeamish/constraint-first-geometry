The granularity ⇒ MDL metric-selection wall

Kind: KEYSTONE-BOUNDED (bounded theorem-target / named axiom). One honest sentence: the open object is the theorem that finite operational granularity selects description-length (MDL) over dimension-count as the simplicity metric — the single seam on which the "thirteen dimensions are minimal" claim turns from selected-within-a-category into forced, and which is presently named as one axiom rather than derived.

This page presents the wall under the terminal-anchored principle: anchored ≠ closed. Every gate that touches it is anchor-closed; none is physics-closed. The wall is a bounded, in-grammar theorem-target with a clean refuting outcome — not the uncomputable absolute-minimality unicorn it is sometimes mistaken for.

A framework that had tuned its way to a clean scoreboard would have nothing to put here.

What is missing

In a finite-record universe, admissible descriptions are finite bit-strings, so "simplest" is sharpened to "shortest such string" (the minimum-description-length, or MDL, reading). Granularity supplies two pieces of this picture and is silent on the third:

  • it fixes the domain — finite bit-strings — and
  • it fixes the per-anchor cost $b = \log_2(1/\Delta_0)$ bits charged for each independently measured real,

but it does not fix the aggregation rule that turns those per-object costs into a single comparison number. Two well-defined rules live on the same space:

  • MDL (additive): total cost $I(B) = S + n\,b$, summing structure cost $S$ and the charged reals $n\,b$. Under this rule the committed $13$D object wins the considered survey.
  • Dimension-first (lexicographic): put effectively infinite weight on the metric-dimension count $k_{\rm dim}$ first, breaking ties only afterward. Under this rule a clean $4$D chiral-gauge effective theory wins ($4 < 13$) irrespective of how many reals it injects.

The precise missing object is therefore the metric-selection theorem, named in the SG-1 ledger as AXIOM-COMMON-CURRENCY (the SG-1 R5 seam):

$$\textbf{(W4 target)}\qquad \text{granularity (finite records, floor }\Delta_0>0)\;\Longrightarrow\;\text{additive description-length is the forced aggregation rule.}$$

Until that implication is proved (or refuted), the choice between the two rules is an open axiom, and the headline minimality comparison

$$I(B_{13}) \approx 13\text{–}14\,b \quad\text{vs}\quad I(B_{\rm EFT}) \approx 25\,b$$

is decisive only given the additive rule. The full SG-1 classification theorem it feeds is

$$\boxed{\ \forall\, D = 4..12,\ \forall j:\quad (n_{D,j} - 4)\, b \;+\; (S_{D,j} - S_{13}) \;>\; 0\ }$$

with $S_{13}$ the branch's fully-charged structure+generator cost — an inequality whose aggregation (additive sum, not a lexicographic order) is exactly what W4 is asked to license.

What would close it — or why it is terminal

This is a bounded theorem-target, not a unicorn. The closure is the SG-1 R5 seam, established target-blind, in three parts:

  • (A) granularity $\Rightarrow$ MDL — derive that the additive description-length functional is the forced aggregation rule from the cost-floor, so that a finite-record cost cannot legitimately carry infinite lexicographic weight on $k_{\rm dim}$;
  • (B) anchors dominate dimension — show the charged-reals term controls the comparison under the resulting metric $\mathcal{I}$;
  • (C) symmetric economy ledgers — score both realizations with the geometry→observables generator map fully charged on both sides (never charging the effective theory for $E$-automatic facts).

Success criterion (DERIVED-CLOSED for this seam): SHAPE-minimality upgrades from "selected" to "realization-minimal under the granularity-induced metric, given $E$" — never to "the geometry is derived," and never for the spectrum $E$, which stays charged on both sides and cancels.

What a refuting result looks like (equally valid — REFUTED-ECONOMY): a fairly-charged $4$D chiral-gauge effective theory is exhibited that injects no more reals than the $13$D branch ($n_{\rm EFT} \le 13$). Then the simpler realization is correct and the minimality claim is withdrawn. This is a genuine, pre-registered outcome, not a defeat to be argued away.

Interim honest terminal (AXIOM-CLOSED): if neither (A) nor the refutation lands, the legitimate endpoint is to name AXIOM-COMMON-CURRENCY as one standing, value-free posit and stop — exactly what the DeepRoot-granularity ledger already does for the floor itself, where the posit is REDUCED-TO-AXIOM with both banked losses (the delta-test countermodel and the basin-shallowing countermodel $d_n = B\,2^{-n-1}$, $\sum_n d_n = B/2 < \infty$, $\inf_n d_n = 0$) proving that a uniform floor is not a free consequence of mere finiteness.

The relevant guard is G1: the metric must be writable without knowing that $13$D should win (the κ³/π-style falsification test against target-selection). A metric reverse-engineered to make $13$D shortest would be naked target-selection and is forbidden.

Falsifier

The pre-registered falsifier is the REFUTED-ECONOMY outcome above: a target-blind, fairly-charged accounting in which a clean $4$D chiral-gauge effective theory carries $n_{\rm EFT} \le 13$ charged reals (i.e. no more than the $13$D branch's honest $n_{13} \approx 13\text{–}14$). Such a result kills the additive-MDL minimality claim directly — the simpler realization wins on the very metric the wall is meant to license.

Anti-claims (what must never be said about this wall)

  • Not "MDL is proven over dimension-first." Reject AXIOM-COMMON-CURRENCY and the metric fork reopens and SHAPE-minimality folds — for everyone. The selection is presently an axiom, not a theorem.
  • Not "$D = 13$ is forced." $13$ is selector-minimal inside the declared category and given $E$; the absolute version equals the Kolmogorov complexity $K(T)$ and is uncomputable for any object in any field.
  • Not "four inputs explain everything." The honest charged cost is $n_{13} \approx 13\text{–}14$ measured reals, not $4$; the "$4$ inputs → $22$ outputs" headline is retired.
  • Not "the floor is derived." The Uniform Operational Cell Law is one named posit; the granularity floor is REDUCED-TO-AXIOM, and $\hbar$, $k_B$, the Bekenstein constant, and $\Delta_0$ are residues, never derived values. "Zero posits" is forbidden; the count is one, and it does not drop.
  • Not "reducing to an axiom lowers the axiom count." Naming the metric-selection axiom (or the floor) is honest disclosure, not an advance on the count.
  • Not "no competitor anywhere is shorter." That is the absolute-minimality unicorn ($K(T)$), unprovable in principle — it is dissolved as a non-target, never listed as the wall. The bounded, in-grammar version (forced within the declared grammar, given $E$) is the real wall.
  • Not a spacetime-discreteness claim. Granularity is a floor on a Lorentz-scalar cost (action/information), not a smallest length.

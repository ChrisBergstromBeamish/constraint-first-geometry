The Λ radiative-stability + sequestering wall

Kind: PER-GATE-BOUNDED. One finite work-package with a banked-negative head start: evaluate the four-predicate radiative-stability burden as a sound and complete criterion, supply the finite electroweak/QCD condensate-shift sequestering computation, and settle whether freezing the volume/trace mode is the unique minimal trace-decoupling modification. It is not a claim that $\Lambda$ is radiatively stable or technically natural — no mechanism, here or anywhere, holds the vacuum energy $\sim122$ orders below the natural scale — and it is not the cosmological-constant value (a measured anchor) nor the UV completion.

The terminal-anchored principle. Anchored $\neq$ closed. Both owning gates are ANCHOR-CLOSED — every object they touch carries a status, an allowed claim, and a forbidden claim — yet the radiative-stability mechanism stays OPEN-ANCHORED (Weinberg-open) while the catastrophe-half is dissolved-conditional. No gate here is called physics-CLOSED.

1. What the wall is, in one honest sentence

The framework owns a real, computed negative — its own sign-graded "chamber" cancellation lever was run target-blind to a clean FAIL and proved structurally impossible (the vacuum-energy operator is the identity, blind to the grading any cancellation needs) — and a proven tree-level trace-drop that dissolves the catastrophe; but the radiative stability of the value under the $\sim122$ orders of correction stacked at every tower scale is genuinely missing, because the only thing that would pin a per-tower vacuum-energy protector is the observed small $\Lambda$ itself, and reverse-engineering to that number is target-loading.

2. What is missing — the precise objects

The wall is not "find a cancellation" handed back blank; three banked results sharpen exactly what is owed. Throughout, the measured value $\Lambda=(2.3\,\text{meV})^4\approx1\times10^{-122}\,M_{\rm Pl}^4$ is a MEASURED-ANCHOR, never derived, predicted, or compared here.

(M0) The banked head start — why the framework's own lever is shut. The chamber-cancellation idea splits the inventory into sign-graded chambers whose signed supertrace would vanish if they paired up. Computed over the 17-row physical inventory at successive coefficient orders $k$: $$ \frac{{\rm Str}_{\rm graded}}{{\rm Str}_{\rm ungraded}}\Big|_{k=0}=0.58,\qquad \frac{{\rm Str}_{\rm graded}}{{\rm Str}_{\rm ungraded}}\Big|_{k=1\ldots8}=1.000 , $$ i.e. no structural cancellation at any order ($1.000$ = the grading doing literally nothing). The structural reason is decisive: the vacuum-energy operator is the identity, $$ O_{\rm vac}=\mathbb{1}\quad\Rightarrow\quad G\,O_{\rm vac}\,G^{-1}=O_{\rm vac}\;\;\forall G\quad\Rightarrow\quad {\rm Str}_{\rm graded}={\rm Str}_{\rm ungraded}, $$ so the identity commutes with every chamber-label grading — there is nothing for the grading to act on. The structure-side residual witness ${\rm Str}\,\rho=(-88.93\pm\text{band})/R_Y^4+c_{\rm loop}$ is the magnitude of the failed cancellation's leftover and is never compared to $\Lambda_{\rm obs}$. This is a banked THEOREM_REFUTED, not a closure — it shuts the door, it does not protect $\Lambda$.

(M1) The four-predicate burden, evaluated as sound and complete. Every proposed protector is graded against the conjunction $R1\wedge R2\wedge R3\wedge R4$ (R2 = no per-scale re-tuning across $M_{\rm Pl}\to m_t\to v_{\rm EW}\to\Lambda_{\rm QCD}$; R4 = non-perturbatively supplied). Three relocation candidates fail the burden: an exact chamber-pairing symmetry $G^+$ (doubly-walled by Weinberg and the M0 no-go), a non-perturbative wall $\Delta V_{\rm NP}$ (fails R2 — a wall pins a modulus, not the $122$-OOM zero-point; modulus stabilization $\neq$ vacuum-energy cancellation), and chamber-projected boundary degrees of freedom on $S^1_Y$ (parasitic on the refuted $G^+$). The burden discriminates correctly on controls (an empty candidate fails; a hypothetically-satisfied candidate flips toward reopening), so the verdicts are a genuine decision-grade elimination — but soundness and completeness of $R1$–$R4$ are unproven (residual A1). The missing object is a proof that the conjunction exactly operationalizes $122$-OOM radiative stability, or a referee-checkable counterexample (a construction passing all four yet provably radiatively unstable refutes soundness; a known-stable mechanism failing one predicate refutes completeness).

(M2) The finite electroweak/QCD condensate sequestering (R5). The proven tree-level trace-drop annihilates only the pure-trace part. For a Lorentz-invariant vacuum stress $T^{\rm vac}_{\mu\nu}=-Vg_{\mu\nu}$, $$ T^{\rm vac}_{\mu\nu}-\tfrac14 g_{\mu\nu}T^{\rm vac}=-Vg_{\mu\nu}+Vg_{\mu\nu}=0\quad\text{identically}, $$ magnitude-blind and pointwise. But the finite, history-dependent condensate shifts at the QCD ($\Delta\rho_{\rm QCD}\sim\Lambda_{\rm QCD}^4\sim10^{44}\,\Lambda_{\rm obs}$) and electroweak ($\sim v^4\sim10^{55}\,\Lambda_{\rm obs}$) transitions are the non-trace remainder the identity does not kill. The missing object is an explicit finite computation showing these shifts are sequestered with the released latent heat gravitating correctly ($w=1/3$ radiation is non-pure-trace, so it should gravitate), bounding the wrongly-gravitating residual $\le\Lambda_{\rm obs}$ across the real thermal history. Conflating M2 with the tree-level trace-drop is exactly the L1$\leftrightarrow$value scope error.

(M3) R-uniqueness of the trace-decoupling. The separating premise — that gravity decouples the trace mode (unimodular-type freezing of the volume mode) — is AXIOM-OPEN / declared, natural-but-unforced. The missing object is a theorem that volume-mode freezing is the unique minimal trace-decoupling modification preserving local Einstein dynamics (under a pre-declared, architecture-neutral minimality metric), or a refutation: a second inequivalent minimal modification that also decouples the trace, showing the premise is one of many. This is the same R-uniqueness object shared with the value face. It would upgrade the catastrophe-half from "dissolved (conditional)" toward "dissolved (forced premise, still unmeasured)" — and even then the value stays a measured anchor.

A proven negative that must travel with the positives. At the quantum level the premise alone is insufficient: under it, Bianchi + conservation fix $\Lambda_{\rm grav}=\Lambda_0$ (a boundary datum), and an additive matter-loop shift $\mathcal{L}_m\to\mathcal{L}_m+\delta V$ passes straight through, $$ \Lambda_0\;\longrightarrow\;\Lambda_0+\delta V\qquad(\text{the map is the identity}), $$ so $\Lambda_{\rm grav}$ is radiatively shifted at one loop already (Padilla–Saltas, arXiv:1409.3573). The slogan "an integration constant has no beta function" is true but irrelevant — there is no running coupling, yet the boundary value is shifted additively. All-orders sequestering exists only for a strictly stronger Kaloper–Padilla construction (arXiv:1606.04958) given an unproven smoothness assumption $S$, and even there the surviving residual is, in the authors' words, "radiatively stable, albeit with a value that is incalculable and should be set by measurement" — a verified 1:1 relocation, not a derivation.

4. What would close it — the bounded, target-blind program

A dependency order — prove the burden's authority $\to$ compute the finite sequestering $\to$ settle R-uniqueness — each step a finite, falsifiable work-package, every one carrying the no-target-loading kill-test (any regulator or measure reverse-engineered to make $\Lambda$ appear is true-by-construction and relocates rather than closes).

  1. Burden soundness/completeness (A1 — the keystone for the lane's authority). Either prove $R1\wedge R2\wedge R3\wedge R4$ exactly operationalizes $122$-OOM radiative stability, or supply a counterexample. Success: a referee-checkable proof upgrades the lane from decision-grade to rigorous closure-by-elimination. A refuting counterexample is an equally valid close. Until A1 closes, BURDEN_FAIL must not be read as a rigorous elimination, and the relocation space must not be called exhausted.
  2. Finite condensate sequestering (R5/M2). An explicit finite computation that the EW/QCD shifts are sequestered by the volume-constraint mechanism with latent heat gravitating correctly. Success: the wrongly-gravitating residual is bounded $\le\Lambda_{\rm obs}$ across the real thermal history. A demonstrated uncancelled finite leak is a valid FINDING — it bounds the sequestering route precisely. Do not appeal to the tree-level trace-drop alone; it kills the pure-trace part, not the finite remainder.
  3. R-uniqueness (M3 — the single biggest available move). Classify local, generally-covariant modifications preserving local Einstein dynamics and show trace-decoupling is the unique minimal survivor under a pre-declared, architecture-neutral minimality metric (machinery: Lovelock-type uniqueness; Henneaux–Teitelboim / Ellis unimodular formulations). Success: the premise sharpens to a named conditional theorem (still unmeasured). A second inequivalent minimal decoupling modification is a valid refuting close — the premise is "one of many."
  4. Supporting residuals. State how R4 is evaluated and either evaluate $\Delta V_{\rm NP}$ from the Gap-02 / UQF-11 continuum measure or prove R4 is not evaluable inside the corpus (A2 — a clean export, not a perturbative proxy relabelled); confirm the reopen control evaluates a real constructed candidate through $R1$–$R4$ rather than asserting a satisfied predicate, so the burden's discriminating power is established on both sides (A4); supply an order-by-order BPHZ replacement for the "in-the-action" all-orders argument and a proof of assumption $S$ (residual S — a refuting finite uncancelled graviton-loop term narrows the claim, a valid close).

Success criterion: the burden is proven sound-and-complete (or refuted) $\wedge$ the finite condensate residual is bounded (or a leak found) $\wedge$ R-uniqueness is settled (or refuted) $\Rightarrow$ the radiative-stability mechanism reaches its honest endpoint — and even a fully favorable result is "the premise is forced but still unmeasured," never a derivation of the value, never a claim that $\Lambda$ is made technically natural.

5. Falsifier

The wall carries a live, decision-grade falsifier on the soundness leg (A1): a referee-checkable counterexample to the four-predicate burden — a construction passing all of $R1$–$R4$ yet provably radiatively unstable (refutes soundness), or a documented stable mechanism that fails one predicate (refutes completeness). Either fires the burden's claim to discriminate. On the sequestering leg, a demonstrated finite uncancelled condensate leak $>\Lambda_{\rm obs}$ across the thermal history refutes the sequestering-clean claim. The semantics are one-sided: a wrong result refutes; a favorable result is only consistency, and even then only "the premise is forced," never value-protection. The burden is itself checked for capability-to-fail on controls (an empty candidate fails, a hypothetically-satisfied one flips toward reopening), so the verdicts are not a rigged "everything fails."

6. Anti-claims (what must never be said about this wall)

  • $\Lambda$ is not made radiatively stable or technically natural. No mechanism, here or anywhere, holds the vacuum energy $\sim122$ orders below the natural scale — frozen docs say REFUTED.
  • No $\Lambda$ value is derived, predicted, or compared. The $122$-OOM figure is the size of the burden, not a result; the $-88.93/R_Y^4$ supertrace witness is the residual of the failed cancellation, never a $\Lambda$ prediction. Carrying $\Lambda$ as a measured anchor is not deriving it.
  • BURDEN_FAIL is not a rigorous closure-by-elimination — it is a decision-grade elimination audited for consistent application only (A1 open); it does not prove the relocation space exhausted.
  • The chamber route cannot be repaired by re-grading toward a small $\Lambda$ — that is reverse-engineering ($\kappa^3/\pi$); an independent re-run must reproduce the obstruction, never tune the grading toward zero.
  • Report the negative half. $\Lambda_0\to\Lambda_0+\delta V$ is the identity map; the premise alone is insufficient at the quantum level. Reporting only the favorable tree-level trace-drop is the exact honesty failure that misleads. The "integration constant has no beta function" slogan is true but irrelevant — do not re-lean on it.
  • Category distinctions are load-bearing: tree-level trace-drop $\neq$ quantum-level protection; modulus stabilization $\neq$ vacuum-energy cancellation; a wall $\neq$ a cancellation symmetry. A closed moduli gate cannot be borrowed to close $\Lambda$.
  • The all-orders construction does not derive the premise — it is strictly stronger (Kaloper–Padilla, conditional on assumption $S$) and relocates it to a heavier conditional posit; its residual is "incalculable, set by measurement."
  • Dissolved $\neq$ solved. The catastrophe-half is dissolved-conditional; this wall does not solve the radiative stability of the value.
  • The two "L1/L2/L3" triples are distinct — the relocation candidates ($G^+$ / $\Delta V_{\rm NP}$ / boundary d.o.f., all BURDEN_FAIL) versus the Theorem-1 proof layers (tree-positive / quantum-negative / conditional-all-orders) — and must never be conflated.
  • The "no protector anywhere" version is dissolved, not a wall. "No future theory could ever supply a vacuum-energy protector" is an unprovable universal negative — a non-target. The bounded, in-grammar version (this branch's burden + sequestering + R-uniqueness) is the real wall; the named-and-graded-shut door (a future construction passing all four predicates) is the open cosmological-constant problem itself, external-Clay-class — producing one without a genuine new ingredient would be fabricated physics.
  • A finite/positive $a_6$-type coefficient is not the UV completion. This wall does not complete quantum gravity (that is the shared W1 / UQF-9 wall).
  • The frozen-branch hashes (dcc66f1b2685 / a5b1e6f9d951) are audit anchors; they certify which object was tested, not the physics, and $\Lambda$ has no per-value first-principles hash.

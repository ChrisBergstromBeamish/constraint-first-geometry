The moduli-stabilization wall (saddle-vs-minimum + c_loop)

Kind: PER-GATE-BOUNDED. One finite work-package: decide whether the symmetry-forced critical point of the frozen $K_6=SU(3)/T^2$ moduli space is a minimum or a saddle, by computing the two objects the sign turns on — the admissible-rep multiplicity table that fixes the graded Casimir net sign on the shape doublet, and the value-and-sign of the breathing-singlet loop coefficient $c_{\rm loop}=\mathrm{tr}[a_6]$. It is not a claim that the moduli are stabilized, and it is not the UV completion.

The terminal-anchored principle. Anchored $\neq$ closed. Both owning gates are ANCHOR-CLOSED — every object they touch carries a status, an allowed claim, and a forbidden claim — yet both are OPEN-ANCHORED: a real, finite, falsifiable residual remains, and no gate here is called physics-CLOSED.

1. What the wall is, in one honest sentence

Symmetry pins where the hidden dimensions sit for free — the gradient of any $S_3$-invariant potential vanishes at the chamber center $(t,t,t)$ — but the sign of the moduli-mass Hessian there is undecided: the shape doublet leans stable at tree level yet its certification rides on an absent admissible-rep multiplicity table, and the breathing singlet rides on a loop coefficient $c_{\rm loop}$ whose two computation routes disagree, so whether the critical point is a true minimum, a saddle, or has flat directions is genuinely open.

2. What is missing — the precise objects

Location is proven; stability is what is missing. The criticality leg is a real, target-blind result. For any $S_3$-invariant functional $V$ on the three Cartan radii $(u_1,u_2,u_3)$, $$ \nabla V\big|_{(t,t,t)} = 0 , $$ fixed by symmetry independent of the form of $V$ — and it carries zero stability content. The perturbation space splits exactly by $S_3$ into a breathing/volume singlet and a traceless shape doublet, $$ 3 = \mathbf{1} \oplus \mathbf{2} , $$ and the symmetric point is a minimum iff both eigenvalues are positive. The two eigenvalue sectors are the two missing objects.

(M1) The shape-doublet Hessian sign — the admissible-rep multiplicity table. Along the shape ray $u=(1+\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon,1)$ on $SU(3)/T^2$ the raw curvature is $$ \left.\frac{d^2R}{d\varepsilon^2}\right|_0 = +4 - 5 = -1 , $$ (unit-normalized $+2-5/2=-1/2$) — diagonal convexity $+4$ versus the structure-constant triangle $-5$, a sign-definite, normalization-invariant result reproduced symbolically and cross-confirmed in both the SG-6 and UQF-10 ledgers. This $-1$ is the raw 3-space Hessian of $+R$ along a volume-contaminated ray ($\mathrm{Vol}=1-\varepsilon^2$) at a non-critical point, since $\nabla R(1,1,1)=(-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12)\neq 0$; the full $3\times3$ Hessian of $R$ has eigenvalues $\{+1,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12\}$. Once the doublet is properly isolated (orthonormal trace-free log basis, fixed volume, shape-critical) it is an $S_3$ irrep, so its Hessian is isotropic ($\propto I$) and a saddle is structurally impossible at the symmetric point. The physical mass$^2$ is $M=G^{-1}\,\mathrm{Hess}(V)$ and the verdict turns entirely on the Einstein-frame sign: $$ V=+R \Rightarrow \text{mass}^2\{-1,-1\}\ (\text{unstable}),\qquad V=-R \Rightarrow \text{mass}^2\{+1,+1\}\ (\text{stable}). $$ The frozen branch finds $V_{\rm phys}\sim -R_{K_6}$ target-blind, hence mass$^2\{+1,+1\}$ — leaning STABLE at tree level. What is missing to certify it is the graded Casimir net sign, the supertrace $\sum (-1)^F\,d\,n\,(w_1^2+w_2^2)$ over the admissible-rep multiplicity table — presently ABSENT — with a regularization-stable $s=-1$ zeta continuation. The chiral index $\chi=-3$ opposes the boson-dominance rescue, so no rescue is banked. The closed-form multiplicity $m_0(p,q)=\min(p,q)+1$ if $(p-q)\equiv 0\bmod 3$ else $0$ checks against the textbook values; the full table and the regularized zeta remain.

(M2) The breathing-singlet loop coefficient $c_{\rm loop}$ — sign and value. The single-$\sigma$ stabilization object is the four-term potential $$ V(\sigma)=c_{\rm KK}\,e^{-4\sigma}+c_{\rm bdry}\,e^{-2\sigma}+c_{\rm Wilson}\cos\theta_W\,e^{-4\sigma}+c_{\rm loop}\,e^{-6\sigma}. $$ Three coefficients are computed and banked — $c_{\rm KK}=-8.892\times10^{1}/R_Y^4$, the fold-winding Casimir $c_{\rm KK}^{\rm wind}=+3.701826\times10^{-2}\,R_Y^{-4}$, $c_{\rm Wilson}$ integer sums $S(0)=+4,\ S(\pi)=+92$, branch pin $\kappa_0'=-3/(64\pi^6)<0$, $c_{\rm bdry}=-1.08\times10^{-2}$ — but none is combined into $c_{\rm loop}$ (a forbidden shortcut). The missing $c_{\rm loop}$ is the $e^{-6\sigma}$ coefficient, the log-scheme residue of $c_{\rm loop}=\mathrm{tr}[a_6]$, the Seeley–DeWitt coefficient of the $\sigma$-fluctuation determinant on $K_6$. Its threshold-resolved FRG supertrace form is $$ c_{\rm loop}=\frac{1}{4(4\pi)^2}\sum_{\rm levels}\Big[\deg_B(\ell)\,l^0_B\!\big(m_B^2(\ell)/k^2\big)-\deg_F(\ell)\,l^0_F\!\big(m_F^2(\ell)/k^2\big)\Big], $$ with the normalization $1/(4(4\pi)^2)$ fixed. The object is route-INCONSISTENT — a stronger blocker than uncomputed.   • The "no competitor anywhere" version is dissolved, not a wall. "No framework could ever deliver a controlled global positive-definite moduli mass matrix" is an unprovable universal negative — a non-target.   • Existence is not stability, and $c_{\rm loop}$ is not the UV completion. A finite $a_6$ supports a bounded coefficient; it does not complete quantum gravity (that is the shared W1 / UQF-9 wall).

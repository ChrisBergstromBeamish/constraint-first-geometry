What is missing

The frozen split offers two candidate inflaton directions $\{\rho,\sigma\}$ — the geometry's own moduli, read off the split, not posited. Two genuine, target-blind narrowing wins are banked: the $\rho$ direction is computed DEAD (it carries an effective mass at the Kaluza–Klein scale, far above the slow-roll scale, so it physically cannot inflate), and the surviving breathing modulus $\sigma$ supports an exponential plateau whose tilt direction — a red tilt, $n_s<1$ — is geometry-fixed given the split. The local narrowing is

$$O_{\rm Gap08,local}(\text{split}) = \big(\rho\ \text{DEAD},\ \lambda^2 = \tfrac16\big),$$

closed only as narrowing given the split (qualitative kill + theorem-candidate slope). What is missing is everything past those two moves. The precise missing objects are:

  • The $\lambda^2=1/6$ forcing theorem (vs candidate). For the $\sigma$ exponential plateau the slope is

$$\lambda^2 = \frac{4}{K_{\sigma\sigma}},\qquad \text{(claimed)}\ K_{\sigma\sigma}=24 \ \Longrightarrow\ \lambda^2 = \frac{4}{24} = \frac16 .$$

The claim is checked at three layers and fails closed at the third. The arithmetic layer ($\lambda^2=1/6 \Leftrightarrow K_{\sigma\sigma}=24$) is exact bookkeeping — algebra, not physics. The plateau-phenomenology layer is a sanity check: a plateau gives $n_s\approx 1-2/N_*$, so at $N_*\approx 55$, $n_s\approx 1-2/55\approx 0.9636$ — the same Planck-dead-center region — not a derivation. The geometry layer is the blocker: that $K_{\sigma\sigma}=24$ is forced by the split rather than chosen requires tracing the frozen $\sigma$-kinetic reduction source (the D.1 region of the frozen branch), which is un-traced. Until it is traced, $\lambda^2=1/6$ stays a THEOREM-CANDIDATE, not a banked derivation. A distinct-quantity caution must also be discharged, not assumed away: $K_{\sigma\sigma}=24$ (slope) and the normalization $K=99/11=9$ are different objects, and the bridge $24/9=8/3$ is a named, un-banked factor.

  • The $81/121$ normalization source-trace. The frozen K13-2 datum is $81/22$, source-confirmed in the frozen branch, with re-verified exact arithmetic

$$\frac{81}{22}=\Big(\frac{11}{2}\Big)\cdot\Big(\frac{9}{11}\Big)^2 = \frac{9^2}{2\cdot 11}.$$

The $\sigma$ magnitude does not reduce — it RELOCATES onto two freedoms, not one. The $(11/2)$ is the raw denominator ratio $(D-2)/(D_4-2)=11/2$ (the parity bit III-a, a declared choice retaining the $13\mathrm{D}$ denominator $11$ over the textbook $4\mathrm{D}$ value $2$); the extra factor $(9/11)^2=81/121$ (the residual III-b) is named by neither convention axis, and no source for it in context is on record. III-b is the named posit AXIOM-K13-2-RESIDUAL-NORMALIZATION-FACTOR, carried AXIOM-OPEN / REDUCE_FURTHER with an open source-trace. It demotes to a derived consequence of the SHAPE-SPLIT only if an external, target-blind trace of the frozen branch shows it follows from $n=(6,2,1)$.

  • The magnitude / amplitude residuals (downstream, not closed here). The absolute amplitude $A_s$ is BLOCKED on two named inputs: the operative convention branch fixed by the frozen $\sigma$-map data (B3, pending), and the loop coefficient $c_{\rm loop}$ (the $\sigma^{-6}$ term, an FRG-2 computation inherited from Gap-04, exported not re-derived). A tilt plus a ratio is not a spectrum: the standing non-claim is that no absolute amplitude is claimed for this gate. The e-fold window $N_*$ is OPEN / NOT STATED, set downstream by the Gap-09 reheating history.

The closed gravitational sub-win (for contrast). The coupling that anchors $A_s$ is closed-by-computation — pure geometry, no dial, with a $\pm$factor-2 fold band:

$$\kappa_{13}^2 = \frac{1}{\pi^4\, M_U^9\, M_{\rm Pl}^2}\qquad\text{(closed form, frozen radii).}$$

This is a genuine DERIVED-CLOSED sub-win, but it does not give the absolute $A_s$ alone — that still rides B3 and $c_{\rm loop}$. A scoping fact carried verbatim: with $c_{\rm bdry}$ negative across its band, the plateau well is unconditional in $c_{\rm loop}$, so $c_{\rm loop}$ cannot threaten the well and does not enter $n_s$, $r$, or the falsifier — its role is confined to $A_s$.

The structural diagnostic (why the result is specific, not trivial). For an exponential plateau the tilt is dominated by the $-2/N_*$ term and only weakly sensitive to $\lambda^2$, which is exactly why $n_s$ is recorded as near-universal across branches ($n_s\in[0.9643,0.9679]$, Planck-dead-center) while the tensor ratio $r$ spans $[3.5,36]\times10^{-3}$ — a factor of $\sim10$ — across the four named convention branches (T6 / T_u / D6 / D_u). That $n_s$ stays pinned while $r$ fans out by an order of magnitude is what makes the structure a real geometric datum rather than a convention-driven artifact: the direction is forced; the magnitude relocates.

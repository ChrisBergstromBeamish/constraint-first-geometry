The black-hole microstate / Page-curve wall

Kind: PER-GATE-BOUNDED (conditional on the keystone W3). A finite, owned work-package: a geometry-native microstate-counting theorem $\log N(A)=A/4G+\text{subleading}$ for the frozen $4$D horizon, plus a target-blind Page-turnover computation — both gated behind one named, shared heat-kernel computation. It is not a closed gate, and it is not a UV completion.

The terminal-anchored principle. Anchored $\neq$ closed. The owning gate (Gap-13) is ANCHOR-CLOSED — every horizon object it touches has a status, an allowed claim, and a forbidden claim — yet it is OPEN-ANCHORED: a real, finite, falsifiable computation remains, and no gate here is called physics-CLOSED.

1. What the wall is, in one honest sentence

The frozen geometry reproduces the Bekenstein–Hawking value $S=A/4G$ to $2.8\times10^{-5}$ relative error as a labeled inheritance diagnostic, and carries a genuinely non-tautological conditional route to the coefficient $1/4$ and an explicit island construction — but it does not count a single microstate, does not derive $1/4$ as an output, and does not produce a quantitative Page curve, because all three ride on one unbuilt horizon-capable saddle and one uncomputed defect heat-kernel coefficient.

2. What is missing — the precise objects

A candidate quantum theory of gravity owes two things at a horizon: the static coefficient (the microstate count behind $S=A/4G$) and the dynamical turnover (the Page curve). Here the value-match is banked, but the derivations are not.

(M0) What is genuinely in hand — the diagnostic and the conditional, so the wall is not mistaken for empty. On the $4$D zero-mode sector of the frozen branch the Bekenstein–Hawking value is recovered as an inherited consistency check,

$$ \left|\,S_{\rm inherited}-\frac{A}{4G}\,\right|\Big/\frac{A}{4G}\;=\;2.8\times10^{-5}\qquad(\text{DIAGNOSTIC; inherited, not derived ab initio}), $$

and a rigorous conditional implication carries the coefficient under four value-free hypotheses with no $1/4$ baked into any of them,

$$ (H1\wedge H2\wedge H3\wedge H4)\ \Rightarrow\ S=\frac{A_H}{4G}. $$

The algebraic core is exact and independently re-verified, in two conventions. With $\ln Z=-I$, $E=\partial_\beta I$, $S=(\beta\partial_\beta-1)I$ and the on-shell Euclidean Schwarzschild action $I(\beta)=\beta^2/16\pi G$, at $\beta_H=8\pi GM$ one gets $S=4\pi GM^2$, and with $A_H=16\pi G^2M^2$ this is $A_H/4G$. In the conical-defect convention the replica geometry $M_n$ carries the distributional tip curvature $\int_{M_n}\!\sqrt{g}\,R=n\int_{M_1}\!\sqrt{g}\,R+4\pi(1-n)A_H+O((1-n)^2)$, giving $I_n=nI_1-(1-n)A_H/4G$ and $S=(n\partial_n-1)I_n|_{n=1}=A_H/4G$, with the decomposition

$$ \frac14=\underbrace{\frac{4\pi}{16\pi G}\cdot G}_{(\text{conical-tip solid angle})\,\times\,(\text{EH normalization})^{-1}\,\times\,(G\ \text{measured anchor})} . $$

No matter loop, no UV cutoff $\epsilon$, no entanglement-cutoff matching enters — the $16\pi G$ is fixed by Newton, the $4\pi$ by $2$D cone topology. What is missing has three distinct parts.

(M1) The geometry-native microstate-counting theorem (the static leg). The owed object is

$$ \log N(A)=\frac{A}{4G}+(\text{known subleading}),\qquad\text{no fitted prefactor, freeze-before-compare clean,} $$

a count over the frozen near-horizon degrees of freedom. The $2.8\times10^{-5}$ value-match is explicitly not this theorem — it counts no microstates. The count is gated on W3 / Gap-01: without a UV-controlled near-horizon Hilbert space there is nothing well-defined to count, and the entanglement route to the count is dead (see M-DEAD below).

(M2) The horizon-capable Euclidean saddle (the prerequisite both legs ride on). The HORIZON-ADMISSIBILITY theorem: that the frozen $13$D zero-mode gravity sector extends from the perturbative graviton carrier to an admissible, dominant Euclidean black-hole replica saddle family $M_n$ under the frozen $C_{\rm admiss}/F^+$ grammar. This is the discharge of $H1$–$H4$ above, and it is OPEN / a global open problem — the single load-bearing object. It is the same shared frontier as the UV-completion wall W1. Within it, $H3$ and $H4$ collapse onto a single named computation: the order-$6$ mixed Neumann/Dirichlet $S^1_Y/\mathbb{Z}_2$-orbifold-boundary $+$ conical-defect Seeley–DeWitt coefficient, whose order-$A$ contribution must be shown to vanish for the frozen $K_6=SU(3)/T^2$, $\chi=-3$ internal weights. That defect/boundary coefficient is absent from the literature (the boundary heat-kernel tower stops at $a_5$) and is exactly the keystone wall W3 — counted once across Gap-01, the graviton certificate, UQF-3, UQF-9 and SG-6.

(M3) The Page-curve turnover and its one missing number. A spherical s-wave reduction to a $2$D dilaton-gravity throat (dilaton $\phi(r)=A(r)/4G$, matter $=c$ free $2$D fields coupled to a bath) extremizes the generalized entropy $S_{\rm gen}=\phi(\partial I)/4G+S_{\rm bulk}(R\cup I)$, and the quantum-extremal surface solves explicitly,

$$ x_\star=-\frac{r_h}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3Gc+9\pi\alpha\,r_h^2}}{6\sqrt{\pi\alpha}}\;;\qquad\text{semiclassical }(Gc\ll\alpha r_h^2):\ x_\star\sim\frac{Gc}{12\pi\alpha\,r_h}, $$

placing the island the textbook distance $\sim Gc/r_h$ outside the horizon, derived from frozen-geometry inputs (the area-term coefficient $\alpha$ kept symbolic and sourced from the conditional, never an assumed $A/4G$). The qualitative turnover survives scheme-independently, but the Page time $t_{\rm Page}\sim 6\,S_{\rm BH}/(c\kappa)$ is OPEN: the $2$D central charge $c$ has no target-blind frozen value — it is gauge-convention-dependent ($c\in\{26.5,50.5\}$), greybody/angular-momentum-dressed, and Hawking-temperature(mass)-dependent ($c\sim2$ at solar mass to $\sim50$ at Planckian), spanning factors $\sim2$–$25$. So the magnitude is unfixed; only the turnover's shape is established, and it is not phrased as a theorem of unitarity.

(M-DEAD) Why one route is excluded by theorem, not by choice. The entanglement route to $1/4$ is a tautology: the same heat-kernel counterterm renormalizes both $1/G$ and the entanglement-area term, so

$$ \frac{(\text{entanglement-area term})}{(\text{induced }1/G)}=\frac14\quad\text{for any minimal content — even a free scalar with no microstates.} $$

This is the true-by-construction signature; it carries zero independent evidential weight, and the strong scheme-independent form is refuted on two grounds (gauge-EE ambiguity is itself an area-coefficient ambiguity; the spin-$2$ mechanism is only proposed). The conditional route in (M0) is admissible precisely because it bypasses this counterterm — its coefficient could have come out wrong (no saddle, a shifted $G_{\rm eff}$, or a non-vanishing order-$A$ Wald term would all break it) and so it is non-tautological but unproven.

5. Falsifier

The wall carries no falsifier of its own yet, by design — a five-clause anti-overclaim wall (PC-1…PC-5) forbids registering a Page-curve claim until a real mechanism exists, so the program cannot be caught faking one. Two sharp tests arm the moment W3 / Gap-01 delivers the near-horizon Hilbert space and Gap-14 delivers a consistent split: (a) a geometry-native state count must give $\log N(A)=A/4G+$ known subleading with no fitted prefactor — a different coefficient refutes; (b) the radiation entropy $S_{\rm rad}(t)$ must turn over at the Page time — no turnover, or a Page time inconsistent with the count, refutes. A non-vanishing order-$A$ defect coefficient is the live falsifier-in-principle of the conditional $1/4$.

6. Anti-claims (what must never be said about this wall)

  • The value-match is not a microstate count. $S=A/4G$ to $2.8\times10^{-5}$ is a DIAGNOSTIC, inherited given $E$ and the measured $G$ — never the counting theorem (M1), and never evidence that $1/4$ is a derived output.
  • $1/4$ is not a derived output. It is DERIVED-GIVEN-E conditional on the unproven HORIZON-ADMISSIBILITY saddle plus the value-free AX-SADDLE-ENTROPY posit. ANCHORED $\neq$ DERIVED: $1/4$ is anchor-transferred onto the measured $G$, never anchor-eliminated; floor $\geq1$.
  • The entanglement route to $1/4$ is a tautology, returning the target for any minimal content (even a microstate-free free scalar) and carrying zero independent evidential weight; the conditional route is admissible only because it never touches that counterterm.
  • No quantitative Page curve is claimed. Only a qualitative, area-dominated turnover survives; the Page time is open, and the turnover is not a theorem of unitarity.
  • The two horizon area laws are unrelated. Borrowing $1/4$ from the framework's $SU(3)_c$ confinement Wilson-loop area law would be target-loaded; that firewall stays in force.
  • This wall is not a UV completion (W1), and consuming the keystone (W3) does not close it — a count and a mechanism are still owed after the coefficient lands.
  • The frozen-branch hashes (dcc66f1b2685 / a5b1e6f9d951) are audit anchors; they certify which object was tested, not the physics — and no horizon-sector object has any hash at all.
  • The unicorn is dissolved, not listed as a wall. "No simpler horizon-capable formulation could ever exist," or "THE universal microstate count for any realistic gravity theory," is a universal negative — unprovable in principle for any theory. The bounded, in-grammar object (a geometry-native count and turnover for this geometry) is the real wall.

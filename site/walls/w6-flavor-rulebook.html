What is missing

$$\frac{m_t}{m_c}=\frac{m_c}{m_u}=\kappa^{-1}=e^{\pi\sqrt3}\approx 231,\qquad \frac{m_b}{m_s}=\frac{m_s}{m_d}=\kappa^{-2/3}=e^{2\pi\sqrt3/3}\approx 38.5.$$

All CKM and PMNS mixing magnitudes, both CP phases, and the Jarlskog $J$ then follow from a single chamber angle $\theta_F$ by diagonalizing frozen operators ($V_{\rm CKM}=U_u^\dagger U_d$, $U_{\rm PMNS}=U_e^\dagger U_\nu$), with no entry to dial. That shape leg satisfies

$$O_{\rm SG8,shape}(E_{\rm frozen}) = 0 \qquad\text{(status: DERIVED-GIVEN-E — for the ratios / mixings / phases only).}$$

What is missing is everything that needs a scale, plus the hardening of the chamber itself. The full gate obstruction carries scale pieces the shape leg does not close:

$$O_{\rm SG8}(E)=\big(O_{\rm shape}(E),\,O_{N_d,N_e,N_\nu}(E),\,O_{M_R}(E)\big),\qquad O_{\rm SG8}(E_{\rm frozen})\ne 0\ \text{(asserted).}$$

Closing the wall means hardening $F^+$ — including target-blind lex-min of the ladders over the full root space, and a uniqueness proof for $\tau=\omega$ — into a reviewable certificate.

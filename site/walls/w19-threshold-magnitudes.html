The threshold-magnitudes wall

Kind: PER-GATE-BOUNDED (a finite, owned computation-debt). One honest sentence: on the frozen $13\mathrm{D}$ branch the signs of the gauge-coupling threshold corrections are recovered from the geometry given the observed Standard-Model content and the measured couplings — gauge+ghost negative, matter and the hypercharge $\sum Y^2$ term positive — but the magnitudes of the threshold rows are demonstrated to be fitted/injected rather than derived (the published per-row formulas miss the printed rows by $\sim 2\times$ to $\sim 18\times$, and a target-blind recompute disagrees with the printed triple on all three components), so the gate is anchored and diagnostic, never closed, and never claims the couplings are predicted to unify.

This page presents the wall under the terminal-anchored principle: anchored $\neq$ closed. The gate that owns it, SG-7, is ANCHOR-CLOSED / WALL-RECORDED and carries one genuine bankable win — the threshold signs — but it is physics-status OPEN / Diagnostic-only and is never called CLOSED. The wall is a bounded, in-grammar computation-debt with named residuals; its absolute form — "this spectrum is the unique unifier across all mathematics" — is an unprovable universal negative, the dissolved unicorn, and is never the wall.

What is missing

One leg is genuinely strong and worth stating exactly before naming what is missing. Given the selected geometry and the observed spectrum $E$, the finite threshold vector is defined by the surviving heat-kernel remainder at the compactification scale,

$$\delta_i=\frac{1}{2\pi}\,\Delta_i^{\rm finite},$$

and its sign structure is recovered from the content and the measured couplings:

$$\operatorname{sign}\big(\delta_i^{\rm gauge+ghost}\big)=-,\qquad \operatorname{sign}\big(\delta_i^{\rm matter},\,\delta_1^{\rm hyper}\big)=+ .$$

This is not a sign convention. The hypercharge term is driven by a non-zero quadratic invariant,

$$\sum_f (\text{mult})\,Y_f^2=\frac{10}{3}\neq 0\quad(\text{per generation}),$$

which is exactly what forces $\delta_1>0$, while gauge+ghost contributions carry the asymptotic-freedom (negative) sign. The signs terminate on the observed content $E$ and the measured $\alpha_i(M_Z)$ — generic heat-kernel physics that survives even if the specific compactification were wrong. The signs are DERIVED-GIVEN-E.

What is missing is the magnitude of every threshold row. The chain invariant $\to$ row $\to$ column-sum is broken at the middle link. The column sums are trivially right — summing the eight printed rows reproduces the printed triple to four decimals — but this is a tautology about injected rows, not a regeneration:

$$\delta_1\to+4.8424,\qquad \delta_2\to-3.1112,\qquad \delta_3\to-1.7313\quad(\pm1.6\times10^{-3}).$$

What would close it — or why it is terminal

This is a bounded, owned computation-debt, not a unicorn. Each residual is a concrete, finite, target-blind work-package, and a refuting result is a valid close. The standing falsification test: a normalization reverse-engineered to hit the known $\delta$-triple is true-by-construction and merely relocates the fit from three numbers to one normalization — it does not close the wall.

  • R2 / threshold magnitudes (the dominant residual — do via the keystone). Because the trace must be non-separable (SG7-R2), this cannot resolve at fixed scheme until the shared route-inconsistency (W3) is reconciled once, target-blind — the fix then propagates to SG-6 and Gap-01 as well.
  • R1 / deep check. Co-package the byte-anchored frozen spectra with the compiler; re-run the deep check (not the re-sum) so each row is provably regenerated from frozen primitives; confirm the meta-hash recomputes to a5b1e6f9d951.   • R5 / the $M_U$ seam. Trace the $m_c/M_U$ ratio in the harness. Why the gate stays terminal even on full success. Two claims are dissolved unicorns — never open weaknesses. First, spectrum uniqueness: "no other spectrum unifies" is a universal negative over an open-ended domain, unprovable in principle for any framework, and rivals tie here — the threshold pass is a consistency filter on the already-selected survivor, not a selector. Second, even a fully derived magnitude is derived given $E$: selection $\neq$ derivation, given-$E$ $\neq$ derivation-of-$E$. The bounded result — signs DERIVED-GIVEN-E, magnitudes either DERIVED-GIVEN-E or definitively Diagnostic-only — is the ceiling, and stating it precisely is the closure, not a hedge.

Falsifier

The wall carries a genuine, certificate-level, target-blind falsifier distinct from any fitted number: the target-blind compiler refuses to be fed the answer ($3/3$ anti-target-loading tests pass), so its output is an honest reconstruction attempt. As armed today it already fires — the blind triple $(-63.897,+70.627,+227172.5)$ disagrees with the printed $(+4.8424,-3.1112,-1.7313)$ on sign and magnitude on all three components, demonstrating that the printed triple is injected/fitted. Once the log_mu placeholder is replaced by the true $\zeta$-regularized finite part and frozen before comparison, the same run becomes the falsifiable prediction of the magnitude claim: definite rows that miss the printed values refute the printed ledger and pin the gate to Diagnostic-only. The refuting object for the signs leg would be a recomputed sign structure on the frozen content that disagrees with gauge+ghost $-$ / matter+hyper $+$.

There is no "couplings unify" falsifier — by design. Fable does not predict the couplings to unify at $M_U$; $M_U$ is where the lines cross, and the unification residual $9.6\times10^{-11}$ is a self-consistency check on the printed numbers, not a measure of derivation.

Anti-claims (what must never be said about this wall)

  • Not "SG-7 is closed" / "a validated unification." The gate is OPEN / Diagnostic-only; it does not move on the strength of the sign-recovery or the re-sum.
  • Not "the threshold magnitudes are derived." The target-blind run demonstrates the printed $\delta$-triple is injected/fitted — wrong sign on all three, $\sim 10^5$ miss on $\delta_3$. The magnitudes are SCHEME-anchored, terminating on no measured invariant today.
  • Not "no row is a free parameter." That strong claim was withdrawn; the No-Hidden-Knob discipline proves enumeration, not forcedness.
  • Not "the couplings are predicted to unify at $M_U\sim10^{16}\,\mathrm{GeV}$." $M_U$ is a declared closure-target convention; the honest verb is "couplings CROSS at $M_U$."
  • Not "the trivial re-sum is a closure." $\sum_{\rm rows}=\delta$ is a tautology about injected rows — exactly, and only, what the captured log verifies.
  • Not "the blind FINDING falsifies the geometry." The finite-part is a placeholder; it demonstrates that the obvious reconstruction fails, not that the geometry is wrong — the $\delta_3$ blow-up is partly a placeholder artifact.
  • Not "SG-7 derives $E$" or "the $b_i$/$M_Z$ are SG-7 outputs." They are given-$E$ / measured inputs.
  • Not "the frozen-branch hashes validate the physics." dcc66f1b2685 / a5b1e6f9d951 are audit anchors certifying which object was tested; they do not validate the physics.
  • Not "consistency is uniqueness." Passing the threshold consistency pass does not single out this spectrum as THE unifier — that universal negative is the dissolved unicorn, and rivals tie.

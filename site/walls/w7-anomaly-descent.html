$$ O_{\rm pert}(E_{\rm frozen})=\big(A_{Y^3},\,A_{{\rm grav}\,Y},\,A_{SU2},\,A_{SU3{\rm sq}},\,A_{SU3{\rm cube}},\,A_{\rm Witten}\big)(E_{\rm frozen})=0, $$

6. Anti-claims (what must never be said about this wall)

  • Anomaly cancellation is a filter, not a selector. $E\mapsto O_{\rm pert}(E)$ is a fixed multilinear functional whose kernel is infinite-dimensional — any vector-like pair $R\oplus\bar R$ leaves every entry fixed — so anomaly-freedom admits infinitely many spectra and cannot single out the Standard Model. Formally $E_{\rm frozen}\in\ker O_{\rm pert}$, never $\ker O_{\rm pert}=\{E_{\rm SM}\}$.
  • Naming the single class is not evaluating it. The Anderson-dual structural compression is real; it is not a closure.
  • The descent class is NOT assumed to vanish. "$[\omega]_{\rm lifted}=0$" is the withdrawn, not-revived candidate; assuming it would be tuning toward the answer and is refused everywhere. The gate has no number to hit — the only "value" is zero, and zero is exactly what is refused.
  • $\xi$ is specified, not proven to exist. OPEN-1 is BLOCKED; $d=4$ vanishing is not claimed (FOS Cor 7.5 proves only $d\le 3$; Cor 7.6 fails here).
  • Quantum nilpotency is NOT verified — only the classical $s^2=0$ is. The order-$\hbar$ piece is a facet of the same open class.
  • The twice-confirmed classical chiral index does not settle the quantum spectrum. APS $(+3,0)$ and BWB $|\mathrm{index}|=3$ are firewalled, zero-mode scope, and are reproduction of one invariant, not two independent anchors.
  • Topology cannot decide the mirror question. A light vectorlike mirror has $n_L-n_R=0$ and is null in every gauge/gravitational/global/spin$^c$/cobordism invariant; the deciding datum is dynamical (symmetric mass generation), not topological.
  • A chosen regulator that makes the descent cancel REFUTES the row — it is a hidden knob, not a closure.
  • The frozen-branch hashes (dcc66f1b2685 / a5b1e6f9d951) are audit anchors; they certify which object was tested, not the physics.
  • Local closure is not whole-gate closure. $O_{\rm pert}(E_{\rm frozen})=0$ does not imply $O_{\rm UQF4}(E_{\rm frozen})=0$. And even a fully clean UQF-4 is an anomaly-consistency leg only — no Osterwalder–Schrader/Wightman existence, no spectral gap — so it cannot touch the Yang–Mills mass gap.

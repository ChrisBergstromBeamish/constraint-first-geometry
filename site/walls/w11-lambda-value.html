The Λ-value wall (measured anchor + R-uniqueness)

Kind: AXIOM/ANCHOR + PER-GATE-BOUNDED. One honest sentence: the value of the cosmological constant $\Lambda$ is the program's fifth and final measured anchor — a single directly-measured input the frozen geometry produces no internal competitor for, terminal as an anchor and never derived — while the only genuinely open, bounded, in-grammar move in its neighborhood is the structural question of whether freezing the internal volume mode is the unique minimal trace-decoupling modification of gravity.

This page presents the wall under the terminal-anchored principle: anchored $\neq$ closed. The gate that owns it, Gap-05-value, is ANCHOR-CLOSED — every object it touches has a status, an allowed claim, and a forbidden claim — yet it rolls up OPEN, governed by its least-closed residual. The value-leg is the floor every honest theory must pay, not a closure; the R-uniqueness sub-target is bounded and falsifiable; the absolute "$\Lambda$ is derived / provably irreducible" statement is a dissolved unicorn, never the wall.

1. What the wall is, in one honest sentence

The frozen $13$D geometry produces no $\Lambda$ to fit, so the comparison set against the observed $(2.3\ \text{meV})^4$ is empty by construction; the value enters the ledger as a measured fifth anchor (row 5, alongside $M_{\rm Pl}$, $\alpha_i$, $y_t$, $|V_{us}|$), the most promising in-framework route to a reduction was computed-refuted, and every known "explanation" of the value relocates the number $1{:}1$ rather than reducing it — leaving the value terminal-as-anchor and exactly one bounded structural residual: is volume-mode freezing the unique minimal trace-decoupling change?

2. What is missing — the precise objects

There are two distinct things here, and they must not be merged. The value is not a missing object — it is a present, measured anchor. What is missing is a structural uniqueness theorem that lives on the neighbor mechanisms, not on the value.

(V) The value is a measured anchor, not a hole. The framework declares exactly $N=5$ "just-is" inputs; $\Lambda$ is row 5:

$$\Lambda = (2.3\ \text{meV})^4 \approx 1\times10^{-122}\,M_{\rm Pl}^4 .$$

Two structural facts force the honest grade. First, the other four rows are consumed against internal structure, but $\Lambda$ has nothing internal to be consumed against — the no-target-loading guarantee is the gate's one closed local leg,

$$\mathcal C_{\rm Gap05}(E_{\rm frozen}) = \varnothing \qquad\text{(status: DERIVED-GIVEN-E),}$$

i.e. the set of structure-side quantities comparable to the observed value is empty, so there is structurally nothing to tune. Second, $\Lambda$ enters only as the dimensionless ratio $\Lambda/M_{\rm Pl}^4$, anchored against the one dimensionful row $M_{\rm Pl}$, never terminal as its own output. The value-leg may therefore anchor but may not close.

(R) The one missing structural object — R-uniqueness. It is not established whether volume-mode freezing (the unimodular trace-decoupling premise) is the unique minimal trace-decoupling modification of gravity. Until then the separating premise stays NON-ATOMIC. Precisely: over a declared candidate space of minimal gravity-coupling modifications — unimodular / trace-free / sequestering / Gauss–Bonnet-augmented — is volume-mode freezing the unique minimal change that leaves local general relativity intact? The decisive relocation fact is that even the heaviest available construction (the Kaloper–Padilla augmented sequestering action) leaves a residual $\Delta\Lambda$ that is, in its authors' own words, "radiatively stable, albeit with a value that is incalculable and should be set by measurement (just like the electron mass)" — a verified $1{:}1$ relocation. So R-uniqueness, even if proven, would upgrade the premise from "conjecture" to "forced-but-unmeasured"; it would not make the value derived.

Scope, stated sharply. R-uniqueness supports the companion mechanism/dissolution rows (Gap-05-stability, the $\Lambda$-catastrophe dissolution), not the value itself. The value stays a measured anchor regardless of how R-uniqueness resolves.

4. What would close it — or why it is terminal

The value is terminal — do not chase a value-derivation. The value-leg has no derivation to show, and that is the point. Its airtightness rests on three checkable structural facts, all DERIVED-GIVEN-E, none of which derives the value:

  1. No-target-loading guarantee — $\mathcal C_{\rm Gap05}(E_{\rm frozen})=\varnothing$: the frozen geometry produces no $\Lambda$, so "we promise we didn't tune" becomes "there is structurally nothing to tune."
  2. The granularity negative control, tripped by $\sim 113$ orders of magnitude — the program's deepest reduction lever (granularity) dissolves continuum/divergent walls; run as a deliberate control on the value, a finite supertrace at the natural granular scale $M_{\rm cutoff}=1/R_0$ gives $\rho_{\rm vac}^{\rm gran}\sim M_{\rm cutoff}^4=(1/R_0)^4$, landing $\sim 113$ OOM from $(2.3\ \text{meV})^4$ and predicting no value. $\Lambda$ is a finite wall; finite walls survive granularity. This is the method correctly reporting "does not apply" — a specificity diagnostic, not a failure to dissolve.
  3. The chamber-cancellation no-go ($\Lambda$-R1) — the only positive in-framework idea, a sign-graded "chamber" sum over $K_6=SU(3)/T^2$, is computed-refuted target-blind: a sign-grading cancels a sum only if it acts on labels distinguishing terms, but the vacuum-energy operator is the identity / unit operator, grading-even and label-blind. The supertrace witness is honest-FAIL — ratio $0.58$ at $k=0$, $1.000$ at $k=1\text{–}8$. Status THEOREM_REFUTED, established at full strength.

The honest endpoint for the value is therefore MEASURED ANCHOR · terminal-as-anchor, never DISSOLVED or DERIVED.

R-uniqueness is bounded — here is the concrete target-blind program. Over a declared candidate space of minimal gravity-coupling modifications (unimodular / trace-free / sequestering / Gauss–Bonnet-augmented / …), prove or refute that volume-mode freezing is the unique minimal trace-decoupling change leaving local GR intact.

  • Inputs: the trace-free Einstein equation; unimodular gravity; the Kaloper–Padilla augmented action (arXiv:1606.04958). The candidate-space enumeration must be architecture-neutral.
  • Success criterion (write the minimality metric before checking which wins): if proven $\to$ a named conditional theorem (the premise becomes atomic-given-the-candidate-space). The trap is a circular definition of "minimal" that picks out volume-mode freezing by construction — the candidate space and the metric must be fixed first.
  • What a refuting result looks like (also a valid close): volume-mode freezing is one of many routes, and the separating premise stays explicitly NON-ATOMIC. A refutation is as useful as a proof.

Either outcome upgrades the premise's atomicity, which is what the catastrophe dissolution and the all-orders sequestering both wait on. Neither outcome moves the value-leg's grade — the value stays a measured anchor.

5. Falsifier

The value-leg carries no live falsifier of its own: a measured anchor is not the kind of object that can be refuted by computation, only superseded by a better measurement or by a future derivation that the program does not claim to have. The standing honest form is earned-irreducible under all known reductions, never provably so — Weinberg-open, measured against the best published no-go, Weinberg (1989), Rev. Mod. Phys. 61, 1, which forecloses the easy routes to a naturally small $\Lambda$.

The R-uniqueness sub-target is, by contrast, pre-registered as a two-sided bounded test: a target-blind enumeration over the declared candidate space either elevates volume-mode freezing to a unique-minimal conditional theorem or demotes it to one-of-many. The minimality metric must be written before the check; a refutation is a fully valid result. There is no preferred outcome and nothing is tuned to the data.

6. Anti-claims (what must never be said about this wall)

  • $\Lambda$ is not derived, predicted, or explained. There is no model value and no internal comparator: $\mathcal C_{\rm Gap05}(E_{\rm frozen})=\varnothing$.
  • The measured value does not close Gap-05. An anchor terminates itself, not its gate; the roll-up is OPEN. Using a claimed-output as a measured anchor to close its own gate is the cardinal sin — refused here.
  • Granularity does not dissolve the value the way it dissolves continuum walls — the $\sim 113$-OOM control proves the opposite — and that datum may not be promoted into "no reduction is possible" (a universal negative).
  • $\Lambda$ is not provably irreducible. "No competitor anywhere / unique across all mathematics / no future theory could do better" is the dissolved unicorn — a universal negative, unprovable in principle, never a wall. The honest form is earned-irreducible-under-all-known-reductions (Weinberg-open).
  • The chamber-cancellation route does not derive $\Lambda$ — it is computed-refuted ($\Lambda$-R1, unit-operator obstruction).
  • R-uniqueness, if proven, does not make the value derived. At most it upgrades the separating premise's atomicity; the value stays $1{:}1$-relocated and anchored.
  • Relocation is not reduction. Unimodular gravity, vacuum sequestering, quintessence, and the anthropic/Weinberg bound each relocate the number $1{:}1$ (or restate it as environmental selection); none reduces it.
  • The "an integration constant has no beta function" slogan is true-but-irrelevant — there is no running coupling, but the boundary value that replaces it shifts additively ($\Lambda_0\to\Lambda_0+\delta V$); it must not be re-leaned on.
  • The value is not a pristine T1 invariant — it is filed T3 (ΛCDM-laden) pending its co-consumption ledger ($H_0$, $\rho_{\rm crit}$, the ΛCDM extraction assumptions).
  • The frozen-branch hashes (dcc66f1b2685 / a5b1e6f9d951) are audit anchors certifying which object was tested; they do not validate the physics.

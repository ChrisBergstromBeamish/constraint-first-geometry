2. What is missing — the precise objects

For a Laplace-type operator $D$ on a $d$-manifold the heat trace organizes as

$$ \mathrm{Tr}\,e^{-tD}\;\sim\;(4\pi t)^{-d/2}\sum_{k\ge 0}t^{k}\!\int\!\sqrt{g}\;\mathrm{tr}_V\!\big[a_{2k}(x)\big],\qquad t\to 0^+, $$

and $a_6$ is the $k=3$, mass-dimension-$6$, cubic-curvature term. The physical object is the de-Donder (harmonic-gauge, Lichnerowicz) graviton with the Faddeev–Popov vector ghost subtracted,

$$ a_6^{\rm phys}\;=\;a_6[\text{grav}]\;-\;2\,a_6[\text{ghost}]\;+\;0, \qquad \dim_{\rm fibre}\mathrm{Sym}^2(T)=91,\quad \dim_{\rm fibre}T=13\ (\text{in }13\text{D}), $$

the $-2$ sign and multiplicity forced by BRST nilpotency. What is genuinely in hand (the scale-free win): the dimensionless content is forced by Gilkey invariance theory and dual-validated target-blind on four exact-rational sphere cross-checks,

$$ a_6(S^2)=\tfrac{4}{315},\quad a_6(S^4)=\tfrac{74}{63},\quad a_6(S^6)=\tfrac{1139}{63},\quad a_6^{\rm conf}(S^6)=\tfrac{5}{63}, $$

agreeing between a Gilkey-contraction route and an independent spectral-peel route to relative error $0$ to $\sim 4\times 10^{-14}$. The normalization-robust ratios such as $a_4/a_2^2=66/125$ are exact because scalars are Levi-Civita–immune. What is missing has three distinct parts:

$$ \text{vector } a_4 \text{ on } K_6:\quad \tfrac{23}{10}\ \to\ \tfrac{281}{120},\qquad \tfrac{281}{120}-\tfrac{23}{10}=\tfrac{1}{24}\ \text{(exact)}, $$

with the graviton $\mathrm{Sym}^2(T)$ analogue an exact gap of $2/21$. The two graded routes therefore disagree, in the physical (Lichnerowicz $E=-\mathrm{Ric}$) ghost sector by

$$ \Big|{-\tfrac{251}{504}}-\tfrac{149}{1008}\Big|=\tfrac{31}{48}\approx 0.646, $$

roughly six orders of magnitude outside the pre-registered $10^{-6}$ tolerance. The earned partial is the Bochner ($E=0$) ghost $a_6/a_0=149/1008=-\tfrac{16}{315}+\tfrac{143}{720}$ — which is not the physical Faddeev–Popov ghost and does not reconcile the routes. The precise missing object is the $SU(3)$ Gelfand–Tsetlin off-diagonal matrix elements that gate the Levi-Civita Lichnerowicz hopping on Peter–Weyl harmonic sections.

The corrected value $23/75$ is DERIVED / proved (target-blind, Bianchi-exact); applying the sign-flip in the read-only engine is owed, not done, so this leg is DISCLOSED-CORRECTED / computation-debt, not CLOSED. Correcting an input is necessary, never gate-closing.

(M3) The positivity functional. The criterion $P(\mathrm{tr}[a_6])\ge 0$ (equivalently $\Pi(a_6)$) that would let $a_6$ certify one-loop consistency has no valid realization on this off-shell, odd-$D$ branch (four sign-free reasons: off-shell gauge dependence; an odd-$D$ predicate; an object/predicate mismatch; no admissible input). No positivity sign is asserted.

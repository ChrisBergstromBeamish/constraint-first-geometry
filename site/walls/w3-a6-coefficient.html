For a Laplace-type operator $D$ on a $d$-manifold the heat trace organizes as

$$ \mathrm{Tr}\,e^{-tD}\;\sim\;(4\pi t)^{-d/2}\sum_{k\ge 0}t^{k}\!\int\!\sqrt{g}\;\mathrm{tr}_V\!\big[a_{2k}(x)\big],\qquad t\to 0^+, $$

and $a_6$ is the $k=3$, mass-dimension-$6$, cubic-curvature term. The physical object is the de-Donder (harmonic-gauge, Lichnerowicz) graviton with the Faddeev–Popov vector ghost subtracted,

$$ a_6^{\rm phys}\;=\;a_6[\text{grav}]\;-\;2\,a_6[\text{ghost}]\;+\;0, \qquad \dim_{\rm fibre}\mathrm{Sym}^2(T)=91,\quad \dim_{\rm fibre}T=13\ (\text{in }13\text{D}), $$

the $-2$ sign and multiplicity forced by BRST nilpotency. What is genuinely in hand (the scale-free win): the dimensionless content is forced by Gilkey invariance theory and dual-validated target-blind on four exact-rational sphere cross-checks,

$$ a_6(S^2)=\tfrac{4}{315},\quad a_6(S^4)=\tfrac{74}{63},\quad a_6(S^6)=\tfrac{1139}{63},\quad a_6^{\rm conf}(S^6)=\tfrac{5}{63}, $$

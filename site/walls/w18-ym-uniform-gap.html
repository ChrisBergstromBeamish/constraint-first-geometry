1. What is missing

The precise missing object is one inequality: a uniform-in-cutoff positive lower bound on the dimensionless mass gap of 4D pure $SU(3)$ Yang–Mills. On a Wilson lattice $\Lambda\subset(a\mathbb{Z})^4$ with action $S_W=\beta\sum_p(1-\tfrac13\,\mathrm{Re}\,\mathrm{tr}\,U_p)$, $\beta=6/g_0^2$, and the mass gap $\Delta(a,L)$ defined as the lowest nonzero eigenvalue of $H=-a^{-1}\log T$, the missing theorem is

$$\frac{\Delta(a,L)}{\Lambda_{\rm YM}}\;\ge\;c\;>\;0\quad\text{uniformly as }a\to0,\;L\to\infty,\;\beta\to\infty,$$

with the same constant $c$ for the same measure for which existence, reflection positivity, and nontriviality also hold in the limit.

The quantifier order is the whole difficulty. The statement requires $\exists(\delta,K,\kappa,a_0,L_0,n_0)\,\forall(a,L,\beta,n)$ — a single $\delta$ uniform over all regulators and all scales. No such uniform lower bound is known. As the gate's ledger puts it: at finite $a$ both the gap and reflection positivity are trivial — the entire wall IS the $a\to0$ limit. The lone inequality above is the entire wall.

What is already rigorous (and what is not). What is established is a conditional, not the gap. The all-operator lemma (SL-1 / M4D)

$$\big[\,H1\wedge H2\wedge H3\wedge H4\,\big]\;\Rightarrow\;\text{no soft physical sequence}\;\Rightarrow\;\Delta>0,$$

with $H4$ = uniform exponential clustering, holds and is hand-checkable; "uniform clustering $\Rightarrow$ the gap" is genuinely rigorous. It derives no value. The exact equivalence SL-0 ($\Delta>0 \equiv \operatorname{Spec}(H)\cap(0,\Delta)=\varnothing$) and the finite-$a$ floor SL-2 (RP $\Rightarrow 0\le T\le 1$, $H\ge 0$; measure existence and $\beta$-analyticity at fixed $a$) are likewise derived. The reduction chain SL-0 $\to$ SL-2 $\to$ SL-1/M4D is DERIVED / CERTIFICATE-CONDITIONAL — a conditional, never a gap. The bridge inequality is the one piece that turns the conditional into the theorem, and it is OPEN — Clay.

The decisive obstruction — rarity $\not\Rightarrow$ domination. In the deep UV the large-field weight $\mu_n\le e^{-c/g^2}\to0$ banks the inequality automatically. But rarity bounds a weight, not a contribution; the bare activity can compensate. Inside the $d=4$ marginal band $g(2^n a)=O(1)$ the inequality becomes a strict finite comparison between two $O(1)$ constants with no spare coercive margin — $\delta=0$ is the $d=4$ stall. The obstruction is method-invariant: both the polymer/cluster route and the asymptotic-freedom RP block-spin monotone

$$M_{n+1}\le(1-c\,b_0\,g_n^2)M_n+O(g_n^4)M_n,\qquad b_0=\tfrac{11N}{3}=11\ (SU(3)),$$

stall on the identical $\delta=0$ object at $g=O(1)$ — the signal that the obstruction is intrinsic to $d=4$ marginality, not an artifact of one tool.

The core idea

Take one thirteen-dimensional shape — ordinary four-dimensional spacetime times a small, stiff internal geometry (the flag manifold K₆ = SU(3)/U(1)²). Feed in four measured numbers: the Planck mass M_Pl, the gauge couplings α_i(M_Z), the top-quark Yukawa y_t, and one flavor-mixing anchor |V_us|. Then read the rest of physics off the shape.

The headline is an economy, not a miracle. Against those four inputs, the geometry returns 22+ independent quantities — the pattern of forces, the mixing angles, the family count, the tilt of the early universe. More outputs than inputs is the fingerprint of a theory that is constrained, not curve-fitted. The most arbitrary-looking fact in particle physics — why exactly three families of matter? — comes out as a topological index (−3) the geometry counts, with no dial to turn it to two or four. That count is forced inside the declared search category, not conjured from nothing, and it is consistent with the LEP measurement of three light neutrino species.

What makes this document different from other "theories of everything" is the second half: it grades its own homework with a fixed vocabulary, and it catches itself. Every quantity sits in exactly one of four terminal states — derived-by-computation, decided at decision-grade, conditional behind a named gate, or precisely open with a falsifier attached. The program tested its own deepest cancellation idea against the cosmological constant Λ, watched it fail, proved the failure was structural, and published the refutation — Λ stays Weinberg-open, and no comparison to the observed value is made anywhere.
